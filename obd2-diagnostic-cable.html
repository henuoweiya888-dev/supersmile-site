The problem we solve for diagnostic cable buyers

Stock diagnostic cables are often too short, use thin wire, or break at the connector after heavy daily use. Many resellers and tool brands cannot find a supplier who will build their exact length, connector and cable structure with quality they can stand behind.

We build the cable exactly to your spec — length, wire gauge, shielding, connector type and overmold — so your product performs reliably and survives the workshop.

OBD2 cable types we produce

OBD2 16-pin diagnostic cables for cars and light trucks (SAE J1962).

Heavy-duty truck diagnostic cables (Deutsch, J1939, 9-pin and 23-pin styles) for trucks and buses.

EV and new-energy vehicle diagnostic cables, including Tesla-style connectors.

USB-to-OBD2, Bluetooth adapter cables and extension leads for diagnostic tools.
