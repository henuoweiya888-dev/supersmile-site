# FPC / FFC Cable Assemblies — editorial brief

## Purpose

Build search coverage and buyer confidence by explaining the complete flexible-interconnect system rather than treating FFC/FPC as a generic ribbon cable.

## Search intent

Custom FFC cable assembly, custom FPC cable assembly, flexible flat cable, flexible printed circuit, ZIF/LIF connector, 0.3/0.5/1.0 mm pitch, top/bottom contact, same-side/opposite-side tail, stiffener, notched FPC, dynamic flex, shielded FFC, display cable and camera cable.

## Editorial sequence

1. Distinguish FFC from FPC.
2. Define interface geometry: pitch, circuit count, thickness, contact side and tail.
3. Explain connector actuation and correct mating.
4. Separate static folding from dynamic flex and plan routing.
5. Show equipment applications without turning application names into unsupported qualifications.
6. Close with magnified inspection, electrical test, revision control and quotation inputs.

## Visual composition

Use a full-bleed dark hero, an opening comparison spread, a three-column geometry chapter, a dark two-stage mating chapter, an asymmetric movement feature, paired application studies and compact verification rows. Peer images in each chapter use consistent proportions; mobile converts to wide text-led rows and edge-to-edge feature images.
