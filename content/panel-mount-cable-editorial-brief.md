# Panel-Mount Cable Assemblies — editorial brief

## Search intent

- Commercial: custom panel mount cable assembly, bulkhead cable assembly, panel mount USB cable, USB-C panel extension, HDMI panel mount cable, RJ45 panel mount Ethernet cable, panel mount power cable.
- Engineering: front mount, rear mount, jam nut, square flange, feedthrough, panel cutout, panel thickness, O-ring, gasket, backshell, strain relief, shield bonding, ingress protection, continuity testing.
- Buyer decision: what drawings and samples are required, how sealing is defined, which tests apply, and how repeat production is controlled.

## Editorial structure

1. Define the equipment boundary.
2. Explain mounting architecture and mechanical fit.
3. Separate environmental performance from component marketing.
4. Explain controlled hardware and seal stack.
5. Cover USB, video and Ethernet panel extensions.
6. Show cross-industry use without claiming certification.
7. Treat high-current work as a thermal and safety design.
8. Close with manufacturing, validation, records and FAQ.

## Claim controls

- Never claim universal IP67/IP68, current, voltage, bandwidth or mating life.
- A component rating does not automatically apply to the installed cable assembly.
- Interface names do not establish end-to-end protocol capability.
- Standards, approvals and certifications require project-specific evidence.
