# Panduit-compatible product-page brief

## Search and customer intent

- Explain that this category covers custom harnesses built with specified Panduit terminals, cable ties, mounts, labels, wiring duct, protective sleeving and related installation hardware; it is not one interchangeable connector family.
- Cover common product and search names naturally: Pan-Term terminals, ferrules, ring terminals, disconnects, Pan-Ty, Dome-Top, Contour-Ty, Hyper-V, Sta-Strap, Dura-Ty, Pan-Steel, Panduct, Pan-Wrap, Quick-Build harness board, heat-shrink labels, grounding and bonding.
- Demonstrate engineering competence through material selection, crimp-tool matching, tie-tension control, bend and strain management, identification, inspection and substitution control.
- Avoid implying Panduit authorization or universal interchangeability. Final compatibility follows the exact part number, drawing and approved source.

## Art direction

- Compact industrial magazine with one full-bleed hero, a wide dark tonal transition and copy directly over the photograph.
- Use nine distinct documentary photographs for nine substantive topics; no topic card without its own supporting image.
- Chapter layouts vary by reading purpose: two-by-two component studies, three vertical manufacturing studies, then two wide protection studies.
- Simple rectangular masks, consistent peer image ratios, short dividers, no outer boxes and no repeated badge on every small title.

## Source basis

- Panduit official wire-harness, wire-termination, routing/protection and control-panel materials for product-family terminology and engineering context.
- Wikimedia Commons photographs for independent visual context; source and license details are recorded beside the downloaded assets.
