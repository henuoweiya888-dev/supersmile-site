## Primary evidence and corrections

- JAE tooling: https://www.jae.com/en/tool/ . Match contact, wire and approved tool.
- MX34: https://www.jae.com/en/connectors/series/detail/id%3D64304%26order%5B%5D%3Dsubject%3Adesc . Non-waterproof; do not confuse vehicle use with sealing.
- MX23A: https://products.jae.com/gl/en/connectors/content-library/featured-content/motorcycle-mx23a/ . Mating seal, wire-entry grommet and unused-cavity plugs are a system.
- IL-AG5: https://www.jae.com/en/connectors/series/detail/id%3D64295%26order%5B%5D%3Dstatus%3Aasc%26order%5B%5D%3Dsubject%3Adesc . 2.5mm, board/cable and cable/cable options.
- IL-AG5 material example: https://www.jae.com/connectors/series/detail/product/id%3D65711 . PBT, copper alloy, tin; not all JAE housings LCP/PA66, not all contacts beryllium copper.
- FI-X: https://www.jae.com/en/connectors/series/detail/id%3D64291 . 1.0mm LCD interface, shell and differential design. Component impedance is not finished cable verification.
- FI: https://www.jae.com/en/connectors/series/detail/?application_code=A2080&id=64285 . Separate family from FI-X.
- AEC: https://www.aecouncil.com/AECDocuments.html . No universal AEC-Q connector certification claim.
- LCP: https://www.celanese.com/products/lcp-vectra-zenite . Thin-wall moulding relevance; specific grade and environment required.
- PA66-GF: https://plastics-rubber.basf.com/global/en/performance_polymers/products/ultramid/30045743 . Grade and moisture conditioning matter.
- Electrical-test distinction: https://cirris.com/?wpdmdl=5728 . Continuity, insulation and withstand are distinct; avoid copying settings into a general page.
