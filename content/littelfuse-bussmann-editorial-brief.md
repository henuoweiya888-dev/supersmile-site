# Littelfuse / Bussmann fuse harness editorial brief

## Page intent

Explain that a fuse harness is a coordinated protection and distribution assembly, not a wire with a fuse inserted. Help engineering and purchasing teams identify the fuse family, holder, conductor, busbar, environment and validation data needed for a buildable specification.

## Search coverage

- Littelfuse fuse harness, Bussmann fuse harness, automotive fuse holder harness
- MICRO2, MICRO3, MINI, low-profile MINI, ATO, MAXI, JCASE, MCASE, MIDI, MEGA, ZCASE
- blade fuse, cartridge fuse, bolt-down fuse, inline fuse holder, battery fuse cable
- rated current, rated voltage, interrupting rating, I2t, time-current curve, derating
- power distribution box, battery disconnect unit, EV high-voltage fuse, busbar and cable lug

## Editorial structure

1. Opening: protection is a circuit decision.
2. Fuse selection: family, electrical duty and holder interface.
3. Distribution assembly: relay/fuse box, busbar joints and crimped power leads.
4. Validation and applications: EV/high-voltage coordination and finished-circuit inspection.
5. Specification checklist and engineering FAQ.

## Accuracy boundary

Littelfuse and Bussmann names identify customer-specified compatibility targets. They do not imply authorization, universal interchangeability or a Super Smile electrical rating. Final selection depends on the original part number, application circuit, equipment approval and validation plan.
