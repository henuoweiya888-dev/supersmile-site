# Waterproof Design Harness — editorial brief

## Search intent

- Commercial: custom waterproof wire harness, sealed cable assembly, IP67 wire harness, IP68 cable assembly, IP69K harness, waterproof connector harness, outdoor equipment harness, marine wiring harness, washdown cable assembly.
- Components: DEUTSCH DT / DTM / DTP, TE AMPSEAL, AMP SUPERSEAL, Molex MX150, M8 and M12 sealed connector, wire seal, cavity plug, cable gland, rear grommet, O-ring and gasket.
- Processes: sealed splice, solder-seal sleeve, adhesive-lined heat shrink, overmolded waterproof cable, potted cable assembly, crimp and seal inspection, water ingress test.
- Buyer decision: test state, exposure, complete mating system, cable diameter, route, drainage, material compatibility and evidence required before repeat production.

## Editorial structure

1. Define waterproofing as one continuous boundary.
2. Separate IP67, IP68 and IP69K test questions.
3. Explain housing seals, wire seals, plugs and the complete mating system.
4. Cover cable entries, capillary migration, sealed branches and adhesive heat shrink.
5. Explain overmolding, potting, condensation and material compatibility.
6. Translate the design into agriculture, marine and industrial-washdown contexts.
7. Close with controlled inputs, verification and FAQ.

## Claim controls

- Never transfer a component IP rating to the complete harness.
- Never present IP67, IP68 and IP69K as interchangeable quality grades.
- Brand and family names are search vocabulary and interface references, not an assertion of authorized supply or universal compatibility.
- Standards and methods guide project validation; they do not constitute product certification.
- Conceptual environmental photographs explain exposure and do not show a passed Super Smile test.
