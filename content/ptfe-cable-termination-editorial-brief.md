# PTFE Cable Termination — editorial brief

## Search and customer intent

The page answers engineering and purchasing searches around PTFE wire termination, Teflon wire harnesses, high-temperature cable assemblies, MIL-DTL-16878 Type E / Type EE, NEMA HP3, UL AWM, silver-plated copper wire, PTFE wire stripping, crimp terminals, solder sleeves, thermocouple extension leads, RTD cables and aerospace/instrument wiring.

## Technical boundary

- Do not treat PTFE, ETFE, FEP and PFA as interchangeable.
- Do not generalize one manufacturer's temperature, voltage, conductor plating or approval to all PTFE wire.
- Type EE, NEMA HP3 and UL AWM terms are used as search vocabulary and exact-document examples, not as blanket Super Smile certification claims.
- Termination selection depends on conductor construction, plating, insulation diameter, terminal range, strip geometry and validated tooling.
- Application photography is contextual and never presented as Super Smile manufacturing evidence.
