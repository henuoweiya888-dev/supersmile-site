# Molded strain relief editorial brief

## Page intent

Explain molded strain relief as a load-transfer and bend-control system, not a decorative boot. Connect geometry, cable-jacket compatibility, insert molding, tooling and validation to real cable-assembly decisions.

## Search coverage

molded strain relief, overmolded cable assembly, cable boot, anti-kink boot, strain relief grommet, bend protection, cable overmolding, connector overmolding, cable pull test, cable flex test, TPE overmold, TPU cable boot, PVC strain relief, panel cable grommet.

## Source basis

- Molex custom cable assembly guidance: overmolded strain relief can reduce mechanical stress, with the final construction defined as a complete assembly.
- HellermannTyton cable-grommet guidance: a grommet can combine edge protection, bend protection, sealing and strain relief depending on the selected system.
- TE Connectivity backshell guidance: cable support and protection against over-flexing belong to connector accessory design.
- UL cord/cable flexibility guidance: flex and mechanical evaluation must follow the applicable product and project requirements.

## Accuracy boundary

Do not claim a universal bend radius, pull force, flex life, IP rating, material grade or chemical resistance. These depend on the cable, connector or insert, overmold resin, geometry, process window, application and referenced test method.
