# 32AWG fine-pitch harness editorial brief

## Page intent

Explain the engineering and manufacturing controls behind fine-wire, compact wire-to-board cable assemblies. Keep 32AWG as a project-specific conductor option, not a universal current claim.

## Search coverage

32AWG wire harness, fine-pitch cable assembly, 1.0 mm pitch connector, 1.25 mm pitch connector, JST SH cable, Molex Pico-Clasp cable, Hirose DF13 harness, micro wire-to-board connector, miniature crimp terminal, micro crimp height, pull-force test, microscope inspection, compact sensor cable.

## Source basis

- JST SH official product page: 1.0 mm pitch, AWG 28/30/32.
- Molex Pico-Clasp official product page and tooling specification: 1.0 mm pitch, 28–32AWG, project-specific crimp-height and pull-force controls.
- Hirose DF13 official catalog: 1.25 mm pitch and contact options covering 30–32AWG for applicable parts.

## Accuracy boundary

Connector family, pitch and nominal wire gauge do not prove current, voltage, mating, plating, tooling or crimp compatibility. Freeze exact component references and validated process data before production.
