# IDC Ribbon Cable Assembly editorial brief

## Search intent

Serve engineers and buyers searching for custom IDC ribbon cable assemblies, flat ribbon cable harnesses, 1.27 mm or 2.54 mm IDC connectors, straight or reversed pin mapping, feed-through and daisy-chain assemblies, mass-termination tooling, strain relief and continuity-tested ribbon cables.

## Technical boundaries

- IDC is a termination method rather than a universal connector definition.
- All dimensions, ratings, wire sizes, pitches and temperatures remain product-family specific.
- Pin-one markings help orientation but never replace an approved point-to-point map.
- Component approvals and material names do not automatically qualify the completed cable assembly.
- Additional environmental, mechanical or signal-integrity testing is project-specific.
