# FTDI Cable Assembly editorial brief

## Search intent

- custom FTDI cable assembly, USB to UART cable, USB to TTL serial cable, USB RS232 cable, USB RS485 cable
- FT232R, FT230X, FT231X, FT234XD, FT2232H, FT4232H
- 3.3 V TTL, 5 V TTL, TX RX RTS CTS, VCP driver, D2XX driver, VID PID, EEPROM programming
- RJ45 console cable, DB9 serial cable, M8 M12 serial cable, programming cable, industrial USB serial cable

## Editorial logic

1. Define the category as an active electronic cable assembly.
2. Separate logical protocol from the physical electrical layer.
3. Explain chip/channel selection, pinout, endpoint and software identity.
4. Cover cable construction, protection, applications and layered validation.
5. End with exact quotation inputs and bounded FAQ answers.

## Guardrails

- Do not claim authorized FTDI distribution, genuine components, compliance or test completion without project evidence.
- Do not imply TTL UART, RS-232, RS-422 and RS-485 are interchangeable.
- Do not invent VID/PID values or reuse another party's identity.
- Standards and product-family names are selection and verification references, not blanket certifications.
