What we customize

Connectors and terminals: OBD2, Deutsch, DB15, RJ45, USB/Type-C, XT series, Anderson and customer-specified types.

Pinout and signal routing: built to your pin definition, diagnostic protocol and equipment interface.

Branding and packaging: neutral or buyer packaging, labels, model stickers, barcodes and instruction sheets.
