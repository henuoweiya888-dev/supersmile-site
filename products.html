02

Engineering Knowledge

Explain connector, conductor, shielding, protection and routing choices in plain purchasing language.

03

Selection Notes

Identify the information that prevents an incorrect fit, pin route, material or testing assumption.

04

Delivery Process

Show how the requirement moves through engineering review, material confirmation, sampling, inspection and shipment review.

From Requirement to Repeatable Production

01

Requirement Input

Drawing, sample, connector photos, pin definition, target equipment and quantity range.

02

Review interfaces, wire structure, materials, process feasibility and inspection points.

03

Sample Validation

Build and confirm the sample against the agreed drawing, function and appearance requirements.

04

Production & Shipment

Incoming inspection, crimping, assembly, continuity checks, identification, packing and shipment review.
