# New-energy low-voltage harness editorial images

All numbered JPG files in this folder are downloaded from the corresponding free-to-use Pexels photo page. The filename is the Pexels photo ID, so the source page is `https://www.pexels.com/photo/<id>/`.

The selection was made after the page copy was fixed. Images support the specific subjects discussed in each caption: EV charging interfaces, battery storage, photovoltaic conversion, electrical panels, circuit verification, controlled assembly, maintenance and environmental exposure. They are contextual editorial evidence and do not claim to depict a Super Smile product or a particular approved harness configuration.
