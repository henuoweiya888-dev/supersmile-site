# Outdoor waterproof harness image sources

Numeric files are free-to-use Pexels photographs at `https://www.pexels.com/photo/<id>/`. Named waterproof-detail photographs are documented in the adjacent `waterproof-design/SOURCES.md` file.

The sequence pairs direct interface details with environmental evidence: rain, splash, moving water, mud, frost, coastal corrosion, outdoor energy equipment and field maintenance. These photographs communicate the exposure or engineering issue discussed by each paragraph; they do not claim a third-party facility or product as Super Smile's own.
