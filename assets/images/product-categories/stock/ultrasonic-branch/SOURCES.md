## Technical references

- [IEC 60352-9:2024](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/68262) — ultrasonically welded solderless connections, requirements, tests and guidance.
- [SAE/USCAR-38-2](https://saemobilus.sae.org/standards/uscar38-2-performance-specification-ultrasonically-welded-wire-terminations) — automotive wire-to-terminal ultrasonic weld scope and validation boundary.
- [TELSONIC Ultrasonic Metal Welding Quality Guidelines](https://www.telsonic.com/fileadmin/media/Ultrasonic_Metal_Welding_-_Quality_Guidelines.pdf) — placement, weld quality and tensile-test guidance.
- [Schunk Sonosystems wire-harness applications](https://www.schunk-group.com/sonosystems/en/applications/wire-harness) — wire-to-wire, terminal and high-current application families.
