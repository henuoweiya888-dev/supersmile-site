# Pigtail and branch harness image sources

The numeric filenames are free-to-use Pexels photo IDs and resolve to `https://www.pexels.com/photo/<id>/`. The two descriptive filenames referenced from the solar and ECU/DCU collections retain their source notes in those folders.

The selection follows the adjacent topics: open leads, splices, branch routing, junctions, automotive installation, industrial equipment, service work, test and traceability. Images provide technical context and do not identify the pictured parts as Super Smile products.
