# IDC Ribbon Cable Assembly image and technical sources

The photographs on this page are editorial context. They illustrate interface geometry, ribbon construction, termination, routing, equipment applications and testing. They are not photographs of Super Smile products and do not establish component certification, brand authorization or validation of a finished assembly. License and attribution details are available on each linked Wikimedia Commons file page.

## Technical references

- [TE Connectivity AMP-LATCH Ribbon Cable Interconnects](https://www.te.com/en/products/connectors/pcb-connectors/wire-to-board-connectors/ffc-fpc-ribbon-connectors/intersection/amp-latch-ribbon-connectors.html) — IDC contact sequence, mass termination and documented centerline examples.
- [TE Connectivity AMP-LATCH Ribbon Cable Interconnect Solutions](https://www.te.com/content/dam/te-com/documents/consumer-devices/global/amp-latch-ribbon-cable-interconnect-solutions.pdf) — ribbon cable-to-board product families and IDC termination sequence.
- [TE Connectivity Madison IDC Ribbon Cable](https://www.te.com/content/dam/te-com/documents/industrial-automation-and-control/global/Madison-IDC%20Ribbon%20Cable%201108.pdf) — conductor-size, center-spacing, plating and polarity-marking examples for a specific product family.
- [Molex Flat Ribbon Cable](https://www.molex.com/en-us/products/wire-and-cable/specialty-wire-and-cable/flat-ribbon-cable) — custom conductor, pitch, hybrid and intermittent-bonding options.
- [Molex IDT Ribbon Cable Hand Tool Module](https://www.molex.com/content/dam/molex/molex-dot-com/products/automated/en-us/applicationtoolingspecificationpdf/621/62100/ATS-621002000-001.pdf) — cable-stop and termination-height setup for a specific tool and connector system.
- [Samtec IDC Cable Systems Guide](https://suddendocs.samtec.com/literature/samtec-flex-stacking-design-guide.pdf) — straight, reversed, breakout, daisy-chain and strain-relief wiring options.
- [3M Ribbon Cable Wiremount Socket 451 Series](https://www.3m.com/3M/en_US/p/d/b00038568/) — mass IDC termination and product-specific ribbon/connector geometry.
