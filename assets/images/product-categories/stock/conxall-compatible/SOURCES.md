# Conxall-compatible cable assemblies — photo sources

Eleven distinct camera-made photographs support this technical category page. None depicts a verified Super Smile build, a Conxall-approved finished assembly, our factory, or an inspection result. Several photographs show **other manufacturers' components**; the captions name them and use them only to explain form, process, cable construction, or installation context. The pictured brand or device must never be read as an endorsement or a claim of interchangeability.

Files were proportionally resized or JPEG-recompressed for web delivery; responsive display frames may crop the visible portion. The edited copies of Creative Commons BY-SA photographs remain under their respective BY-SA licences. The public page presents the photographer, source and licence links plus a modification note; this repository record alone is not public attribution.
