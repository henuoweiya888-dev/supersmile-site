All photographs are used as contextual editorial illustrations. They do not depict Super Smile production parts, prove CAN compatibility, or document a completed network test.

- `hero-diagnostics.jpg` — Pexels photo 10490631, vehicle technician using a diagnostic laptop. Free under the Pexels license.
- `network-boundary.jpg` — Pexels photo 28265032, industrial wiring and distribution equipment. Free under the Pexels license.
- `network-topology.jpg` — Pexels photo 1597776, connected network ports. Free under the Pexels license.
- `twisted-pair.jpg` — Pexels photo 12266914, dense data cabling. Free under the Pexels license; used as a route-control metaphor, not a CAN cable specification.

Technical references reviewed for the page include ISO 11898-1:2024, the current ISO 11898-2 catalogue record, the CAN in Automation CAN dictionary, SAE J1939 top-level documentation and SAE J1939/82 compliance scope. Applicable editions, limits and test methods must be agreed for each project.
