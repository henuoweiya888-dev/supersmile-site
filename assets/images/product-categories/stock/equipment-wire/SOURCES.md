- IEC, [IEC 60228, Conductors of insulated cables](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/1065). The linked 2004 page notes the newer 2023 consolidated version; copy avoids edition-specific conformity claims.
- UL, [Wire and Cable Marking and Application Guide](https://code-authorities.ul.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/WC_MG.pdf).
- UL, [UL 508A Supplement SA](https://www.ul.com/sites/g/files/qbfpbp251/files/2020-11/UL%20508A%20Supplement%20SA%2011_24_20.pdf), used only to confirm the distinct UL 758 AWM component category in industrial control-panel context.

Standards are named for technical orientation. The page does not claim certification, listing or a particular revision for Super Smile products.
