# Turnkey harness image sources

Numeric files are free-to-use Pexels photographs at `https://www.pexels.com/photo/<id>/`. Four factory photographs are original Super Smile production images from `assets/images/factory/`.

The visual sequence follows an actual turnkey programme: engineering review, component control, kitting, assembly, inspection, controlled inventory, packing and logistics. Third-party images are contextual and do not represent a customer programme or a claimed Super Smile facility.
