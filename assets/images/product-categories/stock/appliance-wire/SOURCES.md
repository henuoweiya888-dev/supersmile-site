Technical scope references:

- [UL Solutions Wire & Cable scope table](https://www.ul.com/resources/wire-cable-general-fuii-scope-table-and-addendum-list) — identifies UL 758 for Appliance Wiring Material and UL 62 for flexible cords.
- [UL Product iQ AWM style example](https://iq.ul.com/awm/stylepage.aspx?style=10203) — illustrates that a style page defines construction, ratings and use conditions.
- [IEC 60227-1:2024](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/71989) — general requirements for PVC-insulated cables up to and including 450/750 V.
- [IEC 60245-1:2026](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/92635) — general requirements for rubber-insulated rigid and flexible cables up to and including 450/750 V.
