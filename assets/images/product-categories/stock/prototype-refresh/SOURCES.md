# Prototype and small-batch harness image sources

Numeric files are free-to-use Pexels photographs at `https://www.pexels.com/photo/<id>/`.

The set follows a controlled prototype programme: requirement review, precision measurement, electronics assembly, temporary material control, laboratory testing, revision records, small-batch production and inventory release. Contextual photographs illustrate the engineering activity or proof point; they do not claim that the pictured facility, product or personnel belong to Super Smile.
