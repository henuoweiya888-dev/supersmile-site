## Technical references

- [TE Connectivity MICRODOT Micro-D overview](https://www.te.com/en/products/brands/microdot.html?tab=pgp-story) — connector-family context, compact high-performance interfaces and termination options.
- [Glenair Micro-D catalog](https://www.glenair.com/catalogs/micro-d.pdf) — family configurations, hardware, wire and backshell examples.
- [Glenair high-speed Micro-D](https://www.glenair.com/micro-d-high-speed/) — protocol-specific assemblies, shielded twisted pairs and grounding details.
- [Omnetics standard Micro-D family](https://www.omnetics.com/product/micro-d-straight-thru-hole-mount-dd/) — 0.050 inch / 1.27 mm centerline and series variants.
- [Omnetics Camera Link characterization](https://www.omnetics.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/CL_T180110Rev1.pdf) — one product-specific cable, shielding and differential-channel example; not generalized as a universal rating.
- [Omnetics SpaceWire cable example](https://www.omnetics.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Spacewire.pdf) — shielded-pair, pin-map and material examples tied to a specific assembly.
- [NASA-STD-8739.4](https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/nasa/nasa-std-87394) — workmanship and interconnecting cable/harness process reference; citation does not claim project compliance.
- [NASA reliability study on high-density Micro-D wiring](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20080019636/downloads/20080019636.pdf?attachment=true) — process-control and high-density wiring context.
