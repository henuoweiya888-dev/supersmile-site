Company Profile公司简介

Shenzhen Super Smile-Tech Co., Ltd. (Super Smile-Tech) is a manufacturer of wire harnesses and cable assemblies, combining engineering development, custom production, and quality testing. Based in Shenzhen, the company has a production facility of approximately 3,000 square meters, more than 100 employees, and its own engineering and production teams, with the capability to deliver projects from requirements review and sample development through to volume production.

Our expertise extends from build-to-print production to translating complex connection requirements into practical manufacturing solutions. Connector and terminal selection, pin assignments and circuit definitions, wire lengths and gauges, branch configurations, shielding, and protection can all be customized around the interfaces, installation space, and operating environment of the customer’s equipment.

Our products and custom manufacturing services cover automotive diagnostic cables, heavy-duty vehicle harnesses, and turbo actuator cables, with applications extending to industrial automation, robotics, and low-voltage systems in new energy applications. We provide manufacturing support for power, signal, and control connections across different types of equipment.

In production, we bring material selection, wire cutting and stripping, terminal crimping, assembly, and inspection into a unified process. We perform 100% continuity testing and use confirmed drawings, samples, process documents, and inspection requirements to control manufacturing consistency in every batch.

Through direct collaboration between engineering and production at our own factory, Super Smile-Tech provides OEM/ODM services covering product customization, sample validation, volume production, and branded packaging. We support customers with clear technical references and a defined path for execution, from project development through to ongoing supply.

We solve connection challenges through engineering expertise and help bring our customers’ products to life through manufacturing capability. This is the value of Super Smile-Tech.

深圳市超斯迈尔科技有限公司（Super Smile-Tech）是一家集工程开发、定制生产与质量检测于一体的线束及线缆组件制造商。公司立足深圳，拥有约3,000平方米生产基地、100余名员工及自有工程与生产团队，具备从需求评审、样品开发到批量制造的完整交付能力。

我们的实力不止于按图加工，更在于将复杂的连接要求转化为切实可行的制造方案：从连接器与端子匹配、针脚与电路定义，到线长线规、分支结构、屏蔽与防护配置，均可围绕客户设备的接口、安装空间及使用环境进行定制。

产品与定制服务涵盖汽车诊断线、重型车辆线束、涡轮执行器连接线，并延伸至工业自动化、机器人及新能源低压系统等应用，为不同设备的电源、信号与控制连接提供制造支持。

在生产端，我们将材料选配、裁线剥皮、端子压接、组装与检验纳入统一流程，执行100%导通测试，并以确认图纸、样品、工艺文件和检验要求为依据，控制每批产品的制造一致性。

依托自有工厂与工程、生产的直接协同，超斯迈尔提供覆盖产品定制、样品验证、批量生产及品牌包装的OEM/ODM服务，让客户从项目开发到持续供货，始终拥有清晰的技术依据与执行路径。

以工程能力解决连接难题，以制造实力支撑客户的产品落地——这就是超斯迈尔的价值。
