PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is a fuel-system harness the same as a fuel line?燃油系统线束等同于燃油管吗？

No. The harness carries electrical power and signals to pumps, injectors, valves and sensors. Hoses, rails and pipes carry fuel and require separate fluid-system engineering and verification.不等同。线束向泵、喷油器、阀和传感器输送电源与信号；软管、油轨和管路输送燃油，需另行开展流体系统设计与验证。

Can a connector that fits an injector be used directly?喷油器插头能插上就可以直接用吗？

No. Confirm the injector and controller part numbers, cavity view, driver strategy, circuit assignment, terminal system and installed environment. A shared shell shape does not prove electrical compatibility.不能。还需确认喷油器与控制器料号、孔位视图、驱动策略、回路分配、端子体系和安装环境。外壳形状相同不证明电气兼容。

Does a sealed connector make the whole harness fuel-resistant?密封接头能让整根线束耐燃油吗？

No. Fuel exposure must be defined by liquid, vapor or mist, duration and temperature. Wire insulation, seals, protection and the mated connector all need suitable material evidence and an agreed test if a resistance claim is required.不能。应明确是液体、蒸气还是油雾，以及接触时长和温度。若需耐受声明，线材绝缘、密封、防护及对插连接器均需对应材料证据和约定试验。
