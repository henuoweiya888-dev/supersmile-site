CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the automotive sealed connector flying-lead pigtail set form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以汽车密封接头开口尾线束组的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

02

Open lead ends开放引线端

An end or some branches remain as open leads, with later terminations and insulation preparation defined for the assembly method.一端或部分支路以开放引线形式保留，可结合装配方式定义后续端头和绝缘处理。

03

Pigtail connection form尾线连接形式

A lead section extends behind the connector, allowing length, further termination and support to follow the equipment connection.插接端后保留引线段，便于按设备接线位置确定长度、后续端接与支撑。

04

Grouped cable-set form组合线组形式

Several connection pieces are organized as a cable set, allowing each section to be assigned and identified by assembly sequence and module.多段连接件以线组形式组织，可按装配顺序和设备模块划分各段用途与标识。

05

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Equipment junction boxes and entry positions设备接线盒与入口位置

Define entry orientation, water-trap risks and service access at junction boxes. Record the seal-to-jacket combination separately. Protection targets must identify the full assembly boundary and exact test conditions.接线盒入口需要说明安装朝向、积水风险和检修空间；密封件与电缆护套的匹配关系单独记录。选配时，目标防护要求应包含完整组件边界与具体试验条件。

Outdoor equipment and cable routes室外设备与电缆路径

Describe sunlight, rain, pooling water and retention on outdoor routes. Conduit installation and direct exposure need different material and acceptance requirements. Match wire seals, potting, gaskets and housings for material compatibility.室外路径应说明日照、雨水、积水和固定方式，穿管或直接暴露属于不同的材料与验收条件。选配时，线封、灌封、垫圈与壳体按材料相容性配套。

Protection boundaries at detachable interfaces可拆接口的防护边界

Define protection in both mated and unmated states. Include reassembly checks after service in the installation requirements. Record actual cable diameter, unused cavities and potential capillary paths.可拆连接需说明插合与未插合两种状态的防护方式，检修后重新装配的检查点也应写入使用要求。选配时，实际线缆外径、空孔和潜在毛细通道均须记录。

Enclosure entries and cable retention机箱穿线与入口固定

Match cable entries to actual diameter, panel thickness and fixing method. Keep jacket retention independent of the internal termination. Specify mating state, installation orientation, temperature cycling and condensation conditions.线缆入口需匹配实际外径、板厚和固定方式；护套夹持与内部端接之间应保留独立的支撑路径。选配时，规定插合状态、安装姿态、温度循环及冷凝边界。

Module connections in laboratory equipment实验室设备的模块配线

Coordinate sample areas, moving mechanisms and serviceable modules in laboratory equipment. Take isolation and cleaning requirements from the target device documents. Protection targets must identify the full assembly boundary and exact test conditions.实验设备连接需要协调样品区、运动机构和可维护模块，电气隔离与清洁要求应来自目标设备文件。选配时，目标防护要求应包含完整组件边界与具体试验条件。

Detachable circular connections可拆卸圆形连接

Check gender, keying, coupling and end-face views together. Equal contact counts do not replace confirmation of the exact series and part numbers. Match wire seals, potting, gaskets and housings for material compatibility.圆形接口需要同时核对公母、键位、锁紧方式和端视图，针数相同并不能替代完整系列与料号确认。选配时，线封、灌封、垫圈与壳体按材料相容性配套。
