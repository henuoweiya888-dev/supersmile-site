CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Conductor, insulation and material choices导体、绝缘与选材要点

Use the red/black power plug to ring-terminal harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以红黑供电插头转环形端子线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Ring-terminal end环形端子端头

A ring-terminal end provides a mounting-hole connection. Lead direction, support and insulation coverage can follow the stud position.末端采用带安装孔的环形端子形式，可围绕螺柱位置安排接线方向、端头支撑与绝缘覆盖。

02

End transition layout端头转接与组合

The assembly organizes a transition or combined connection, with end directions, transition sections and fixing arranged around the equipment positions.组件以转接或组合形式组织连接，可围绕两端设备位置安排端头方向、过渡段与固定。

03

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

04

Current path and end termination载流与端部连接

Select conductors and terminations from continuous and peak loads, installed length and temperature rise. Confirm the end interface, contact surface, insulation coverage and fixing as a set; check mounting-bore size where ring terminals are specified.依据持续与峰值负载、安装长度和温升要求选择导体与端接；端部接口、接触面、绝缘覆盖及固定方式需要成套确认；仅在采用环形端子时核对安装孔径。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Confirm cross-linked material type, process and exact supply specification.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，确认交联材料类型、工艺及准确来源规格。

Interfaces within engine-bay layouts发动机舱内的接口布置

Map heat sources, fluid exposure and service routes together. Allow assembly space around connector ends and cable supports. Record conductor area, insulation thickness and diameter separately.发动机舱连接要把热源、油液暴露和检修路径放在同一张布线图中，端头与固定点也需留出装配空间。选配时，导体截面、绝缘厚度与外径分别列入材料定义。

Routing boundaries around hot equipment热区设备的布线边界

Separate continuous temperature, peaks and exposure time around hot equipment. Review wire, terminals and protective parts against their own limits. Check temperature, voltage and thermal-aging evidence for the actual model.热区布线需要区分连续温度、峰值和暴露时间，线材、端子及保护件应按各自限制评审。选配时，按实际型号核对温度、电压及热老化依据。

Terminal wiring inside control cabinets控制柜内的端子配线

Coordinate terminal positions, duct exits and service access within the cabinet. Wire labels should correspond to the circuit table and equipment ports. Match termination, stripping and bending conditions to the selected insulation.柜内连接需协调端子位置、线槽出口和可维护空间，线号应能对应回路表与设备端口。选配时，端接、剥皮和弯曲条件应适合所选绝缘结构。

Battery and removable power connections电池与可拆电源连接

Define polarity, continuous and peak load, and permitted connection conditions. Record auxiliary signals separately from the main power path. Confirm cross-linked material type, process and exact supply specification.电池连接要明确极性、持续与峰值负载以及允许插拔条件，辅助信号回路与主电源路径应分别记录。选配时，确认交联材料类型、工艺及准确来源规格。

Contact and fine-wire termination端子与细线端接

Contact selection depends on conductor construction, insulation diameter and tooling limits. Set wire preparation and crimp inspection for the actual contact part. Record conductor area, insulation thickness and diameter separately.端子选择同时取决于导体结构、绝缘外径和工具范围；备线尺寸与压接检查应落到实际接点型号。选配时，导体截面、绝缘厚度与外径分别列入材料定义。

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Do all cross-linked materials have the same temperature rating?交联材料都有相同温度等级吗？

Cross-linking does not establish one fixed temperature class. Confirm cross-linked material type, process and exact supply specification.交联不等于任何固定温度等级。确认交联材料类型、工艺及准确来源规格。

Does cross-linked insulation mean the jacket is also cross-linked?交联绝缘是否说明护套也交联？

Record cross-linked insulation separately from jacket material. Record conductor area, insulation thickness and diameter separately.交联绝缘与护套材料分别记录。导体截面、绝缘厚度与外径分别列入材料定义。

Can heat-resistance evidence replace flame-test evidence?耐热证据能代替阻燃证据吗？

Heat and flame properties need separate evidence. Check temperature, voltage and thermal-aging evidence for the actual model.耐热与阻燃性能需要各自依据。按实际型号核对温度、电压及热老化依据。
