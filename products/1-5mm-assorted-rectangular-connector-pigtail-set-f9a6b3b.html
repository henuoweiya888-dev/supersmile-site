CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the 1.5mm assorted rectangular connector pigtail set form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以1.5mm接口 · 多规格矩形接头尾线束组的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Rectangular interface form矩形接口外形

The rectangular interface form allows end position and service clearance to follow the mating face, latch and cable exit.接口采用矩形轮廓，可围绕插入面、卡扣与线束出口安排端头位置和拆装空间。

02

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

03

Pigtail connection form尾线连接形式

A lead section extends behind the connector, allowing length, further termination and support to follow the equipment connection.插接端后保留引线段，便于按设备接线位置确定长度、后续端接与支撑。

04

Grouped cable-set form组合线组形式

Several connection pieces are organized as a cable set, allowing each section to be assigned and identified by assembly sequence and module.多段连接件以线组形式组织，可按装配顺序和设备模块划分各段用途与标识。

05

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。
