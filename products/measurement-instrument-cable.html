ENGINEERING OVERVIEW工程概览

Preserve the measurand from sensor interface to instrument input从传感接口到仪器输入，保持被测量的真实性

A measurement cable is part of the measurement system. Thermocouples, RTDs, strain gauges, accelerometers, current probes, oscilloscopes, DAQ modules and precision sensors do not share one universal cable. The assembly can add resistance, capacitance, leakage, triboelectric noise, thermal EMF, attenuation or coupling, so the required uncertainty and bandwidth must be known before construction.

测量电缆属于测量系统的一部分。热电偶、RTD、应变计、加速度计、电流探头、示波器、DAQ 模块与精密传感器并不共用一种万能线缆。组件可能引入电阻、电容、泄漏、摩擦电噪声、热电势、衰减或耦合，因此必须在设计前明确不确定度与带宽要求。

01

SYSTEM DEFINITION

系统定义

Define the measurand and source定义被测量与信号源

Match the cable to the signal source

让线缆匹配信号源

Low-resistance RTD circuits, microvolt thermocouples, bridge sensors, charge-output piezoelectric devices and high-speed digital instruments respond differently to cable resistance, capacitance and shielding. Record sensor type, excitation, output range, source impedance, channel bandwidth and instrument input. Extension wire, compensation cable, low-noise coax, triax and twisted-pair constructions are chosen for different error mechanisms; connector appearance is not enough.

低阻 RTD、微伏级热电偶、电桥传感器、电荷输出压电器件与高速数字仪器对线缆电阻、电容和屏蔽的反应不同。应记录传感器类型、激励、输出范围、源阻抗、通道带宽与仪器输入。补偿导线、低噪声同轴、三同轴与双绞线分别针对不同误差机制，不能只按连接器外观选择。

Shield, guard and ground are different conductors

屏蔽、保护环与地线承担不同作用

A cable shield intercepts electric-field coupling; a guard may be driven near the measured potential to reduce leakage; protective earth and signal reference serve other functions. Combining them without the instrument schematic can increase noise or create unsafe current paths. Define single-ended or differential measurement, shield termination, chassis connection and connector shell relationship. For low-level work, clean insulation surfaces and controlled cable handling can matter as much as nominal shielding coverage.

屏蔽层用于拦截电场耦合；保护环可被驱动到接近被测电位以降低泄漏；保护接地与信号参考又承担其他作用。脱离仪器原理图随意合并，可能增加噪声或形成不安全电流路径。应明确单端或差分测量、屏蔽端接、机壳连接与连接器外壳关系。低电平测量中，清洁绝缘表面和受控线缆操作可能与屏蔽覆盖同样重要。

02

CONSTRUCTION + INSTALLATION

结构与安装

Control the complete measurement path控制完整测量路径

Bandwidth belongs to the complete probe and cable path

带宽属于完整探头与线缆路径

Coaxial measurement paths require controlled impedance and suitable connectors; passive oscilloscope probes depend on a compensated divider matched to the instrument input. Cable length, source termination and adapter transitions affect rise time, reflection and amplitude. A continuity pass cannot prove frequency response. Define the connector family, impedance, attenuation ratio, maximum voltage, compensation range and required calibration or verification fixture.

同轴测量路径需要受控阻抗和合适连接器；无源示波器探头依赖与仪器输入匹配的补偿分压器。线缆长度、源端终端和转接过渡都会影响上升时间、反射与幅度。导通合格不能证明频率响应。应明确连接器家族、阻抗、衰减比、最高电压、补偿范围及所需校准或验证夹具。

Motion and temperature can create measurement error

运动与温度也会制造测量误差

Repeated flexing can change capacitance, generate triboelectric charge or fatigue shields and conductors. Temperature gradients across dissimilar metals can create thermoelectric voltages; long sensor routes can add resistance and pickup. Specify stationary, occasional-flex or continuous-motion duty, minimum bend radius, temperature zones and strain relief. Low-noise cable claims should be checked against the sensor and actual handling environment.

重复弯曲会改变电容、产生摩擦电荷，或使屏蔽与导体疲劳。异种金属之间的温度梯度会产生热电势，较长传感器路径会增加电阻与耦合。应规定固定、偶尔弯曲或连续运动工况、最小弯曲半径、温区与应力释放。低噪声线缆声明必须结合传感器和真实操作环境判断。

03

EVIDENCE + RELEASE

证据与放行

Calibrate the configured system校准实际配置系统

Calibrate the configured system and control substitutions

校准实际配置，并控制替代

Calibration establishes the performance of a defined instrument, sensor, lead, adapter and setup. Replacing cable length, connector material or wiring topology can change that configuration. Production records should preserve part numbers, pinout, polarity, shield treatment, length and inspection evidence. Where cable contribution is significant, include it in system calibration or an agreed comparison fixture rather than treating every electrically continuous lead as equivalent.

校准建立在规定仪器、传感器、引线、适配器与设置之上。更换线长、连接器材料或接线拓扑会改变该配置。生产记录应保持料号、针脚、极性、屏蔽处理、长度与检验证据。若线缆影响显著，应把它纳入系统校准或约定比对夹具，而不能把所有导通的引线视为等同。
