CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the blue connector multi-branch equipment harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以蓝色接头多分支设备线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Branched end layout分支端头布局

Conductors separate toward different ends, creating individual connection points. Branch lengths and directions can follow the equipment modules.导线向不同端头分出，形成独立接入位置。各支路可围绕设备模块分别安排长度与方向。

02

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

03

External color scheme外部配色

The external color scheme includes blue. Labels and assembly identification can be arranged around the corresponding ends or cable sections.外部配色包含蓝色，可结合端头或线束段的识别安排标签和装配指示。

04

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

05

Interface and function review接口与功能确认

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is a waterproof connector enough to make the harness waterproof?使用防水连接器就代表整条线束防水吗？

No. Both mated halves, seals, cavity plugs, wire diameters, cable entries, branches, strain relief and installed orientation affect the complete result.不代表。对插两端、密封、堵孔、线径、出线、分支、应力消除与安装方向都会影响完整结果。

Can you provide a specific IP rating?可以提供指定IP等级吗？

A target can be reviewed when the complete configuration, specimen, test method and acceptance criteria are defined. The category name alone is not an IP claim.完整配置、试样、测试方法与验收标准明确后可以审核目标，小类名称本身不构成IP等级承诺。

What information is needed for development?开发需要哪些资料？

Provide both mating interfaces, pin map, wire and cable diameters, unused cavities, installation direction, exposure, movement, quantity and the required test reference.请提供对插两端、针脚、线材与电缆外径、空腔、安装方向、暴露、运动、数量与所需测试依据。
