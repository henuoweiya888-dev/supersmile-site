Does outdoor suitability include direct burial and long-term immersion?户外适用是否包含直埋和长期浸水？

What documents must a weathering claim identify?耐候声明需要对应什么文件？

Why is water entry at cable ends a separate concern?端头进水为什么需要单独防护？
