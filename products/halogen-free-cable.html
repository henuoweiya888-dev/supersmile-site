ENGINEERING OVERVIEW工程概览

Separate material composition, gas acidity, smoke and flame performance分别定义材料组成、气体酸度、烟密度与阻燃性能

IEC 60754-1 specifies a method for determining the amount of halogen acid gas evolved during combustion of materials from cables. Smoke density and flame propagation are evaluated by other standards. A project should therefore name the exact gas, smoke and flame requirements instead of assuming one “LSZH” claim proves all three.

IEC 60754-1 规定了电缆材料燃烧时卤酸气体释放量的测定方法；烟密度与火焰蔓延则由其他标准评价。因此项目应分别写明气体、烟密度与阻燃要求，不能假定一个“LSZH”声明同时证明三项性能。

01

SYSTEM DEFINITION

系统定义

Define the material claim定义材料声明

Halogen content and combustion gas are defined by method

卤素含量与燃烧气体必须按方法定义

Halogen-free claims may refer to elemental composition, halogen-acid gas released during combustion, or a customer material restriction. These are related but not identical questions. State the applicable test method, specimen source, threshold and report requirement. Material declarations should identify insulation, jacket, fillers, tapes and printed markings because a complete cable contains more than its outer sheath.

无卤声明可能指元素组成、燃烧时释放的卤酸气体，或客户材料限制，这些问题相关但并不相同。应写明适用试验方法、取样位置、限值与报告要求。材料声明还应覆盖绝缘、护套、填充、包带和印字，因为完整电缆并不只有外护套。

Low smoke is a separate visibility requirement

低烟属于独立的能见度要求

Smoke-density testing evaluates light transmission under defined combustion conditions. It does not by itself establish halogen content, toxicity, flame propagation or circuit integrity. In rail vehicles, tunnels, public buildings, data centres and enclosed machinery, visibility can be part of the evacuation or maintenance strategy. Specify the exact smoke method and limit alongside, not instead of, the material and flame requirements.

烟密度试验在规定燃烧条件下评价透光率，并不能单独证明卤素含量、毒性、火焰蔓延或线路完整性。在轨道车辆、隧道、公共建筑、数据中心和封闭机械中，能见度可能属于疏散或维护策略的一部分。应把准确烟密度方法与限值，与材料和阻燃要求并列写出。

02

CONSTRUCTION + INSTALLATION

结构与安装

Separate smoke, flame and mechanics区分烟、阻燃与机械性能

LSZH compounds bring mechanical trade-offs

LSZH 配方伴随机械性能取舍

Mineral-filled polyolefin compounds are common in low-smoke halogen-free constructions, but compound family and formulation affect flexibility, tear resistance, abrasion, cold bend, oil resistance, extrusion and colour stability. A material suitable for a fixed building cable may not suit a robot, hand-held lead or outdoor harness. Conductor, insulation, filler and jacket must be selected against the complete electrical and mechanical duty.

矿物填充聚烯烃常用于低烟无卤结构，但材料体系与配方会影响柔性、抗撕裂、耐磨、低温弯曲、耐油、挤出与颜色稳定性。适合固定建筑电缆的材料不一定适合机器人、手持引线或户外线束。导体、绝缘、填充和护套必须结合完整电气与机械工况选择。

03

EVIDENCE + RELEASE

证据与放行

Control the exact construction控制准确结构

Public transport and enclosed equipment need a property matrix

公共交通与封闭设备需要性能矩阵

Rail, marine, building, appliance and industrial projects can call for different combinations of halogen gas, smoke, flame, voltage, temperature, oil, UV and mechanical performance. Do not copy one sector’s specification into another without checking scope. The bill of materials, cable print, supplier declaration and test report should point to the same exact construction and revision so procurement substitutions remain visible.

轨道、船舶、建筑、电器和工业项目可能要求不同组合的卤酸气体、烟密度、阻燃、电压、温度、耐油、紫外与机械性能。不能未经范围审核就把一个行业的规格复制到另一个行业。BOM、线缆印字、供应商声明与试验报告应指向同一准确结构和版本，让采购替代保持可见。

Engineering inputs工程输入资料

  1. 01Exact halogen, acidity, conductivity, smoke and flame methods准确卤素、酸度、电导率、烟密度与阻燃方法
  2. 02Electrical, temperature, movement and environmental duty电气、温度、运动与环境工况
  3. 03Cable marking, material declaration and report traceability线缆印字、材料声明与报告追溯

No. These properties use different test methods and must be specified separately.

不是。这些性能采用不同试验方法，必须分别规定。
