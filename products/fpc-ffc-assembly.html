Configuration & Project Review

01 FFC or FPC interface

Confirm whether the design needs a flat-flex jumper, printed flex circuit or connectorized FFC harness.

02 Contact geometry

Define pitch, circuit count, contact side, exposed contact length and stiffener thickness.

03 Mating connector

Use the exact mating connector drawing to check contact orientation and insertion depth.

04 Fold and dynamic route

Identify static folds, bend zones and any repeated motion before choosing the flex construction.

05 Signal and ground plan

For high-speed links, define impedance, ground allocation and any required shield layer.

06 Assembly acceptance

Agree circuit mapping, dimensions, insertion/retention and device function checks.

Send the part number, drawing or sample to confirm the replacement configuration and sourcing plan.
