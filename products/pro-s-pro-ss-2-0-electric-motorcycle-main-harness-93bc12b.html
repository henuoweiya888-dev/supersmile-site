PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does this category include high-voltage battery or traction cables?这个小类包括高压电池或牵引电缆吗？

No. It covers project-defined low-voltage auxiliary power, control, sensing, communication and diagnostic circuits. High-voltage requests require separate capability and compliance review.不包括。本小类覆盖项目定义的低压辅助供电、控制、传感、通信与诊断回路，高压需求需要独立能力与合规审核。

How is the low-voltage boundary defined?低压边界怎样定义？

The customer provides nominal and maximum voltage, platform rules, circuit list and isolation boundaries. The label low voltage is not assumed from connector size or application name.由客户提供标称与最高电压、平台规则、回路与隔离边界，不能根据接口大小或应用名称自行判断。

Can power and communication circuits share one harness?供电与通信回路可以放在同一线束里吗？

They may when current paths, separation, pair structure, shielding, grounding and connector capacity are reviewed for the actual controller architecture.可以，但必须结合实际控制器架构审核电流路径、隔离、线对结构、屏蔽、接地与接口容量。
