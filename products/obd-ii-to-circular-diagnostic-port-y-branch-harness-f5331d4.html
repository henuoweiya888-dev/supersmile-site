CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the obd-ii to circular diagnostic port y-branch harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以OBD-II转圆形诊断口Y形线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Y-shaped breakoutY形分出结构

The breakout follows a Y-shaped route toward separate ends. Bend clearance and fixing can be arranged around the split.分出位置形成Y形走向，可把一处接入分向不同端头。安装时围绕分出点安排弯曲和固定。

02

Circular connection end圆形连接端

The end uses a circular interface form, with connection positions planned around mating direction, retention and cable exit.端头具有圆形接口形式，可围绕对插方向、端部固定和线缆出口规划连接位置。

03

OBD-form connection endOBD形式接入端

The OBD-form end connects at an equipment or vehicle access position. Allow space for insertion, removal and cable turns.端头以OBD接口形式连接到设备或车辆接入位置，布局需保留插入、拔出及线缆转向空间。

04

End transition layout端头转接与组合

The assembly organizes a transition or combined connection, with end directions, transition sections and fixing arranged around the equipment positions.组件以转接或组合形式组织连接，可围绕两端设备位置安排端头方向、过渡段与固定。

05

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Interfaces within engine-bay layouts发动机舱内的接口布置

Map heat sources, fluid exposure and service routes together. Allow assembly space around connector ends and cable supports. Record the exact Aptiv family and complete contact and housing suffixes.发动机舱连接要把热源、油液暴露和检修路径放在同一张布线图中，端头与固定点也需留出装配空间。选配时，记录具体Aptiv系列和端子、壳体的完整后缀。

Vehicle control-module leads车辆控制模块引线

Separate interface cavities, power circuits and signal circuits at controller leads. Set branch dimensions from the module and mounting-point positions. Match seals and plugs to actual cavities, wire diameters and materials.控制模块尾线需要清楚区分接口腔位、电源和信号回路；分支尺寸应对应模块与固定点的位置。选配时，线封与盲塞按实际孔位、导线外径及材料配套。

Vehicle service connections车辆检修转接位置

Provide access for mating and service tools. Record end-face views and cable retention after service; appearance alone does not establish interchangeability. Set separate inspection points for TPA, CPA and mechanical keying.可拆卸尾线应保留插拔与工具空间，并记录端视图和维修后的固定方式，避免只凭外观确定替换关系。选配时，TPA、CPA及机械键位分别形成装配检查点。

Equipment control-board connections设备控制板连接

Document pin-row views, mating direction and nearby wire support. Check access to the latch and contacts after assembly. Create a controlled cavity connection table for the target vehicle mate.板端连接要给出针排视图、插合方向和近端线缆支撑；检查装配后是否仍能操作锁扣并查看接点。选配时，按目标车辆对插件建立受控腔位连接表。

Chassis and mobile-machine connections底盘与移动机械连接

Keep routing clear of moving parts, abrasion points and water traps. Define connector orientation and nearby retention with the installation route. Record the exact Aptiv family and complete contact and housing suffixes.底盘和移动机械的走线需要避开运动件、磨损点与积水位置，接口朝向和近端固定应随安装路径一起定义。选配时，记录具体Aptiv系列和端子、壳体的完整后缀。

Vehicle lighting and body connections车载灯具与车身连接

Coordinate mating clearance, cable retention and body routing around lighting units. Define circuit functions from the target equipment wiring diagram. Match seals and plugs to actual cavities, wire diameters and materials.灯具附近的引线需要协调插接空间、线缆固定和车身走线路径，电路功能以目标设备接线图为依据。选配时，线封与盲塞按实际孔位、导线外径及材料配套。
