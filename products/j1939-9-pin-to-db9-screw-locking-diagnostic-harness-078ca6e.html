PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does every nine-pin heavy-duty connector provide the same J1939 access?所有九针重型车辆接口都提供相同 J1939 访问吗？

No. Keying, connector generation, populated cavities, power arrangement and available network channels can differ. The exact equipment and released pin definition must be checked.不是。防错、连接器代次、装针、供电安排和可用网络通道都可能不同，必须核对准确设备与放行针脚定义。

What is the difference between black Type 1 and green Type 2 interfaces?黑色 Type 1 与绿色 Type 2 接口有什么区别？

They use different keying associated with different J1939 physical-layer generations. A color observation supports identification but does not replace confirmation of the vehicle network, speed and actual pin population.两者使用与不同 J1939 物理层代次相关的防错结构。颜色观察有助于识别，但不能替代对车辆网络、速率和实际装针的确认。

Can one splitter support a diagnostic tool and telematics unit at the same time?一根分支线可以同时连接诊断仪和车联网设备吗？

Sometimes, but only after reviewing branch length, device loading, termination, traffic, power draw, ground paths and the vehicle's permitted connected state.有时可以，但必须审核支线长度、设备负载、终端、通信流量、取电、接地路径及车辆允许的连接状态。
