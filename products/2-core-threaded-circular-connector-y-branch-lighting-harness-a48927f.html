CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Conductor, insulation and material choices导体、绝缘与选材要点

Use the 2-core threaded circular connector y-branch lighting harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以二芯圆形螺纹接头Y型灯光线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Y-shaped breakoutY形分出结构

The breakout follows a Y-shaped route toward separate ends. Bend clearance and fixing can be arranged around the split.分出位置形成Y形走向，可把一处接入分向不同端头。安装时围绕分出点安排弯曲和固定。

02

Circular connection end圆形连接端

The end uses a circular interface form, with connection positions planned around mating direction, retention and cable exit.端头具有圆形接口形式，可围绕对插方向、端部固定和线缆出口规划连接位置。

03

Threaded coupling form螺纹连接形态

The interface has a threaded coupling form, with clearance arranged for tightening, end orientation and cable exit.接口带螺纹连接形态，布局时可以为旋紧操作、端头方向和线缆出口预留空间。

04

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

05

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Outdoor equipment and cable routes室外设备与电缆路径

Describe sunlight, rain, pooling water and retention on outdoor routes. Conduit installation and direct exposure need different material and acceptance requirements. Provide temperature range, sunlight and ultraviolet exposure conditions.室外路径应说明日照、雨水、积水和固定方式，穿管或直接暴露属于不同的材料与验收条件。选配时，提供温度范围、日照与紫外线暴露条件。

Equipment junction boxes and entry positions设备接线盒与入口位置

Define entry orientation, water-trap risks and service access at junction boxes. Record the seal-to-jacket combination separately. State rain, pooling water, conduit, burial or direct-burial duty separately.接线盒入口需要说明安装朝向、积水风险和检修空间；密封件与电缆护套的匹配关系单独记录。选配时，分别注明雨水、积水、穿管、埋地或直埋用途。

Exposed routing at rooftop equipment屋顶设备的外露走线

Coordinate supports, bends and entry positions at rooftop equipment. Select jackets and sealing boundaries for long-term exposure conditions. Tie jacket material and weathering data to the exact supplied model.屋顶设备走线需要协调支撑、转弯和接入位置，护套选型与密封边界要考虑长期暴露条件。选配时，护套材料与耐候数据须对应准确供货型号。

Equipment sensors and moving detection points设备传感器与移动检测点

Define signal type, mounting position and movement at sensor leads. Select shielding and grounding for the equipment noise environment. Record installation tension, abrasion points and sealing at both ends.传感器引线需明确测量信号、安装位置和运动范围，屏蔽与接地选择应对应设备干扰环境。选配时，记录安装拉力、磨损点及两端密封结构。

Enclosure entries and cable retention机箱穿线与入口固定

Match cable entries to actual diameter, panel thickness and fixing method. Keep jacket retention independent of the internal termination. Provide temperature range, sunlight and ultraviolet exposure conditions.线缆入口需匹配实际外径、板厚和固定方式；护套夹持与内部端接之间应保留独立的支撑路径。选配时，提供温度范围、日照与紫外线暴露条件。

Internal machine connections and service设备机内连接与维护

Keep internal connections clear of working zones and moving parts. Allow appropriate cable length between supports, interfaces and service paths. State rain, pooling water, conduit, burial or direct-burial duty separately.设备机内连接要避开加工区与运动部件，并在固定点、接口和检修路径之间保留合理线缆余量。选配时，分别注明雨水、积水、穿管、埋地或直埋用途。
