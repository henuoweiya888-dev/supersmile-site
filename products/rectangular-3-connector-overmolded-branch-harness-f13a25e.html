CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the rectangular 3-connector overmolded branch harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以矩形三接头注塑分支线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Branched end layout分支端头布局

Conductors separate toward different ends, creating individual connection points. Branch lengths and directions can follow the equipment modules.导线向不同端头分出，形成独立接入位置。各支路可围绕设备模块分别安排长度与方向。

02

Rectangular interface form矩形接口外形

The rectangular interface form allows end position and service clearance to follow the mating face, latch and cable exit.接口采用矩形轮廓，可围绕插入面、卡扣与线束出口安排端头位置和拆装空间。

03

Molded-end outline包覆端头轮廓

The end transition has an overmolded form. Its outline can be configured around mating access and cable-turn clearance.端头过渡段具有包覆形态，可围绕接口插拔和线缆转向空间调整外部轮廓。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

External interfaces on portable equipment便携设备的外部接口

Consider handling, plug grip and cable bends together. Align the exit direction with the device position during use. Define boot length, profile, exit angle and nearby fixing points.便携设备连接要同时考虑操作手势、插头握持和尾部弯曲空间，线缆出口方向应与实际使用姿态协调。选配时，定义尾套长度、外形、出线角度和附近固定点。

Cable exits and nearby support电缆出口与近端支撑

Keep bending loads away from contacts through appropriate rear support. Review exit angle, retention distance and boot dimensions with the enclosure. Select hardness and bonding for the jacket material and intended bending duty.端头后方的支撑要避免把弯折载荷直接传到接点，出线角度、固定距离和尾套尺寸需与机壳一起评审。选配时，按护套材料与目标弯曲条件选硬度和粘接方式。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Insert support, parting lines and molding conditions should protect internal terminations.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，嵌件支撑、分型位置和成型条件应保护内部端接。

Equipment panels and circular interfaces设备面板与圆形接口

Start with the cutout, panel thickness, mounting direction and rear clearance. Coordinate contact arrangement and cable exit with the complete assembly view. Verify bending, pull force and fixing retention in the installed configuration.面板连接以开孔、板厚、装配方向和后方净空为起点；接点排列与电缆出口还需要对应完整总装视图。选配时，按完整安装状态验证弯折、拉力与固定保持。

Detachable circular connections可拆卸圆形连接

Check gender, keying, coupling and end-face views together. Equal contact counts do not replace confirmation of the exact series and part numbers. Define boot length, profile, exit angle and nearby fixing points.圆形接口需要同时核对公母、键位、锁紧方式和端视图，针数相同并不能替代完整系列与料号确认。选配时，定义尾套长度、外形、出线角度和附近固定点。

Enclosure entries and cable retention机箱穿线与入口固定

Match cable entries to actual diameter, panel thickness and fixing method. Keep jacket retention independent of the internal termination. Select hardness and bonding for the jacket material and intended bending duty.线缆入口需匹配实际外径、板厚和固定方式；护套夹持与内部端接之间应保留独立的支撑路径。选配时，按护套材料与目标弯曲条件选硬度和粘接方式。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does a molded boot alone establish waterproofing?模制尾套本身能证明防水吗？

External molded geometry alone does not establish sealing. Define boot length, profile, exit angle and nearby fixing points.外部模制结构不能单独证明防水。定义尾套长度、外形、出线角度和附近固定点。

Why inspect the transition between soft and rigid materials?软硬材料交界为什么需要检查？

Check bend concentration at soft/hard material transitions. Select hardness and bonding for the jacket material and intended bending duty.软硬材料交界处需检查弯折集中。按护套材料与目标弯曲条件选硬度和粘接方式。

How does molding protect the internal cable and joints?包胶工艺如何保护内部线缆和焊点？

Process temperature must not damage internal insulation or solder joints. Insert support, parting lines and molding conditions should protect internal terminations.工艺温度不能损伤内部绝缘或焊点。嵌件支撑、分型位置和成型条件应保护内部端接。
