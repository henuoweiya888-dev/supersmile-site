CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the d-sub and circular interface harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以D-SUB与圆形接口混合控制线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

D-Sub interface endD-Sub接口端

The D-Sub-form end allows equipment connection to be arranged around the mating face, fixing screws and rear cable route.端头采用D-Sub形式，可围绕插合面、固定螺钉和后部线缆走向安排设备连接。

02

Circular connection end圆形连接端

The end uses a circular interface form, with connection positions planned around mating direction, retention and cable exit.端头具有圆形接口形式，可围绕对插方向、端部固定和线缆出口规划连接位置。

03

End transition layout端头转接与组合

The assembly organizes a transition or combined connection, with end directions, transition sections and fixing arranged around the equipment positions.组件以转接或组合形式组织连接，可围绕两端设备位置安排端头方向、过渡段与固定。

04

Circular mating interface圆形接口匹配

Match the series, keying, contact count, mating direction and locking method. Similar shell outlines do not establish compatible dimensions or contact assignments.接口选型需核对系列、键位、针数、插合方向与锁紧方式；相近外形不能替代对插尺寸和接点定义。

05

Multipin interface and exit多针接口与出线

Review contact mapping, screw or latch retention, backshell space and cable exit together. Keep contact positions and circuit revision identifiable after assembly.针位对应、螺钉或锁紧结构、后壳空间和出线方向需要一起审核，装配后应能够识别针位和电路版本。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does industrial equipment harness mean one standard product?工业设备线束是一个固定产品吗？

No. It is a family covering cabinet, motor, sensor, I/O, sealed, internal and moving harnesses. The actual assembly is defined by your electrical and mechanical data.不是。它覆盖控制柜、电机、传感器、I/O、密封、机内及运动线束等多种结构，最终产品依据项目的电气与机械资料定义。

What can I send if I do not have a complete drawing?没有完整图纸时可以提供什么？

A sample, connector photos, mating-part references, pin table, equipment layout and required lengths can start the review. Unknown electrical functions must still be confirmed before production.可先提供样品、接口照片、对插型号、针脚表、设备布局和长度要求；不确定的电气功能仍需在生产前确认。

Can you make prototypes and small batches?可以做样品和小批量吗？

Yes. Prototype and small-batch builds can be used to check fit, routing and electrical mapping before a controlled repeat order.可以。可先通过样品或小批量确认装配、布线和电气对应，再转入受控的重复生产。
