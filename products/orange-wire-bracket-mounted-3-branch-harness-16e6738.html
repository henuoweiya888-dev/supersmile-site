CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the orange wire bracket-mounted 3-branch harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以橙色导线带固定支架三分支线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

02

Conductor and insulation selection导体与绝缘选配

Select conductors and insulation from circuit loads, diameter limits, bending and exposure. A material name does not replace ratings or validation results.从电路负载、外径限制、弯曲方式与环境条件选择导体和绝缘体系；材料名称不应直接代替额定参数或验证结果。

03

Interface and function review接口与功能确认

Orange jacket does not establish high-voltage ratings橙色护套不能证明高压额定

04

Installation and acceptance安装与验收

Validate interlock continuity separately from the power circuit联锁连续性与功率回路分别验证

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does orange insulation prove an 800 V rating?橙色绝缘就代表可以用于 800 V 吗？

No. Identification color is separate from voltage rating. Confirm the cable and connector variants, insulation coordination and the project's verification requirements.不是。识别颜色与额定电压是不同概念，需要确认线材和接头变型、绝缘配合及项目验证要求。

Can the same harness serve every EV platform?同一套线束可以适配所有电动车平台吗？

No. Current duty, interfaces, control functions, route and environment change by platform. A similar-looking connector does not establish interchangeability.不能。电流任务、接口、控制功能、走线和环境随平台变化，外观相似的接头并不代表可以互换。

What should be supplied for engineering review?工程审核需要提供哪些资料？

Provide system voltage, current profile, circuit and interface drawings, route dimensions, environmental requirements, quantity and the required test or approval documents.请提供系统电压、电流曲线、回路与接口图、路线尺寸、环境要求、数量以及所需试验或批准文件。
