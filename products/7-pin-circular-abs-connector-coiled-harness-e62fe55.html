CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the 7-pin circular abs connector coiled harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以七针圆形ABS接头螺旋线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Circular connection end圆形连接端

The end uses a circular interface form, with connection positions planned around mating direction, retention and cable exit.端头具有圆形接口形式，可围绕对插方向、端部固定和线缆出口规划连接位置。

02

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

03

Coiled or spiral form卷绕或螺旋形态

The cable is arranged in a coiled or spiral form, with straight tails and ends placed for storage or connection positions.线缆以卷绕或螺旋形态排列，直线尾段与端头可以结合收纳或连接位置安排。

04

Circular mating interface圆形接口匹配

Match the series, keying, contact count, mating direction and locking method. Similar shell outlines do not establish compatible dimensions or contact assignments.接口选型需核对系列、键位、针数、插合方向与锁紧方式；相近外形不能替代对插尺寸和接点定义。

05

Extension and retraction伸缩与回弹空间

Define retracted and extended lengths, coil diameter and straight tails from the working stroke. Repeated motion also requires review of retraction force, fixing points and cable fatigue.结合工作行程确认收缩长度、伸展长度、圈径和直线尾段；反复运动时还需审核回弹力、固定点与线材疲劳表现。

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Handheld equipment coiled leads手持设备卷线

Define extension and recovery from normal working reach and equipment loading. Record retracted length, normal working extension and coil diameter separately.以正常工作距离和设备受力定义拉伸与回收状态。选配时，分别记录收缩长度、正常工作伸长和圈径。

Moving bench leads移动台架引线

Retracted envelope, coil diameter and tail direction must suit the bench motion space. Define coil direction, straight-tail lengths and both cable exits.收缩包络、圈径和直尾方向要适合台架运动空间。选配时，确定螺旋方向、直尾长度及两端出线方向。

03

Extensible connections可伸缩连接

Confirm restoring force, fixing positions and termination protection as one moving system. Select conductor and jacket construction for restoring force and operating duty together.将回弹力、固定位置与端接保护作为一个运动系统确认。选配时，导体与护套结构按回复力和工况共同选择。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is maximum extension the same as long-term working reach?最大拉伸长度等于长期工作长度吗？

Maximum extension is not the continuous working length. Record retracted length, normal working extension and coil diameter separately.最大拉伸长度不等于长期工作长度。分别记录收缩长度、正常工作伸长和圈径。

Does coil appearance establish cycle life?卷绕外观能证明循环寿命吗？

Coiled appearance does not establish extension-cycle life. Define coil direction, straight-tail lengths and both cable exits.卷绕外观不能证明耐伸缩寿命。确定螺旋方向、直尾长度及两端出线方向。

Can the terminals directly carry the coiled-cord tension?卷线拉力可以直接由端子承受吗？

Do not transfer extension strain directly to terminals. Select conductor and jacket construction for restoring force and operating duty together.伸缩应力不得直接传至端子。导体与护套结构按回复力和工况共同选择。
