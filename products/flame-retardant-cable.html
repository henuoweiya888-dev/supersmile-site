Specify the fire test, cable arrangement and acceptance evidence明确燃烧试验、敷设方式与验收证据

A useful flame-retardant cable specification names the exact test method, specimen arrangement, flame application, duration and acceptance criteria. IEC 60332 contains different tests for a single insulated wire or cable and for vertically installed bunched cables. Passing one method cannot be translated into every installation.

有效的阻燃电缆规格必须写明准确试验方法、试样排列、施焰方式、持续时间和判定条件。IEC 60332 包含单根绝缘电线电缆试验，以及垂直安装成束电缆试验。通过其中一种方法，不能直接推导所有安装场景。

01

SYSTEM DEFINITION

系统定义

Name the fire-test question明确燃烧试验问题

Single cable and bunched cable tests ask different questions

单根与成束试验回答不同问题

A single vertical specimen test evaluates flame propagation on one wire or cable under prescribed conditions. A bunched-cable test introduces multiple cables, defined non-metallic volume and ladder arrangements, creating a more severe propagation scenario. Project drawings should state how cables are installed and which method applies. Generic terms such as FR, flame proof or self-extinguishing are not sufficient release criteria.

单根垂直试样试验用于评估一根电线或电缆在规定条件下的火焰蔓延；成束试验则引入多根电缆、规定的非金属材料体积和梯架排列，形成更严苛的蔓延场景。项目图纸应注明实际敷设方式和适用方法。“FR”“防火”或“自熄”等笼统词语不能作为放行标准。

Compound, wall thickness and cable geometry work together

材料配方、壁厚与线缆几何共同作用

PVC, polyolefin and other compounds can be formulated for flame-retardant behaviour, but the finished result also depends on insulation and jacket thickness, fillers, shield, cable diameter and manufacturing consistency. A resin trade name or additive does not certify the completed cable. Electrical rating, flexibility, abrasion, temperature and chemical resistance still need independent review when the fire formulation changes.

PVC、聚烯烃等材料可以通过配方获得阻燃特性，但成品表现还取决于绝缘和护套壁厚、填充、屏蔽、外径及制造一致性。树脂名称或某一种添加剂不能证明成品电缆合格。改变阻燃配方时，电气额定、柔性、耐磨、温度与化学介质性能仍需分别审核。

02

CONSTRUCTION + INSTALLATION

结构与安装

Separate material and fire properties区分材料与火灾性能

Flame retardancy, fire resistance, smoke and acidity are separate properties

阻燃、耐火、烟密度与酸性气体是不同性能

A flame-retardant cable is intended to limit propagation after ignition. A fire-resistant or circuit-integrity cable is evaluated for continued function during a defined fire exposure. Smoke density and halogen-acid gas are covered by separate test families. If a railway, public building, data centre or enclosed equipment project needs several properties, each test and threshold should be written explicitly rather than compressed into one marketing label.

阻燃电缆用于限制点燃后的火焰蔓延；耐火或线路完整性电缆则评价在规定火灾暴露中继续工作的能力。烟密度和卤酸气体另有独立试验体系。轨道交通、公共建筑、数据中心或封闭设备若同时需要多项性能，应逐项写明试验与限值，不能压缩成一个营销标签。

03

EVIDENCE + RELEASE

证据与放行

Release traceable evidence放行可追溯证据

Release the exact marking and supporting report

放行准确印字与对应报告

The purchase specification should link the cable designation, conductor size, voltage and temperature rating, flame-test method, report or certification reference, production marking and applicable revision. Incoming inspection checks the identity of the supplied cable; assembly inspection preserves spacing, sleeving and routing that may affect the installed fire load. Super Smile records agreed material and process evidence but does not convert a component report into equipment certification.

采购规格应把线缆型号、导体截面、电压与温度等级、燃烧试验方法、报告或认证编号、生产印字和适用版本关联起来。来料检查确认线材身份，装配检查则保持可能影响安装火载荷的间距、套管与布线。超斯迈尔记录约定的材料和工艺证据，但不会把元件报告等同于整机认证。

Engineering inputs工程输入资料

  2. 02Cable construction, installed grouping and equipment fire strategy线缆结构、安装成束方式与设备防火策略
  3. 03Required reports, marking, traceability and inspection scope所需报告、印字、追溯与检验范围

Common questions常见问题

Does flame-retardant mean the cable keeps working in a fire?阻燃是否表示火灾中仍能工作？

Not necessarily. Circuit integrity or fire resistance is a different performance claim with a different test method.

不一定。线路完整性或耐火属于另一类性能声明，需要不同试验方法。
