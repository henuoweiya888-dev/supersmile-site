PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is truck harness one standard product?卡车线束是一种标准产品吗？

No. It is a broad category that includes many complete and zone-specific assemblies. Vehicle platform, year, wheelbase, cab, powertrain, installed options and body equipment determine the released configuration.不是。它是包含整车线束与多种区域分线束的大类，车辆平台、年份、轴距、驾驶室、动力总成、选装与上装共同决定放行配置。

Can a harness be copied from an existing sample?可以按照现有样品复制线束吗？

A sample is useful for geometry, connector and circuit tracing, but hidden materials, damaged areas, field repairs, option content and electrical intent still need documentation or controlled validation.样品有助于还原几何、连接器和回路，但隐藏材料、损伤区域、现场维修、选装内容与电气意图仍需文件或受控验证。

Can 12 V and 24 V truck harnesses use the same design?12 V 与 24 V 卡车线束可以共用同一设计吗？

Not by assumption. Load current, device rating, fuse strategy, wire size, relay or controller behavior, connector limits and voltage-drop budget must be reviewed for the actual system.不能直接假设。必须针对实际系统审核负载电流、设备额定值、保险策略、线径、继电器或控制器行为、连接器限制与压降预算。
