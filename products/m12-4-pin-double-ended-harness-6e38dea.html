APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These connection directions concern circular equipment interfaces. Confirm fit, circuits and performance from the actual equipment data.以下连接方向围绕圆形设备接口展开，实际适配、回路与性能依据具体设备资料确认。

Sensors and detection modules传感器与检测模块

Plan equipment connections from the sensor interface family, keying and pin definition. Measure cable length from the fixing positions.依据传感器的接口系列、键位和针脚定义安排设备接入点，线长从固定位置量取。

Control cabinets and I/O connections控制柜与I/O接入

Map circular-interface pins to cabinet endpoints and preserve access for mating, coupling and service.将圆形接口的针号与柜内端点逐一对应，保留插拔、锁紧和检修操作空间。

Instruments and measurement modules仪器与测量模块

Separate power, signal and reference circuits using the instrument documentation. Interface shape does not define the signal function.按仪器资料区分电源、信号与参考回路；接口形状不能代替实际信号定义。

Machine-tool auxiliary interfaces机床与设备辅助接口

Route leads around sensors, auxiliary modules and equipment fixing points. Confirm oil exposure, temperature and any movement.围绕传感器、辅助模块与设备固定点安排连接线，并确认油污、温度与运动条件。

Analytical and automation modules分析设备与自动化模块

Define end orientation, cable exits and length around module positions and removal paths, using the equipment interface data.结合模块位置和拆装路径定义端头朝向、出线与线长，保持与设备接口资料一致。

Equipment panels and service connections设备面板与维护连接

Check complete mating references, contact arrangement and coupling before replacement or extension. Allow cable bends for the installation position.更换或延长连接前核对完整对插型号、接点排列和锁紧结构，按安装位置预留弯曲空间。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Can M12 leads with equal contact counts be substituted directly?针数相同的M12线可以直接替换吗？

No. Check coding, keying, gender, pin numbering, coupling and the equipment circuit definition.不能。还需核对编码、键位、公母、针号、锁紧结构和设备回路定义。

Does a metal shell or threaded shape establish shielding and waterproofing?金属壳体或螺纹外形能证明屏蔽与防水吗？

No. Confirm shield termination, shell continuity, seals, cable diameter and the complete mated state for the exact configuration.不能。屏蔽端接、壳体连续、密封件、线缆外径及完整插合状态都需要按具体配置确认。

Can a coiled or extended lead inherit the original cable performance?卷线或延长线能直接沿用原线的性能吗？

Not automatically. Review working length, bends and fixing, voltage drop or signal path, and the required mechanical and environmental validation.不能直接沿用。需重新核对使用长度、弯曲与固定、压降或信号路径，以及要求的机械和环境验证。
