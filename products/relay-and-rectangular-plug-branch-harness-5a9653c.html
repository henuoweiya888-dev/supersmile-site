CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the relay & rectangular plug branch harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以继电器及矩形插头分支线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Branched end layout分支端头布局

Conductors separate toward different ends, creating individual connection points. Branch lengths and directions can follow the equipment modules.导线向不同端头分出，形成独立接入位置。各支路可围绕设备模块分别安排长度与方向。

02

Relay connection section继电器连接部位

Ends or branches are organized around relay connection points. Routing can distinguish control-side and switched-circuit paths.端头或分支围绕继电器连接位置组织，线束布局可区分控制端与受控回路的走向。

03

Rectangular interface form矩形接口外形

The rectangular interface form allows end position and service clearance to follow the mating face, latch and cable exit.接口采用矩形轮廓，可围绕插入面、卡扣与线束出口安排端头位置和拆装空间。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Can you build both a relay pigtail and a complete fuse-box harness?能制作继电器尾线和完整保险丝盒线束吗？

Yes. The scope can include a pre-wired socket, fused single branch, dual-relay kit or multi-circuit distribution assembly. Drawings, load data, interfaces and installation requirements define the build.可以，范围可包括预接插座、带熔断单路分支、双继电器套件或多回路配电组件，具体结构由图纸、负载资料、接口与安装要求定义。

Can the same assembly work on 12 V and 24 V?同一组件可以同时用于 12 V 和 24 V 吗？

Only when every affected component and operating condition supports both systems. Relay coil voltage, suppression, fuse voltage rating, electronics and load behavior require explicit review.只有相关元件与工况均支持两种系统时才可以，继电器线圈电压、抑制器件、熔断器额定电压、电子部件与负载行为都需明确审核。

Does a larger fuse solve repeated fuse failures?反复烧保险丝可以直接换更大的吗？

Not without reviewing the fault, load, conductor and holder. A larger fuse can leave a smaller branch inadequately protected. Investigate shorts, overload, inrush, poor contact and thermal conditions first.不能未经审核直接加大规格，更大保险丝可能使小线径分支保护不足。应先检查短路、过载、浪涌、接触不良和热条件。
