ENGINEERING OVERVIEW工程概览

Circular connections specified by series, contact and mating condition按系列、触点与插合状态定义圆形连接

Conxall is a Switchcraft brand with several distinct circular-connector families. Micro-Con-X, Mini-Con-X and Multi-Con-X differ in shell size, contact arrangements and available terminations. The broader Switchcraft/Conxall product line also offers shielded Mini-Con-X constructions with their own assembly rules. A round black connector is therefore not a sufficient compatibility specification. We begin with the customer's installed receptacle or cable-end part number, its matching-part documentation, pin map and environmental duty. Super Smile can review a cable assembly using specified original components or an approved alternative, but this page does not imply brand authorization, universal interchangeability or an environmental rating for an unverified finished lead.

Conxall 是 Switchcraft 旗下品牌，包含多个圆形连接器系列。Micro-Con-X、Mini-Con-X 与 Multi-Con-X 的外壳尺寸、触点排列和端接形式不同；Switchcraft／Conxall 产品线还提供有独立装配要求的屏蔽 Mini-Con-X 结构。因此，黑色圆形接头不足以说明兼容性。我们从客户设备上的插座或线端完整料号、官方配对资料、针脚表及环境工况开始审核。超斯迈尔可按指定原装零件或经认可的替代件评估电缆组件，但本页不表示品牌授权、跨系列通用互换，也不替未经验证的成品引线宣称防护等级。

FAMILY BEFORE FORM

先识别系列

Connector families连接器系列

Micro-Con-X and Mini-Con-X are different systems

Micro-Con-X 与 Mini-Con-X 是两套不同系统

The manufacturer's Micro-Con-X and Mini-Con-X ranges use different shell envelopes and contact layouts. Within each range, pin count, contact size, cable-end versus panel form and termination style vary by order code. One example Mini-Con-X cable-end part has a manufacturer-published list of mating panel components; that list is more useful than comparing outer diameters. First identify the installed side, then select the exact matching cable-side part and verify its drawing. A similarly sized connector from another family is not automatically a mate.

原厂 Micro-Con-X 与 Mini-Con-X 系列采用不同的外壳尺寸和触点排列；同一系列内部也会随订货号改变针数、触点规格、线端或面板形式及端接方式。某款 Mini-Con-X 线端件在原厂页面列有明确的对插面板件清单，这比单看外径更有用。应先识别设备上的一侧，再选准确匹配的线端料号并核对图纸。尺寸相近的其他系列不能直接视为可对插。

Multi-Con-X and shielded variants add different constraints

Multi-Con-X 与屏蔽版本有各自边界

Multi-Con-X includes larger contact arrangements and separate cable-end, in-line and panel configurations. Shielded Mini-Con-X uses a metal body and a defined path from the cable shield through its backshell. Some catalogue variants mix contact sizes for separate circuit duties. These are family-specific options, not a promise that any power and signal circuit can share one insert. Confirm contact arrangement, current and voltage limits, wire range, coupling method and the mating manufacturer's documentation for the selected exact part numbers.

Multi-Con-X 提供较大触点排列，以及线端、线对线和面板安装配置；屏蔽 Mini-Con-X 则采用金属外壳，并规定线缆屏蔽层到尾壳的连接路径。部分目录型号还提供不同规格触点混排，以容纳不同回路任务。这些都是按系列和料号定义的选项，并不意味着任意动力和信号回路都能共用插芯。仍须逐项核对触点排列、额定电压电流、线径、锁紧方式及官方对插资料。

02

EXACT MATING

准确对插

Exact mating准确对插

Match face, key, contact and installed panel together

插合面、键位、触点与面板一起核对

A mating review must use the full part number on both sides. Check series, pin and socket gender, numbered contact positions, key or polarization, coupling hardware and panel cutout. The wiring-side view can be the mirror of the mating-face view; copying pin numbers from an unlabeled photograph is an avoidable error. For panel mounting, verify wall thickness, gasket placement and the specified retaining parts. Record the approved pair and pin-to-pin map on the drawing so a later substitute requires a deliberate review.

对插审核须拿到两侧完整料号，并核对系列、公母触点、编号位置、防错键位、锁紧件和面板开孔。接线面与插合面可能互为镜像，照着一张未标视角的照片抄针号容易出错。面板安装还要核对板厚、垫圈位置和规定紧固件。把获认可的配对组合与逐针回路写进图纸，后续替代料才有明确的复核基准。

03

CONTACT + CABLE

触点与线缆

Cable and contact materials线材与触点

Contact size and termination set the wire range

触点规格与端接方式决定适用线径

Depending on the exact configuration, cable-end parts can use crimp or solder-cup contacts, while some panel or board-side parts use PCB tails. The contact's allowed conductor range and the cable-entry opening both matter; a wire that fits the solder cup may still be unsuitable for the backshell or seal. For crimp contacts, use the specified terminal and tooling and confirm seating in the insert. For solder contacts, follow the component's preparation and heat instructions. Leave no exposed strand where it can reach an adjacent contact or sealing surface.

依具体配置，线端件可采用压接或焊杯触点，部分面板或板端件使用 PCB 尾针。触点允许线径和线缆入口尺寸都要满足；导线能进入焊杯，也不代表其护套适合尾壳或密封件。压接触点应匹配指定端子与工具，并确认在插芯内锁定；焊接触点应遵守器件的备线和加热要求。裸露铜丝不能靠近相邻触点或密封面。

Cable diameter, jacket and molded exit must agree

电缆外径、护套与模制出口要相互匹配

Select conductor count and cable construction for the circuit, then check whether the jacket outside diameter fits the chosen connector's entry and strain-relief parts. A field-assembled backshell, a factory-molded end and a cable-to-cable body have different process and service boundaries. Molding can shape a compact exit, but its material compatibility, bend behavior and sealing depend on the actual design. Neither a glossy boot nor a sealed connector catalogue rating establishes the ingress rating of a third-party finished assembly.

先按回路确定芯数与线缆结构，再检查护套外径是否落在所选接头的进线和应力释放范围内。现场组装尾壳、工厂模制端和线对线外壳的工艺及维护边界不同。模制可以形成紧凑出口，但材料相容性、弯曲表现与密封性取决于实际设计。光滑尾套或原厂接头目录上的防护额定，都不能直接证明第三方整条成品的防护等级。

04

ENDPOINT ARCHITECTURE

端点结构

Connection solutions连接解决方案

Panel, cable-end and in-line leads solve different interfaces

面板、线端与线对线引线对应不同界面

A sensor lead may plug into an equipment panel, while an extension cable joins two cable ends in the field. Specify which side is fixed, which can be removed, the approach angle and how the assembly is retained. A right-angle molded end can help at a shallow panel, but the bend and mating clearance still need a dimensional check. For a branching lead, define every branch datum, connector orientation and circuit map; a Y shape is not enough to establish electrical function.

传感器引线可能接入设备面板，延长段则在线路中连接两个线端。应确定哪一侧固定、哪一侧可拆、插合角度及线缆固定方式。直角模制端有助于浅空间安装，但仍要核对弯曲与插合余量。若采用分支引线，每条分支的长度基准、接头朝向和回路映射都应明确；仅有 Y 形外观不能说明电气功能。

A shield works only when its path is defined

屏蔽层要有明确的连接路径

For a shielded Mini-Con-X construction, the manufacturer's assembly instructions specify jacket preparation, shield treatment, insulator orientation and backshell tightening. The shield should not wander onto signal contacts, and an arbitrary branch can interrupt its return path. State whether the equipment design calls for screen termination at one end, both ends or another defined arrangement. Use the actual cable, connector and installation layout to plan any EMC measurement; a metal shell alone is not proof of noise immunity.

屏蔽 Mini-Con-X 的原厂装配说明明确了护套剥除、屏蔽层处理、绝缘件方向与尾壳紧固。屏蔽层不能碰到信号触点，随意分支也可能中断回流路径。设备设计应规定屏蔽在单端、双端或其他方式端接。若要测量 EMC 表现，须以实际电缆、接头和安装布局为对象；金属外壳本身不能证明抗干扰能力。

APPLICATION DUTY

应用工况

Industries and applications行业与应用

Industrial sensing and machine control

工业传感与设备控制

Circular leads can connect a sensor, actuator, local controller or test fixture where the selected device actually uses this interface. The equipment designer defines supply, signal, return and any protective-earth path; the cable assembly then carries those paths to the matching endpoint. Confirm connector coding and cable routing around moving parts before selecting a length. A series name describes a connector family, not a protocol converter or permission to connect unlike device circuits.

如果设备实际采用相应接口，圆形引线可连接传感器、执行机构、就地控制器或测试夹具。供电、信号、回流与保护接地路径由设备设计方定义，电缆组件把这些路径送到匹配端点。定长前要核对接口防错与运动部件周围的布线路径。系列名称说明连接器家族，不表示协议转换，也不意味着不同设备回路可以随意接在一起。

Outdoor and cleaned equipment need an installation review

户外与清洗设备要审核完整安装状态

Rain, dust, oil, cleaning fluids and repeated unmating affect different parts of the connection. The official rating of one Con-X variant may depend on being mated and on a specified molded cable assembly; another variant has different conditions. Select the connector, cap, cable jacket, panel gasket and exit together for the site's exposure. If the application requires an IP claim, identify the precise part and complete test configuration instead of transferring a catalogue number to every assembled cable.

雨水、粉尘、油液、清洗剂与反复拆插会影响连接中的不同部位。某款 Con-X 型号的原厂防护额定可能以插合并采用指定模制线缆为条件；另一款型号的条件可能不同。应把接头、护盖、线缆护套、面板垫圈和出线方式一起选入现场工况。如果项目需要 IP 声明，应明确准确料号和完整受试配置，不能把目录数值平移到每条组装线缆。

APPROVAL EVIDENCE

认可依据

Project review项目审核

Release the approved part pair and inspection plan

按认可的对插组合与检验计划放行

The build package should hold both mating part numbers, manufacturer drawings, contact arrangement, cable specification, orientation, dimensions and label map. First-article checks can include visual seating, pin-to-pin continuity and short-circuit review. Pull, insulation, shield or ingress checks require agreed methods and acceptance limits. Record the exact revision and configuration actually examined. Super Smile's compatible-assembly service is independent of Switchcraft/Conxall; neither an original component nor a similar-looking substitute confers brand authorization or finished-product certification.

生产资料应同时保存两侧对插料号、原厂图纸、触点排列、线缆规格、朝向、尺寸与标识映射。首件可按计划检查触点到位、逐针导通和短路。拉力、绝缘、屏蔽或防护检查须另有约定的方法与验收限值，并记录实际检查的版本与配置。超斯迈尔的兼容组件服务独立于 Switchcraft/Conxall；无论使用原装零件还是外观相似的替代件，都不自动获得品牌授权或成品认证。

Common questions常见问题

Are Micro-Con-X, Mini-Con-X and Multi-Con-X interchangeable?Micro-Con-X、Mini-Con-X 和 Multi-Con-X 可以互换吗？

They are different connector families. Confirm the exact mating part numbers and drawings; similar circular shape or contact count does not establish interchangeability.

它们是不同连接器家族。须核对准确的对插料号和图纸；圆形外观或针数接近都不能证明互换。

Does a manufacturer's IP-rated connector make our completed cable IP-rated?原厂接头标有 IP 等级，整条成品电缆就有同等级吗？

No automatic transfer applies. A rating can depend on the precise variant, mating state, cable assembly and test configuration. A finished-lead claim needs evidence for that exact build.

不能自动沿用。额定可能取决于具体型号、插合状态、电缆组件结构和受试配置；成品声明须有针对准确结构的证据。

Can you build to a specified Conxall interface without being an authorized distributor?不是品牌授权经销商，也能按指定 Conxall 接口制作引线吗？

An independently built cable can be reviewed around specified original parts or customer-approved alternatives. We do not claim to be Switchcraft/Conxall or to hold its brand authorization; sourcing and permitted substitutions are confirmed for each order.

可围绕指定原装零件或客户认可的替代件审核独立制作的电缆组件。本页不宣称超斯迈尔属于 Switchcraft/Conxall 或持有品牌授权；物料来源与允许替代范围按订单确认。

What should be sent for a mating-connector quotation?询问对插组件报价应提供什么？

Send both connector references or clear face and label photographs, the device drawing, pin map, cable specification, length, installation environment, quantity and required inspection criteria.

请提供两侧接头料号，或清晰的插合面与标签照片，并附设备图、针脚表、线缆规格、长度、安装环境、数量及检验要求。
