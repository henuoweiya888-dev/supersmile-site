CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the ecu braided sleeve dual branch extension harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以ECU编织外套双分支延长连接线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Branched end layout分支端头布局

Conductors separate toward different ends, creating individual connection points. Branch lengths and directions can follow the equipment modules.导线向不同端头分出，形成独立接入位置。各支路可围绕设备模块分别安排长度与方向。

02

Braided outer form编织形态外层

The outer covering has a braided form. Coverage segments and finishing can follow breakouts, routing passages and fixing positions.外层呈编织形态，可按分支出口、穿线位置和固定点定义覆盖段与收尾。

03

Extension connection layout延伸连接布局

The lead extends the connection to another position, with length, end orientation and fixing routed to the target location.连接线把接入位置延伸到另一端，可围绕目标位置定义长度、端头朝向与固定路径。

04

Braided outer protection编织外层

A braid can organize the bundle and protect its surface. Define its material, coverage and end treatment around the routing path; distinguish insulating braid from metallic shielding.编织层可用于束线与表面防磨；材料、覆盖长度和端部收口需结合安装路径确定。绝缘编织与金属屏蔽编织应分别定义。

05

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is an ECU or DCU harness defined by the connector housing?ECU 或 DCU 线束可以只按连接器壳体定义吗？

No. The exact controller, cavity schedule, terminal and seal system, wire specifications, branches, loads, network topology, protection and mounting zone define the assembly.不能。准确控制器、孔位表、端子与密封体系、线材规格、分支、负载、网络拓扑、保护与安装区域共同定义组件。

Can you build from an existing damaged sample?可以依据损坏的现有样品制作吗？

A sample can support geometry and connector identification, but hidden splices, materials, shielding and earlier repairs require independent confirmation. Electrical data and the intended function are still needed.样品有助于确认几何与连接器，但隐藏接续、材料、屏蔽和既往维修仍需独立确认，同时仍需电气资料与预期功能。

Can a Y harness keep the ECU connected while measurements are taken?Y 型线束可以在保持 ECU 连接时进行测量吗？

Yes, when the branch topology, stub length, instrument loading, shielding, exposed-point protection and connected-state test method are approved for the target circuits.可以，但需针对目标回路批准分支拓扑、支线长度、仪器负载、屏蔽、裸露测点保护与连接状态测试方法。
