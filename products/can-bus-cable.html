Selection & Project Review

01 Application Boundary

The exact CAN profile, physical layer, topology, termination, pair construction and validation scope must be identified together.

02 Project Inputs

Provide device models, bit rates, topology, bus and stub lengths, termination locations, cable and connector specifications, environment and test requirements.

03 Release Records

Release records the cable, pair and shield construction, connectors, pinout, topology, termination, lengths, process, traceability and agreed evidence.

04 Interface Review

Confirm the exact mating interface, pin map, dimensions and installation orientation.

05 Sample Acceptance

Agree the sample configuration and electrical, mechanical or environmental checks required by the project.

06 Change Control

Record changes to materials, drawings and process instructions before repeat manufacture.

Send the part number, drawing or sample to confirm the replacement configuration and sourcing plan.
