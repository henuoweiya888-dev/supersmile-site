Selection & Project Review

01 Application Boundary

An engine harness is a coordinated power, signal, communication and mechanical routing system, not a bundle copied from connector appearance. Engine family, controller, sensor and actuator set, firing order, transmission, emissions equipment, system voltage, thermal map, vibration, fluids, service route and vehicle interface determine the released design.

02 Project Inputs

Provide engine and vehicle or equipment identity; controller and calibration family; system voltage; connector and mating-part data; pin and circuit tables; sensor, injector, ignition, valve, motor and heater loads; CAN, LIN or other communication details; grounding and shielding intent; on-engine and body-side breakpoints; route photographs and datums; heat, vibration, oil, fuel, coolant, water and abrasion exposure; drawings or sample; labels;

03 Release Records

Approved requirements become controlled drawings, pin and circuit tables, BOMs, cut lists, splice maps, branch datums, shielding and protection instructions, labels and test definitions. First articles are checked for mating, reach, clip position, engine installation, service access, electrical identity and agreed powered functions before repeat production.

04 Interface Review

Confirm the exact mating interface, pin map, dimensions and installation orientation.

05 Sample Acceptance

Agree the sample configuration and electrical, mechanical or environmental checks required by the project.

06 Change Control

Record changes to materials, drawings and process instructions before repeat manufacture.

Send the part number, drawing or sample to confirm the replacement configuration and sourcing plan.
