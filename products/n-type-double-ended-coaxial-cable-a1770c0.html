CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Conductor, insulation and material choices导体、绝缘与选材要点

Use the n-type double-ended coaxial cable form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以N型双端同轴连接线的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Signal path信号路径

Define signal type, pair allocation, shield continuity and termination as well as the connector shape. Establish transmission performance from the actual cable, length and system validation.在接口外形之外确认信号类型、线对分配、屏蔽连续性和端接方法；传输能力应按实际线材、长度及整机验证结果确定。

02

Conductor and insulation selection导体与绝缘选配

Select conductors and insulation from circuit loads, diameter limits, bending and exposure. A material name does not replace ratings or validation results.从电路负载、外径限制、弯曲方式与环境条件选择导体和绝缘体系；材料名称不应直接代替额定参数或验证结果。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

RF interfaces and measurement boundaries射频接口与测量边界

Define ports, impedance, frequency range and measurement reference planes. Test records should correspond to the actual length and termination configuration. Tie the exact cable model to centre conductor, dielectric and supplier data.射频连接应明确端口、阻抗、频段和测量参考面，测试报告要对应实际长度与端接配置。选配时，准确型号须对应中心导体、介质和厂家数据表。

Antenna connections and environmental exposure天线端连接与环境暴露

Define equipment ports, retention and outdoor exposure at antenna connections. Confirm contact protection and cable-loss requirements separately. Specify impedance, frequency range and required attenuation data separately.天线端走线需说明设备端口、固定和室外暴露条件，接点防护与线缆损耗要求应分别确认。选配时，分别规定阻抗、频率范围及所需衰减数据。

Instrument inputs and coaxial measurement leads仪器输入与同轴测量引线

Match coaxial instrument leads to equipment ports, impedance and measurement frequency range. Define reference planes, length and shield termination. Select the cable and connector combination for the required loss and repeated-connection conditions.仪器同轴引线需匹配设备端口、阻抗与测量频段，说明参考面、线长及屏蔽端接。线缆与连接器的组合应对应项目所需的损耗和重复连接要求。

Coaxial construction and termination geometry同轴层结构与端接几何

Coordinate center conductor, dielectric, shield and connector dimensions. Set strip lengths and bend conditions from the exact cable type. Match diameter, minimum bending conditions and termination dimensions together.同轴端接需协调中心导体、介质、屏蔽层与连接器尺寸，剥线长度和弯曲条件应按准确线缆型号确定。选配时，外径、最小弯曲条件与端接加工尺寸成套匹配。

RF panels and cable ports射频面板与线缆端口

Match the connector mounting form and cable preparation at RF panel ends. Set mating and tightening-tool clearance from the actual assembly. Tie the exact cable model to centre conductor, dielectric and supplier data.射频面板端需要匹配连接器安装形式与线缆端接尺寸，插接和紧固工具空间以实际总装为准。选配时，准确型号须对应中心导体、介质和厂家数据表。

Oscilloscope and bench-instrument ports示波器与台架端口连接

At bench-instrument ports, define connector form, measurement reference plane and cable length. Provide retention and bending clearance for repeated connection. Specify impedance, frequency range and required attenuation data separately.仪器端口转接需明确连接器形式、测量参考面和线缆长度，端头固定与弯曲空间应便于重复连接。选配时，分别规定阻抗、频率范围及所需衰减数据。
