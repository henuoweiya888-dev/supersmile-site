A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

01

Branched end layout分支端头布局

Conductors separate toward different ends, creating individual connection points. Branch lengths and directions can follow the equipment modules.导线向不同端头分出，形成独立接入位置。各支路可围绕设备模块分别安排长度与方向。

02

Main-trunk routing form主干布线形式

The connections follow a main-trunk form, with the overall route, end positions and service allowance arranged around equipment datums.连接以主干布线形式组织，可围绕设备固定基准安排整体走向、端头位置和可拆卸余量。

03

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

Does a large diameter establish a high-voltage rating?粗直径能证明电缆高压额定吗？

Large diameter does not establish high-voltage rating. Record operating voltage, transient peaks and applicable specifications separately.粗直径不能证明高压额定。分别记录工作电压、瞬态峰值与适用规范。

Is test voltage the same as continuous operating voltage?测试电压就是持续工作电压吗？

Record test voltage separately from working voltage. Review conductor area for load, temperature rise and installation derating.测试电压与工作电压分开记录。导体截面按负载、温升和安装降额审核。

Can raw-cable inspection replace finished-assembly validation?原缆检验能替代端接后组件验证吗？
