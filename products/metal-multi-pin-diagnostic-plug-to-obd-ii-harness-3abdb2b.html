PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does every 16-pin OBD connector have the same wiring?所有 16 针 OBD 接口的线路都一样吗？

No. The standardized connector defines controlled functions and leaves some contacts for manufacturer discretion. Vehicle generation, market, protocol and target task still determine the released pin map.不一样。标准化接口定义受控功能，同时保留部分制造商自定义触点；车辆代际、市场、协议和目标任务仍决定最终针脚表。

Can you make a full 16-core male-to-female extension?可以制作全针 16 芯公对母延长线吗？

Yes, after confirming connector type, orientation, all pin-to-pin circuits, conductor sizes, length, exit direction and intended use. A fully populated extension can also pass manufacturer-specific circuits, so the application still matters.可以，但需确认连接器类型、方向、全部针对针回路、导体截面积、长度、出线与用途。全针延长线也会传递制造商自定义回路，因此仍需明确应用。

Will an OBD-to-DB9 or OBD-to-RJ45 cable work with any tool?OBD 转 DB9 或 OBD 转 RJ45 线能兼容任何设备吗？

No. DB9 and RJ45 describe connector families, not a universal diagnostic contact assignment. The exact tool model and approved device-side pinout are required.不能。DB9 与 RJ45 只是连接器家族，并不代表通用诊断针脚；必须提供准确设备型号和批准的设备端针脚。
