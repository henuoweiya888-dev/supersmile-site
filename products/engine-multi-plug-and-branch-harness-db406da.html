CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the engine multi-plug & branch harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以发动机多组大插头及支路线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Branched end layout分支端头布局

Conductors separate toward different ends, creating individual connection points. Branch lengths and directions can follow the equipment modules.导线向不同端头分出，形成独立接入位置。各支路可围绕设备模块分别安排长度与方向。

02

Rectangular interface form矩形接口外形

The rectangular interface form allows end position and service clearance to follow the mating face, latch and cable exit.接口采用矩形轮廓，可围绕插入面、卡扣与线束出口安排端头位置和拆装空间。

03

Multiple connector positions多端头连接位置

Multiple ends provide separate connection positions. Layout and spacing can follow the module arrangement.多个端头提供分别接入的位置，线束展开方向与各端头间距可围绕模块布局定义。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is an engine harness a universal product?发动机线束是通用产品吗？

No. It is a broad family of on-engine, injector, ignition, sensor, aftertreatment, transmission and body-interface assemblies. Engine, controller, emissions equipment, vehicle configuration and route determine the released version.不是。它是发动机本体、喷油、点火、传感、后处理、变速箱及车身接口组件组成的广泛家族，发动机、控制器、排放设备、车辆配置与走线共同决定放行版本。

Can a matching connector prove that two engine harnesses are compatible?连接器能插合就能证明两根发动机线束兼容吗？

No. Keying does not prove pin assignment, terminal rating, cylinder order, reference ground, shielding, communication, load current or controller compatibility. These must be verified against the actual system.不能。防错不能证明针脚定义、端子额定值、气缸顺序、参考接地、屏蔽、通信、负载电流或控制器兼容性，必须结合真实系统核对。

Can you build gasoline, diesel and industrial engine harnesses?可以制作汽油机、柴油机和工业发动机线束吗？

Yes, when the engine, controller, circuit information, interfaces, environment and validation requirements are defined. Each project is reviewed for its actual fuel system, ignition or injection, aftertreatment, voltage and equipment duty.可以，但需要明确发动机、控制器、回路信息、接口、环境和验证要求。每个项目都按真实燃油系统、点火或喷射、后处理、电压与设备任务审核。
