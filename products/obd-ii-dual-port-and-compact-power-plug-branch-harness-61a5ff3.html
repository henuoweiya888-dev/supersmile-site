CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the obd-ii dual-port & compact power plug branch harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以OBD-II双接口及小型电源插头分支线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Branched end layout分支端头布局

Conductors separate toward different ends, creating individual connection points. Branch lengths and directions can follow the equipment modules.导线向不同端头分出，形成独立接入位置。各支路可围绕设备模块分别安排长度与方向。

02

OBD-form connection endOBD形式接入端

The OBD-form end connects at an equipment or vehicle access position. Allow space for insertion, removal and cable turns.端头以OBD接口形式连接到设备或车辆接入位置，布局需保留插入、拔出及线缆转向空间。

03

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does one GPS harness fit every 12 V vehicle?一套 GPS 线束适配所有 12 V 车辆吗？

No. Supply voltage alone does not define connector pinout, ignition behavior, vehicle data access or installation geometry. Confirm the exact platform and tracker.不适配。供电电压不能单独确定针脚、点火逻辑、车辆数据访问或安装形状，需要确认具体车型与定位器。

Does a longer antenna cable improve reception?天线线缆越长，接收就越好吗？

Only if relocation improves the antenna environment enough to justify the added loss. Length itself does not amplify the signal; verify the receiver and antenna link budget.只有改善天线位置带来的收益足以抵消新增损耗时才可能有帮助。长度本身不会放大信号，需要审核接收器与天线的链路预算。

Can the harness convert CAN data into GPS reports?线束能把 CAN 数据自动转换成 GPS 报告吗？

A passive harness cannot. Data interpretation and reporting require compatible gateway electronics, software and authorized access.无源线束不能。数据解释与上报需要兼容的网关电子设备、软件和授权访问。
