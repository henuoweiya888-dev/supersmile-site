APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Internal device links设备内部连接

Define internal assemblies for device isolation, signals and fixing. Separate internal, external and patient-contact boundaries first.按设备隔离、信号和固定要求定义内部组件。选配时，先划分设备内部、外部和患者接触边界。

External sensor leads外部传感器引线

Define device, electrode/sensor ends and contact boundaries separately. Follow the device design for interfaces, isolation and power.设备端、电极或传感器端及接触边界分别明确。选配时，接口、隔离与供电服从医疗设备设计定义。

Serviceable medical-equipment modules可维护的医疗设备模块

For a serviceable module, confirm removal sequence, outlet positions and the user's specified cleaning method. A photograph does not establish reuse conditions. Record media, duration and cycles for cleaning, disinfection or sterilization.可维护模块的引线需确认拆装顺序、出口位置和使用方规定的清洁方式，避免由照片推定重复使用条件。选配时，清洁、消毒或灭菌需记录介质、时间和循环。

Device panels and removable modules设备面板与可拆模块

Define fixing, mating and cleaning boundaries for enclosure and operator use. Match material and biocompatibility evidence to the actual contact scope.固定、插拔及清洁边界结合机壳与操作者使用方式。选配时，材料与生物相容证据对应实际接触范围。

Diagnostic-instrument auxiliary links诊断仪器辅助连接

Separate auxiliary power and measurement paths to device isolation requirements. Separate internal, external and patient-contact boundaries first.辅助供电与测量回路按设备隔离要求分开处理。选配时，先划分设备内部、外部和患者接触边界。

Medical-equipment service interfaces医疗设备的检修接口

Match service leads to equipment interfaces, pins and accessible check points. Record length, mating direction and measurement access for maintenance. Follow the device design for interfaces, isolation and power.检修引线需匹配设备接口、针脚和可接近的检查位置；将线长、插拔方向和维修时的测量接入写入确认资料。选配时，接口、隔离与供电服从医疗设备设计定义。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does ‘medical cable’ mean the assembly is already approved for patient use?“医疗线缆”是否表示组件已经获批用于患者？

No. It describes an application category. Intended use, patient-contact boundary, device risk controls, materials, manufacturing records and verification must be defined by the legal manufacturer and applicable regulatory route.不是。它只是应用类别。预期用途、患者接触边界、器械风险控制、材料、制造记录与验证必须由法定制造商和适用法规路径确定。

Can you make ECG, SpO₂ or temperature lead sets from a sample?可以依据样品制作 ECG、SpO₂ 或体温导联吗？

A sample supports interface and dimensional study, but device model, pinout, signal requirements, electrode or sensor interface, materials, cleaning method and acceptance criteria still require written confirmation.样品可支持接口与尺寸研究，但设备型号、针脚、信号要求、电极或传感器接口、材料、清洁方法与验收准则仍需书面确认。

Which jacket material is best for reusable medical cables?可重复使用医疗线缆最适合哪种护套？

There is no universal answer. Flex, abrasion, skin proximity, disinfectant chemistry, temperature, sterilization or cleaning cycle, color and processing all matter. Candidate materials are screened, then evaluated in the finished construction.没有统一答案。弯折、耐磨、皮肤接近、消毒剂化学、温度、灭菌或清洁循环、颜色与加工都需考虑。候选材料先筛选，再在成品结构中评价。
