CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Conductor, insulation and material choices导体、绝缘与选材要点

Use the flat-sheath signal branch harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以扁平护套控制信号分支线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Branched end layout分支端头布局

Conductors separate toward different ends, creating individual connection points. Branch lengths and directions can follow the equipment modules.导线向不同端头分出，形成独立接入位置。各支路可围绕设备模块分别安排长度与方向。

02

Flat conductor arrangement扁平排列结构

The cable uses a flat arrangement, allowing compact routing around insertion direction, folds and end orientation.线缆采用扁平排列形态，可围绕插入方向、折转位置和端头朝向安排紧凑布线。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Terminal wiring inside control cabinets控制柜内的端子配线

Coordinate terminal positions, duct exits and service access within the cabinet. Wire labels should correspond to the circuit table and equipment ports. Specify the flame standard, test method and required classification.柜内连接需协调端子位置、线槽出口和可维护空间，线号应能对应回路表与设备端口。选配时，指定阻燃标准、试验方法及要求等级。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Define single-cable or bunched testing to avoid mixing their scopes.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，明确单根或成束试验，避免混用适用范围。

Routing and retention in confined spaces狭小空间的布线与固定

Define bends, retention points and assembly order in confined spaces. Do not compensate for an incorrect interface direction or length with forced folds. Match insulation, jacket and test specimen to the supplied construction.狭小空间布线需明确转弯、固定点和拆装顺序，不能用强行折线弥补接口方向或长度误差。选配时，绝缘、护套与试样规格对应实际供货结构。

Equipment power-distribution branches设备电源分配支路

Select power branches from actual loads, circuit protection and route length. Include termination, retention and service positions in the wiring definition. Reports must map to the exact model, construction and supply batch.供电分支以实际负载、回路保护和线长为选配依据，端头、固定和检修位置也需列入布线定义。选配时，报告需可对应准确型号、结构和供应批次。

Contact and fine-wire termination端子与细线端接

Contact selection depends on conductor construction, insulation diameter and tooling limits. Set wire preparation and crimp inspection for the actual contact part. Specify the flame standard, test method and required classification.端子选择同时取决于导体结构、绝缘外径和工具范围；备线尺寸与压接检查应落到实际接点型号。选配时，指定阻燃标准、试验方法及要求等级。

Internal machine connections and service设备机内连接与维护

Keep internal connections clear of working zones and moving parts. Allow appropriate cable length between supports, interfaces and service paths. Define single-cable or bunched testing to avoid mixing their scopes.设备机内连接要避开加工区与运动部件，并在固定点、接口和检修路径之间保留合理线缆余量。选配时，明确单根或成束试验，避免混用适用范围。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does flame retardance mean the circuit stays operational in fire?阻燃是否意味着火灾中保持通电？

Flame retardance is not fire-resistant circuit integrity. Specify the flame standard, test method and required classification.阻燃不等于耐火保持通电。指定阻燃标准、试验方法及要求等级。

Does flame retardance also mean halogen-free and low-smoke?阻燃是否同时代表无卤和低烟？

Flame retardance is not halogen-free or low-smoke performance. Define single-cable or bunched testing to avoid mixing their scopes.阻燃不等于无卤或低烟。明确单根或成束试验，避免混用适用范围。

Can classifications from different flame-test systems be treated as equivalent?不同阻燃体系的等级能直接等同吗？

Do not equate classifications from different test systems. Match insulation, jacket and test specimen to the supplied construction.不同试验体系等级不得直接等同。绝缘、护套与试样规格对应实际供货结构。
