CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the usb & 3.5mm panel-mount branch harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以USB与3.5mm接口 · 面板安装分支线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Branched end layout分支端头布局

Conductors separate toward different ends, creating individual connection points. Branch lengths and directions can follow the equipment modules.导线向不同端头分出，形成独立接入位置。各支路可围绕设备模块分别安排长度与方向。

02

Panel-mounted connection面板安装连接

The interface is arranged at a panel or feedthrough, with front/rear clearance, fixing positions and cable exit defined around the enclosure.接口与面板或穿壳位置配合，前后端连接空间、固定孔位和出线方向可围绕机壳定义。

03

USB connector endUSB连接端

The USB-form end provides a device connection boundary, with end orientation and lead routing arranged for the installation space.USB形式的端头可作为设备接入边界，端头朝向与引线走向按安装空间布置。

04

Signal path信号路径

Define signal type, pair allocation, shield continuity and termination as well as the connector shape. Establish transmission performance from the actual cable, length and system validation.在接口外形之外确认信号类型、线对分配、屏蔽连续性和端接方法；传输能力应按实际线材、长度及整机验证结果确定。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Equipment panels and circular interfaces设备面板与圆形接口

Start with the cutout, panel thickness, mounting direction and rear clearance. Coordinate contact arrangement and cable exit with the complete assembly view. Provide panel thickness, tolerance, cutout and nearby installation envelope.面板连接以开孔、板厚、装配方向和后方净空为起点；接点排列与电缆出口还需要对应完整总装视图。选配时，提供面板厚度、公差、开孔及邻近安装包络。

Terminal wiring inside control cabinets控制柜内的端子配线

Coordinate terminal positions, duct exits and service access within the cabinet. Wire labels should correspond to the circuit table and equipment ports. Specify mounting direction, threads and fastener construction.柜内连接需协调端子位置、线槽出口和可维护空间，线号应能对应回路表与设备端口。选配时，指定前后安装方向、螺纹和固定件结构。

Enclosure entries and cable retention机箱穿线与入口固定

Match cable entries to actual diameter, panel thickness and fixing method. Keep jacket retention independent of the internal termination. Review sealing and grounding locations against gasket material and compression.线缆入口需匹配实际外径、板厚和固定方式；护套夹持与内部端接之间应保留独立的支撑路径。选配时，按垫圈材料与压缩量审查密封及接地位置。

Equipment junction boxes and entry positions设备接线盒与入口位置

Define entry orientation, water-trap risks and service access at junction boxes. Record the seal-to-jacket combination separately. Include exit angle, adapter length and service access in dimensional approval.接线盒入口需要说明安装朝向、积水风险和检修空间；密封件与电缆护套的匹配关系单独记录。选配时，出线角度、转接长度及拆装空间纳入尺寸确认。

Detachable circular connections可拆卸圆形连接

Check gender, keying, coupling and end-face views together. Equal contact counts do not replace confirmation of the exact series and part numbers. Provide panel thickness, tolerance, cutout and nearby installation envelope.圆形接口需要同时核对公母、键位、锁紧方式和端视图，针数相同并不能替代完整系列与料号确认。选配时，提供面板厚度、公差、开孔及邻近安装包络。

External interfaces on portable equipment便携设备的外部接口

Consider handling, plug grip and cable bends together. Align the exit direction with the device position during use. Specify mounting direction, threads and fastener construction.便携设备连接要同时考虑操作手势、插头握持和尾部弯曲空间，线缆出口方向应与实际使用姿态协调。选配时，指定前后安装方向、螺纹和固定件结构。
