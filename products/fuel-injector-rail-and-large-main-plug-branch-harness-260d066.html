Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Fuel-pump power燃油泵供电

Select conductors/protection from drive load and connection location. Distinguish fuel pumps, injectors and sensors by exact model.驱动负载与连接位置确定导体和保护。选配时，燃油泵、喷油器与传感器按准确型号区分。

Injector branches喷油器分支

Define circuits from cylinder order and controller drive method. Wire drive, feedback, power and reference ground separately.缸序与控制器驱动方式共同定义回路。选配时，驱动、反馈、供电与参考地分开接线。

Pressure measurement and signal termination压力检测与信号端接

For pressure-measurement connections, compare equipment interfaces, signal types and termination definitions before arranging measurement access and service space. Define exposure locations as splash, vapor or immersion.压力检测连接需核对设备接口、信号形式与端接定义，再安排测量接入和检修空间。选配时，介质接触位置明确为飞溅、蒸气或浸泡。

Internal fuel-tank module wiring油箱模组内部接线

Define supply, level and other signal circuits separately from the equipment pin table, with materials and assembly routes confirmed. Match sealing, temperature and safety requirements to the installation zone.油箱模组内的供电、液位或其他信号回路需按设备针脚表分别定义，确认材料与装配路径。选配时，密封、温度与安全要求对应实际安装区。

Common-rail control branches共轨控制分支

Align controller revision, cylinder order and drive definitions. Distinguish fuel pumps, injectors and sensors by exact model.控制器版本、缸序和驱动定义应相互对应。选配时，燃油泵、喷油器与传感器按准确型号区分。

Service-end interfaces for fuel components燃油部件的维修端接口

For a service-end interface, compare the original component contacts, latch, wires and environment. Similar appearance does not establish a match. Wire drive, feedback, power and reference ground separately.维修端接口需核对原部件接点、锁止、导线及环境条件，外形相近不能代替匹配确认。选配时，驱动、反馈、供电与参考地分开接线。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is a fuel-system harness the same as a fuel line?燃油系统线束等同于燃油管吗？

No. The harness carries electrical power and signals to pumps, injectors, valves and sensors. Hoses, rails and pipes carry fuel and require separate fluid-system engineering and verification.不等同。线束向泵、喷油器、阀和传感器输送电源与信号；软管、油轨和管路输送燃油，需另行开展流体系统设计与验证。

Can a connector that fits an injector be used directly?喷油器插头能插上就可以直接用吗？

No. Confirm the injector and controller part numbers, cavity view, driver strategy, circuit assignment, terminal system and installed environment. A shared shell shape does not prove electrical compatibility.不能。还需确认喷油器与控制器料号、孔位视图、驱动策略、回路分配、端子体系和安装环境。外壳形状相同不证明电气兼容。

Does a sealed connector make the whole harness fuel-resistant?密封接头能让整根线束耐燃油吗？

No. Fuel exposure must be defined by liquid, vapor or mist, duration and temperature. Wire insulation, seals, protection and the mated connector all need suitable material evidence and an agreed test if a resistance claim is required.不能。应明确是液体、蒸气还是油雾，以及接触时长和温度。若需耐受声明，线材绝缘、密封、防护及对插连接器均需对应材料证据和约定试验。
