CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the high-current fuse holder ring-terminal harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以大电流保险丝座环形端子线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Ring-terminal end环形端子端头

A ring-terminal end provides a mounting-hole connection. Lead direction, support and insulation coverage can follow the stud position.末端采用带安装孔的环形端子形式，可围绕螺柱位置安排接线方向、端头支撑与绝缘覆盖。

02

Fuse-holder connection section保险座连接部位

The assembly includes a fuse-holder connection section, separating supply, protected-side routing and service access in the layout.组件包含保险座相关连接部位，可将供电、受保护端和维护接近位置在布局中区分。

03

Terminal connection section端子接入部位

Leads use terminal-form ends at the connection points. Orientation, fixing and insulation coverage can follow the equipment assembly.引线以端子形式接入相应连接位置，可按设备装配方式安排端头方向、固定和绝缘覆盖。

04

Current path and end termination载流与端部连接

Select conductors and terminations from continuous and peak loads, installed length and temperature rise. Confirm lug bore, contact surface, insulation coverage and fixing as a set.依据持续与峰值负载、安装长度和温升要求选择导体与端接；端子孔径、接触面、绝缘覆盖及固定方式需要成套确认。

05

Protection and control circuits保护与控制回路

Define loads, control logic, protection-device positions and service access before conductor and branch selection. Housing shape alone does not establish ratings or control functions.先核对负载、控制逻辑、保护器件位置与维修可达性，再安排线径和分支。不能仅凭外壳形状判断保护额定或控制功能。
