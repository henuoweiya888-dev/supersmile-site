PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Can an RJ45-looking plug guarantee Gigabit Ethernet?外形像 RJ45 的插头就能保证千兆以太网吗？

No. The complete cable and connector construction, active pairs, pin map, length and measured link performance matter. Agree the required data rate and test limit before production.不能。完整线材与连接器结构、有效线对、针脚映射、长度和实测链路性能都有关。生产前应约定目标速率与测试限值。

Can M12 D-coded and X-coded connectors be swapped?M12 D 编码与 X 编码能互换吗？

No. Their mating geometry and intended pair arrangements differ. Select the exact device-side coding and release the mapping to the modular side.不能。两者对插几何和预期线对布局不同，应确认设备端准确编码，并放行到模块化接口一侧的映射。

Does a shielded cable need a shielded plug?用了屏蔽线就一定需要屏蔽插头吗？

That depends on the system bonding and EMC design. If a continuous screened path is specified, the plug, shell and termination must support it; a shielded jacket alone cannot establish continuity.取决于系统接地与电磁兼容方案。若要求连续屏蔽路径，插头、外壳和端接必须共同支持；只有屏蔽线材不能形成完整路径。
