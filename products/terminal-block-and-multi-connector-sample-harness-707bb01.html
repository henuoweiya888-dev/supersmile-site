CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the terminal block & multi-connector sample harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以端子排与多接头样品线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Terminal-block connection form端子排接入形式

Leads gather at terminal-block or wiring-board connection points, allowing circuits to be organized by terminal number.多根引线汇集到端子排或接线板连接位置，便于按端子编号组织不同回路。

02

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

03

Terminal connection section端子接入部位

Leads use terminal-form ends at the connection points. Orientation, fixing and insulation coverage can follow the equipment assembly.引线以端子形式接入相应连接位置，可按设备装配方式安排端头方向、固定和绝缘覆盖。

04

Multiple connector positions多端头连接位置

Multiple ends provide separate connection positions. Layout and spacing can follow the module arrangement.多个端头提供分别接入的位置，线束展开方向与各端头间距可围绕模块布局定义。

05

Terminal-block connection端子排接入

Check terminal numbering, conductor range, strip length and tightening method. Match labels to the circuit drawing for installation and fault finding.核对端子编号、线径范围、剥线长度及紧固方法，并让标签与电路图保持对应，方便现场安装和故障排查。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

How much information is needed to start a prototype?开始打样需要多少资料？

A drawing is helpful but not mandatory. A sample, pin table, connector references, photos and equipment layout can start review when the intended test purpose and open points are stated.图纸最有帮助但不是必须。只要说明测试目的与待确认项，样品、针脚表、接口资料、照片和设备布局也可启动审核。

Can temporary materials be used?可以使用临时物料吗？

They may be used when the customer accepts the substitution and its effect on fit, function or testing is recorded. Temporary parts are not silently converted into the production BOM.客户接受且记录其对装配、功能或测试的影响后可以使用，但临时物料不会未经确认直接进入量产BOM。

Does a working sample mean the design is ready for mass production?样品能工作就代表可以量产吗？

No. A functional sample may still use manual methods, provisional dimensions or unqualified materials. Production release requires a controlled design, repeatable process and agreed inspection plan.不代表。功能样品可能仍使用手工方法、临时尺寸或未完成验证的物料，量产放行需要受控设计、可重复工艺与约定检验计划。
