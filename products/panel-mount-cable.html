Configuration & Project Review

01 Panel geometry

Provide panel thickness, cutout, mounting side and the available interior space.

02 Interface and retention

Specify connector part number, keying, thread or fastener and required mating access.

03 Cable transition

Choose wire leads, an internal cable or a board interface according to the equipment layout.

04 Edge and strain protection

Match grommets, edge protection and cord grips to the panel and actual cable diameter.

05 Seal and shield boundary

Define sealing faces, gasket compression and any chassis-to-shield bond.

06 Installed verification

Verify fit, retention and electrical function in a representative panel fixture.

Send the part number, drawing or sample to confirm the replacement configuration and sourcing plan.
