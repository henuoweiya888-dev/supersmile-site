CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the latching rectangular connector braided sleeve y-branch harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以矩形锁扣接头编织套Y形线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Y-shaped breakoutY形分出结构

The breakout follows a Y-shaped route toward separate ends. Bend clearance and fixing can be arranged around the split.分出位置形成Y形走向，可把一处接入分向不同端头。安装时围绕分出点安排弯曲和固定。

02

Rectangular interface form矩形接口外形

The rectangular interface form allows end position and service clearance to follow the mating face, latch and cable exit.接口采用矩形轮廓，可围绕插入面、卡扣与线束出口安排端头位置和拆装空间。

03

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

04

Braided outer form编织形态外层

The outer covering has a braided form. Coverage segments and finishing can follow breakouts, routing passages and fixing positions.外层呈编织形态，可按分支出口、穿线位置和固定点定义覆盖段与收尾。

05

Braided outer protection编织外层

A braid can organize the bundle and protect its surface. Define its material, coverage and end treatment around the routing path; distinguish insulating braid from metallic shielding.编织层可用于束线与表面防磨；材料、覆盖长度和端部收口需结合安装路径确定。绝缘编织与金属屏蔽编织应分别定义。

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

01

Wiring in damp equipment潮湿设备配线

Map mating interfaces, cable entries and joints as a complete sealing boundary. Protection targets must identify the full assembly boundary and exact test conditions.把插合口、线缆入口和接头位置绘成完整密封边界。选配时，目标防护要求应包含完整组件边界与具体试验条件。

Outdoor equipment connections室外设备连接

Define validation conditions for rain, pooling water, orientation and temperature changes. Match wire seals, potting, gaskets and housings for material compatibility.按雨水、积水、姿态与温度变化确定需要验证的条件。选配时，线封、灌封、垫圈与壳体按材料相容性配套。

03

Serviceable sealed interfaces维修可拆接口

Define protection while unmated and the resealing requirements after service. Record actual cable diameter, unused cavities and potential capillary paths.说明未插合时如何防护，以及检修后的重新密封要求。选配时，实际线缆外径、空孔和潜在毛细通道均须记录。

04

Equipment cable entries设备线缆入口

Connections in cleaning zones需要清洁的连接区
