CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the 64-pin idc multi-connector ribbon cable assembly form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以64针IDC接口 · 多接头排线组件的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

02

Flat conductor arrangement扁平排列结构

The cable uses a flat arrangement, allowing compact routing around insertion direction, folds and end orientation.线缆采用扁平排列形态，可围绕插入方向、折转位置和端头朝向安排紧凑布线。

03

Multiple connector positions多端头连接位置

Multiple ends provide separate connection positions. Layout and spacing can follow the module arrangement.多个端头提供分别接入的位置，线束展开方向与各端头间距可围绕模块布局定义。

04

Grouped cable-set form组合线组形式

Several connection pieces are organized as a cable set, allowing each section to be assigned and identified by assembly sequence and module.多段连接件以线组形式组织，可按装配顺序和设备模块划分各段用途与标识。

Application directions应用方向

Equipment control-board connections设备控制板连接

Cabinet data ports and cable routes机柜数据端口与布线路径

Keep port mapping clear while coordinating cable bends, termination support and module access. Validate protocol performance separately for the project. Define contact arrangement, pitch and locking from interface drawings.数据端连接需保持端口映射清晰，并协调线缆弯曲、端接支撑和模块检修路径；协议性能需另按项目验证。选配时，针位排列、间距和锁止按接口图纸确定。

Equipment panels and circular interfaces设备面板与圆形接口

Include contact plating, wire and processing requirements in the same BOM.面板连接以开孔、板厚、装配方向和后方净空为起点；接点排列与电缆出口还需要对应完整总装视图。选配时，端子镀层、导线与加工规范纳入同一BOM。
