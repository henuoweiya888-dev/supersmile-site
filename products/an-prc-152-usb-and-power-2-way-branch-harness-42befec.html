PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does ‘military-spec’ mean the complete assembly is qualified?“军规”是否表示整套组件已经获得鉴定？

No. Qualification applies to the exact product, specification, class, source and scope supported by current evidence. A project assembly must separately satisfy its drawing, contract, workmanship, inspection and platform approval requirements.不是。鉴定只适用于当前证据覆盖的准确产品、规范、等级、来源与范围。项目组件仍需单独满足图纸、合同、工艺、检验和平台批准要求。

Can you build with MIL-DTL-38999 connectors?可以使用 MIL-DTL-38999 连接器制作吗？

Projects using this family can be reviewed when the exact series, class, slash-sheet PIN, insert, contact, keying, backshell, mating half and approved-source requirement are supplied. The connector name alone is not enough.在提供准确系列、等级、分规范型号、孔位、触点、键位、尾罩、对插件与批准来源要求后，可进行项目审核；只有连接器名称还不够。

Can an existing sample be reverse engineered?可以依据现有样品进行开发吗？

A sample supports dimensions and interface study, but it may not reveal wire designation, derating, shield topology, hidden splices, sealing materials, approved sources or qualification history. Released engineering data and authority are still required.样品有助于尺寸和接口研究，但可能无法揭示线材牌号、降额、屏蔽拓扑、隐藏接续、密封材料、批准来源或鉴定历史，仍需放行工程资料与授权。
