PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is a vehicle lighting harness a universal product?汽车灯线束是通用产品吗？

No. It is a broad family of lamp pigtails, front and rear sub-harnesses, protected power looms, control leads and complete kits. Vehicle, market, lamp technology, pinout, load, controller and route determine the released version.不是。它是灯具尾线、前后分线束、受保护电源线束、控制引线与完整套件组成的广泛家族，车辆、市场、灯具技术、针脚、负载、控制器与走线共同决定放行版本。

Can a matching lamp connector prove compatibility?灯具连接器能插合就能证明兼容吗？

No. Connector keying does not prove pin assignment, terminal rating, ground strategy, lamp load, PWM, diagnostics or LIN compatibility. These must be verified against the actual lamp and vehicle system.不能。连接器防错不能证明针脚定义、端子额定值、接地策略、灯具负载、PWM、诊断或 LIN 兼容性，必须结合真实灯具与车辆系统核对。

Can you build harnesses for halogen, HID and LED lamps?可以制作卤素、HID 与 LED 灯线束吗？

Yes, when lamp, ballast or driver data and the vehicle control architecture are defined. Their current, heat, switching and diagnostic behaviour differ, so the same wire and relay assumptions do not apply to every technology.可以，但需要明确灯具、镇流器或驱动器资料及车辆控制架构。不同技术的电流、热、开关和诊断行为不同，不能全部套用同一线径与继电器假设。
