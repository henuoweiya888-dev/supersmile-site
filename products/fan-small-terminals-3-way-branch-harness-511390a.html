APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Equipment control-board connections设备控制板连接

Document pin-row views, mating direction and nearby wire support. Check access to the latch and contacts after assembly. Select the exact material grade and check jacket and component compatibility.板端连接要给出针排视图、插合方向和近端线缆支撑；检查装配后是否仍能操作锁扣并查看接点。选配时，选择准确材料牌号并核对护套及器件相容性。

Branch joining and joint protection分支汇接与接点保护

Define each conductor, stacking direction and joint geometry before selecting tooling, insulation protection and inspection methods. Define encapsulation boundaries, wall thickness, cavities and protected regions on the drawing.分支汇接需定义每根导体、叠放方向和接头几何，再选择工装、绝缘保护与检验方法。选配时，图纸定义包覆边界、壁厚、腔体和需保护区域。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Set temperature, pressure and fill paths within component limits.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，按器件限制确定温度、压力及灌注路径。

Cable exits and nearby support电缆出口与近端支撑

Keep bending loads away from contacts through appropriate rear support. Review exit angle, retention distance and boot dimensions with the enclosure. Agree electrical, bonding, void and environmental checks separately.端头后方的支撑要避免把弯折载荷直接传到接点，出线角度、固定距离和尾套尺寸需与机壳一起评审。选配时，分别约定电气、粘接、空洞与环境检查方法。

Leads for small electronic modules小型电子模块的引线

Define contact mapping, fine-wire processing limits and enclosure clearance for compact modules. Locate support close to the cable ends. Select the exact material grade and check jacket and component compatibility.小型模块的接口定义需要覆盖针位、细线加工范围和壳内空间，靠近端头的支撑位置尤其重要。选配时，选择准确材料牌号并核对护套及器件相容性。

Contact and fine-wire termination端子与细线端接

Contact selection depends on conductor construction, insulation diameter and tooling limits. Set wire preparation and crimp inspection for the actual contact part. Define encapsulation boundaries, wall thickness, cavities and protected regions on the drawing.端子选择同时取决于导体结构、绝缘外径和工具范围；备线尺寸与压接检查应落到实际接点型号。选配时，图纸定义包覆边界、壁厚、腔体和需保护区域。
