APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Define the halogen-free standard, measurement method and limits.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，明确无卤标准、测定方法及要求限值。

Terminal wiring inside control cabinets控制柜内的端子配线

Coordinate terminal positions, duct exits and service access within the cabinet. Wire labels should correspond to the circuit table and equipment ports. Specify separate tests where low smoke or gas corrosivity matters.柜内连接需协调端子位置、线槽出口和可维护空间，线号应能对应回路表与设备端口。选配时，需要低烟或气体腐蚀性时单独规定试验。

Routing and retention in confined spaces狭小空间的布线与固定

Define bends, retention points and assembly order in confined spaces. Do not compensate for an incorrect interface direction or length with forced folds. Check flame requirements separately from halogen-free material requirements.狭小空间布线需明确转弯、固定点和拆装顺序，不能用强行折线弥补接口方向或长度误差。选配时，阻燃要求与无卤材料要求分别核对。

Equipment power-distribution branches设备电源分配支路

Select power branches from actual loads, circuit protection and route length. Include termination, retention and service positions in the wiring definition. Map reports to the exact model and supply batch.供电分支以实际负载、回路保护和线长为选配依据，端头、固定和检修位置也需列入布线定义。选配时，报告、具体型号及供应批次建立对应关系。

Screw terminals and removable wire ends螺钉端子与可拆接线端

For screw-clamped connections, check conductor range, strip length and end preparation. Use the exact terminal documentation for tightening requirements. Define the halogen-free standard, measurement method and limits.螺钉夹持连接需核对导体范围、剥线长度和端头形式，紧固要求应来自准确端子文件。选配时，明确无卤标准、测定方法及要求限值。

Internal machine connections and service设备机内连接与维护

Keep internal connections clear of working zones and moving parts. Allow appropriate cable length between supports, interfaces and service paths. Specify separate tests where low smoke or gas corrosivity matters.设备机内连接要避开加工区与运动部件，并在固定点、接口和检修路径之间保留合理线缆余量。选配时，需要低烟或气体腐蚀性时单独规定试验。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does halogen-free automatically mean low-smoke and flame-retardant?无卤是否自动等于低烟和阻燃？

Halogen-free does not automatically mean low smoke or flame retardance. Define the halogen-free standard, measurement method and limits.无卤不自动意味着低烟或阻燃。明确无卤标准、测定方法及要求限值。

What evidence is needed for an LSZH designation?LSZH名称需要哪些依据？

An LSZH label needs evidence for each relevant test. Specify separate tests where low smoke or gas corrosivity matters.LSZH名称需分别对应各项试验依据。需要低烟或气体腐蚀性时单独规定试验。

Can colour or jacket feel identify halogen-free material?颜色和护套手感能识别无卤吗？

Color or jacket texture cannot identify halogen content. Check flame requirements separately from halogen-free material requirements.颜色或护套质感不能识别无卤。阻燃要求与无卤材料要求分别核对。
