Microswitch Harness微动开关连接线束

Microswitch Harness can be configured around equipment interfaces and routing. Contact logic must be read from the exact switch data sheet and approved circuit. APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Control-panel switches控制面板开关

Wire buttons, contacts and indicators from the panel circuit. Define contacts, action and wiring from the switch model.按钮、触点和指示灯按实际面板电路分开接线。选配时，按开关型号定义触点、动作和接线关系。

Equipment limits and controls设备限位与操作

Match NO/NC and latching/momentary action to equipment logic. Review inrush and switching for motor, lamp or inductive loads.常开常闭、保持或复位动作与设备逻辑匹配。选配时，电机、灯具或感性负载分别核对浪涌与开断。

Auxiliary load switching辅助负载开关

Lamp or motor inrush affects terminations and protection. Identify indicators, supplies and common terminals separately.灯具或电机负载的浪涌影响端接和保护。选配时，指示灯、电源与公共端单独标明。

Remote-switch contacts and service远端开关接点与检修

For remote controls, confirm contact construction, normally open or closed states and cable length before arranging accessible service connections. Match cutout, leads and protection to the assembly.远端控制先确认开关接点形式、常开常闭状态及线缆长度，再安排检修时可接近的连接位置。选配时，面板开孔、引线和防护配合实际装配。

Status-indication modules状态指示模块

Identify indicator power, common terminals and control contacts separately. Define contacts, action and wiring from the switch model.指示灯供电、电路公共端和操作触点分别标识。选配时，按开关型号定义触点、动作和接线关系。

Moving structures and limit interfaces移动结构与限位接口

Define limit connections from mechanical movement, contact states and safety-design requirements. The harness follows the approved circuit and interfaces. Review inrush and switching for motor, lamp or inductive loads.限位连接需结合机械动作、接点状态和安全设计要求定义，线束只按获批电路与接口制作。选配时，电机、灯具或感性负载分别核对浪涌与开断。
