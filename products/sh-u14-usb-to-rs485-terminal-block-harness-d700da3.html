CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the sh-u14 usb to rs485 terminal-block harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以SH-U14 USB转RS485端子排线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Terminal-block connection form端子排接入形式

Leads gather at terminal-block or wiring-board connection points, allowing circuits to be organized by terminal number.多根引线汇集到端子排或接线板连接位置，便于按端子编号组织不同回路。

02

USB connector endUSB连接端

The USB-form end provides a device connection boundary, with end orientation and lead routing arranged for the installation space.USB形式的端头可作为设备接入边界，端头朝向与引线走向按安装空间布置。

03

End transition layout端头转接与组合

The assembly organizes a transition or combined connection, with end directions, transition sections and fixing arranged around the equipment positions.组件以转接或组合形式组织连接，可围绕两端设备位置安排端头方向、过渡段与固定。

04

Terminal connection section端子接入部位

Leads use terminal-form ends at the connection points. Orientation, fixing and insulation coverage can follow the equipment assembly.引线以端子形式接入相应连接位置，可按设备装配方式安排端头方向、固定和绝缘覆盖。

05

Signal path信号路径

Define signal type, pair allocation, shield continuity and termination as well as the connector shape. Establish transmission performance from the actual cable, length and system validation.在接口外形之外确认信号类型、线对分配、屏蔽连续性和端接方法；传输能力应按实际线材、长度及整机验证结果确定。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does every Type-C cable support high-speed data and high power?所有 Type-C 电缆都支持高速数据和大功率吗？

No. Type-C identifies the connector system. Data lanes, power duty, Configuration Channel wiring, electronic marking, directionality and equipment capability determine the usable functions.不能。Type-C 只识别接口系统，实际功能取决于数据通道、供电负载、Configuration Channel 接线、电子标记、方向性与双方设备能力。

Does passing continuity prove a high-speed USB cable is good?导通合格能证明高速 USB 电缆性能吗？

No. Continuity proves point-to-point connection. High-speed performance also depends on impedance, loss, crosstalk, termination, length, bending and the complete channel.不能。导通只证明点对点连接，高速性能还取决于阻抗、损耗、串扰、端接、长度、弯曲与完整链路。

Can you build a charging-only cable?可以制作仅供电 USB 线吗？

Yes, when the required identification, CC or other circuits, current, voltage drop, temperature rise and misuse controls are explicitly defined and approved.可以，但必须明确并批准所需识别、CC 或其他回路、电流、压降、温升以及防误用措施。
