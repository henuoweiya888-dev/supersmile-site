CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

The layout combines grouped routing with individual connection points. Braided sections gather the longer cable runs, while connector ends and leads separate near the modules. The installation drawing defines the trunk route, branch measurement datums and orientation of each termination.这套结构的重点是把集中布线与独立接入结合起来。长段线缆由编织套收束，接近模块处再分出插接端和引线；安装图需要同时说明主干路径、分支测量基准和每个端头的方向。

01

Segmented braided sleeves分段编织护套

The sleeves cover separate sections. Ties finish their exits; coverage and end finishing can follow the equipment’s abrasion points, fixing positions and service clearances.米色编织套覆盖不同线束段，出口处采用束扎收尾。定制时根据摩擦位置、固定点和维修空间，确定覆盖长度与收口方式。

02

White connector groups白色多组插接端

White housings sit at the trunk and branch ends for connections to separate modules. Selection follows the actual mating parts, defining the series, contact arrangement, orientation and terminals together.多个白色壳体分布在主干与分支端部，形成不同模块的插接位置。接口需按实际对插端确认系列、针位、方向及端子配套。

03

Breakouts and twisted conductors分支与可见绞线

Colored leads and some twisted conductor sections are visible outside the sleeves. Each branch can follow its own route. Wire colors and twisting are defined with the circuit requirements; the pin table establishes their functions.护套外可见多色引线及部分绞合线段，便于分别规划各支路走向。导线颜色与绞合形式结合电路要求定义，功能以针脚表为准。

04

Separate ring-terminal lead独立环形端子

A ring terminal ends one separate lead. For this screw-fastened connection, stud-hole size, terminal material, crimp construction and circuit purpose are selected to suit the connection point.一条独立引线以环形端子结束，为螺钉式连接提供一种端接形式。孔径、端子材质、压接结构和电路用途按对应安装点选配。

05

Leads prepared for further connection保留接线的引线端

Some leads remain unhoused. Their preparation follows the equipment’s assembly method, with strip length, end treatment, connection location and insulation protection recorded in the assembly drawing.部分引线未装插接壳体，可按设备装配方式定义后续端接。剥皮长度、端头处理、连接位置与绝缘保护应在组件图中写明。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Robotics and repeated-motion equipment机器人与重复运动设备

Flexible protective braid organizes cables around moving arms and tooling while branch, bend and attachment points remain matched to the motion path. Separate bundle management, abrasion, heat protection and conductive shielding goals.柔性防护编织整理运动臂与工具周围电缆，分支、弯曲和固定点仍需匹配运动路径。选配时，先分清束线、耐磨、热防护与导电屏蔽目标。

Machine tools and industrial equipment机床与工业设备

Abrasion, chips, oil and service contact drive braid, conduit and transition choices around cabinets, spindles, sensors and auxiliary systems. Set segmented coverage around abrasion zones, breakouts and service clearance.机柜、主轴、传感器与辅助系统周围的磨损、切屑、油污和检修接触决定编织、波纹管与过渡结构。选配时，分段覆盖围绕摩擦区、分支出口和维修空间布置。

Heating and high-temperature zones加热与高温区域

Glass fibre, aramid or specified high-temperature braid protects cable surfaces near heaters, ovens and thermal equipment when the full temperature profile is known. Use suitable heat shrink, ties or mechanical retention at sleeve ends.在掌握完整温度曲线后，玻纤、芳纶或指定耐高温编织可用于加热器、炉体与热设备附近的线缆表面防护。选配时，端口收尾可结合热缩、束扎或适用的机械固定。

Instrumentation and noise-sensitive systems仪器与噪声敏感系统

Conductive braid and controlled terminations can support shielding for measurement, sensor and communication circuits where the grounding scheme is defined. Use bending and temperature conditions to select braid and support.在接地方案明确时，导电编织与受控端接可支持测量、传感与通信回路的屏蔽。选配时，弯曲与温度条件用于选择编织材料和支撑结构。

Outdoor, marine and process equipment户外、船舶与工艺设备

Moisture, salt, chemicals and sunlight require braid material and underlying cable jackets that are compatible with the complete exposure, not just an attractive outer weave. Separate bundle management, abrasion, heat protection and conductive shielding goals.湿气、盐分、化学品与日照要求编织材料和内部电缆护套共同适配完整环境，而不仅是外层织物看起来耐用。选配时，先分清束线、耐磨、热防护与导电屏蔽目标。

Commercial equipment and visible harnesses商用设备与可见线束

Neat abrasion-resistant braid supports organized, serviceable visible cable runs in kiosks, appliances, packaging equipment and production fixtures. Set segmented coverage around abrasion zones, breakouts and service clearance.整洁耐磨编织适用于售卖设备、电器、包装设备与生产工装中可见且需要检修的布线路径。选配时，分段覆盖围绕摩擦区、分支出口和维修空间布置。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does the braid act as an electromagnetic shield?编织套可以当作电磁屏蔽吗？

The braided appearance does not establish shielding. An EMI requirement needs conductive materials, coverage and drain or shell termination defined, followed by the relevant system validation.编织外观本身不能确定屏蔽功能。如果项目需要EMI屏蔽，需要明确导电材料、覆盖结构、排流或外壳端接方式，并结合系统验证。

Can the connectors, branch count and lengths change?可以更改白色插头、分支数量和线长吗？

They can be redefined for the equipment. Provide the mating parts, pin mapping and installation dimensions so terminal/wire matching, breakout positions and assembly space can be reviewed together.可以按设备需求重新定义。请提供对插件、针脚关系和安装尺寸；调整后同时复核端子与线径匹配、分支位置和装配空间。

How can customization start from an existing sample?只有原样品，怎样开始定制？

Start by recording the interfaces, visible routing, dimensions and terminations, then confirm the internal circuits, materials and operating conditions. The approved drawing and sample establish the production configuration.可先记录接口、可见布线、尺寸和端头结构，再确认内部电路、材料与设备工况。确认图纸与样品后，确定生产配置。

Planning a similar multi-branch connection?需要类似的多分支连接结构？

Send the equipment layout, interface photos or details of an existing sample, together with the branch count and required quantity. We will review the interfaces, circuits and installation positions for a custom configuration.发送设备布局、接口照片或现有样品说明，并告诉我们分支数量与数量需求。我们将围绕接口、电路和安装位置评估定制方案。
