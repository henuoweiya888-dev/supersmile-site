CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the db25 plug to db25 socket rainbow ribbon cable form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以DB25插头转DB25插座彩色排线的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

D-Sub interface endD-Sub接口端

The D-Sub-form end allows equipment connection to be arranged around the mating face, fixing screws and rear cable route.端头采用D-Sub形式，可围绕插合面、固定螺钉和后部线缆走向安排设备连接。

02

End transition layout端头转接与组合

The assembly organizes a transition or combined connection, with end directions, transition sections and fixing arranged around the equipment positions.组件以转接或组合形式组织连接，可围绕两端设备位置安排端头方向、过渡段与固定。

03

Flat conductor arrangement扁平排列结构

The cable uses a flat arrangement, allowing compact routing around insertion direction, folds and end orientation.线缆采用扁平排列形态，可围绕插入方向、折转位置和端头朝向安排紧凑布线。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Equipment control-board connections设备控制板连接

Document pin-row views, mating direction and nearby wire support. Check access to the latch and contacts after assembly. Confirm the IDC family, connector pitch and cavity views together.板端连接要给出针排视图、插合方向和近端线缆支撑；检查装配后是否仍能操作锁扣并查看接点。选配时，IDC系列、连接器节距与针位视图成套确认。

Inter-board ribbon cables and pin orientation板间排线与针位方向

Relate the marked edge, polarization feature and both end views. Document fixed folds and strain support separately on the assembly drawing. Match ribbon conductor count, gauge and spacing to the connector.排线连接应把标识边、极化缺口和两端针位视图对应起来，固定折弯与拉力支撑在装配图中单列。选配时，带状线的芯数、线规和导体节距匹配连接器。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Show marked edge, polarization notch and pin one at every end.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，图纸明确红边、极化缺口和1脚在每一端的关系。

Board service and module removal板卡检修与模块拆装

Keep latches and cable ends accessible for board service. Review removal paths and cable allowance alongside the controlled pinout. Define location tooling, press travel and strain-clamp assembly in the process.板卡检修要保留锁扣与连接端的操作空间，拆装路径和线缆余量应与受控针脚表一起确认。选配时，定位工装、压合行程与应力夹装配形成工艺要求。

Equipment panels and circular interfaces设备面板与圆形接口

Start with the cutout, panel thickness, mounting direction and rear clearance. Coordinate contact arrangement and cable exit with the complete assembly view. Confirm the IDC family, connector pitch and cavity views together.面板连接以开孔、板厚、装配方向和后方净空为起点；接点排列与电缆出口还需要对应完整总装视图。选配时，IDC系列、连接器节距与针位视图成套确认。

Internal connections in racks and multi-board equipment机架与多板卡内部连接

In multi-board equipment, define slots, board orientation and pin mapping at each end. Keep ribbon folds and retention clear of module-removal paths. Match ribbon conductor count, gauge and spacing to the connector.多板卡设备需要清楚定义插槽、板间方向和各端针位，排线的折返与固定不能妨碍模块抽出。选配时，带状线的芯数、线规和导体节距匹配连接器。
