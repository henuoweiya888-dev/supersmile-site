APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Detachable circular connections可拆卸圆形连接

Check gender, keying, coupling and end-face views together. Equal contact counts do not replace confirmation of the exact series and part numbers. Include the Binder family, coding and complete part number in the enquiry.圆形接口需要同时核对公母、键位、锁紧方式和端视图，针数相同并不能替代完整系列与料号确认。选配时，Binder系列、编码和完整料号同时纳入询价。

Terminal wiring inside control cabinets控制柜内的端子配线

Coordinate terminal positions, duct exits and service access within the cabinet. Wire labels should correspond to the circuit table and equipment ports. Confirm locking, contact layout and polarity against the mating part.柜内连接需协调端子位置、线槽出口和可维护空间，线号应能对应回路表与设备端口。选配时，锁紧形式、接点排布及极性按对插端确认。

Shield layers and end terminations屏蔽层与端部接续

Define shield coverage, drain wires and grounding method. Treat shield-continuity checks and system interference testing as separate tasks. Define field-assembled or molded form and match the entry clamping diameter.屏蔽选择需要明确覆盖结构、排流线和接地方式，屏蔽连续性检查与整机抗干扰试验应分别定义。选配时，明确装配式或模制式，并匹配入口夹持外径。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Specify test conditions for the mated, locked and installed state.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，规定插合、锁紧和安装状态下的试验条件。

Equipment panels and circular interfaces设备面板与圆形接口

Start with the cutout, panel thickness, mounting direction and rear clearance. Coordinate contact arrangement and cable exit with the complete assembly view. Include the Binder family, coding and complete part number in the enquiry.面板连接以开孔、板厚、装配方向和后方净空为起点；接点排列与电缆出口还需要对应完整总装视图。选配时，Binder系列、编码和完整料号同时纳入询价。

Internal machine connections and service设备机内连接与维护

Keep internal connections clear of working zones and moving parts. Allow appropriate cable length between supports, interfaces and service paths. Confirm locking, contact layout and polarity against the mating part.设备机内连接要避开加工区与运动部件，并在固定点、接口和检修路径之间保留合理线缆余量。选配时，锁紧形式、接点排布及极性按对插端确认。
