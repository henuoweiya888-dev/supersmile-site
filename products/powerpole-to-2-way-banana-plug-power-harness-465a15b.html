APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Housing and contact combinations壳体与接点配套

Define housings, contacts and locking parts as a matched series. Color and a similar outline can aid identification but cannot replace mating drawings. Define housings and matching contacts for the exact Anderson family.壳体、接点与锁止件需按具体系列成套定义，外壳颜色或相近轮廓只能帮助识别，不能代替对插图纸。选配时，按具体Anderson系列确定壳体及配套接点。

Battery and removable power connections电池与可拆电源连接

Define polarity, continuous and peak load, and permitted connection conditions. Record auxiliary signals separately from the main power path. Confirm colour together with mechanical keying and polarity.电池连接要明确极性、持续与峰值负载以及允许插拔条件，辅助信号回路与主电源路径应分别记录。选配时，颜色须结合机械键位和极性一并确认。

Internal machine connections and service设备机内连接与维护

Keep internal connections clear of working zones and moving parts. Allow appropriate cable length between supports, interfaces and service paths. Match conductor range, plating and tooling to the contact part documents.设备机内连接要避开加工区与运动部件，并在固定点、接口和检修路径之间保留合理线缆余量。选配时，接点导体范围、镀层和工具按料号文件匹配。

Contact and fine-wire termination端子与细线端接

Contact selection depends on conductor construction, insulation diameter and tooling limits. Set wire preparation and crimp inspection for the actual contact part. Specify continuous current, temperature-rise conditions and permitted mating duty separately.端子选择同时取决于导体结构、绝缘外径和工具范围；备线尺寸与压接检查应落到实际接点型号。选配时，分别规定持续电流、温升条件与允许插拔工况。

Equipment power-distribution branches设备电源分配支路

Select power branches from actual loads, circuit protection and route length. Include termination, retention and service positions in the wiring definition. Define housings and matching contacts for the exact Anderson family.供电分支以实际负载、回路保护和线长为选配依据，端头、固定和检修位置也需列入布线定义。选配时，按具体Anderson系列确定壳体及配套接点。

Vehicle service connections车辆检修转接位置

Provide access for mating and service tools. Record end-face views and cable retention after service; appearance alone does not establish interchangeability. Confirm colour together with mechanical keying and polarity.可拆卸尾线应保留插拔与工具空间，并记录端视图和维修后的固定方式，避免只凭外观确定替换关系。选配时，颜色须结合机械键位和极性一并确认。
