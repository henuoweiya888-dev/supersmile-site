CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Conductor, insulation and material choices导体、绝缘与选材要点

Use the scsi 50-pin 4-connector idc ribbon cable form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以SCSI五十针四接头IDC排线的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

02

Flat conductor arrangement扁平排列结构

The cable uses a flat arrangement, allowing compact routing around insertion direction, folds and end orientation.线缆采用扁平排列形态，可围绕插入方向、折转位置和端头朝向安排紧凑布线。

03

Conductor and insulation selection导体与绝缘选配

Select conductors and insulation from circuit loads, diameter limits, bending and exposure. A material name does not replace ratings or validation results.从电路负载、外径限制、弯曲方式与环境条件选择导体和绝缘体系；材料名称不应直接代替额定参数或验证结果。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Inter-board ribbon cables and pin orientation板间排线与针位方向

Relate the marked edge, polarization feature and both end views. Document fixed folds and strain support separately on the assembly drawing. Record conductor spacing, count and individual area separately.排线连接应把标识边、极化缺口和两端针位视图对应起来，固定折弯与拉力支撑在装配图中单列。选配时，分别记录导体间距、芯数及每芯截面。

Routing and retention in confined spaces狭小空间的布线与固定

Define bends, retention points and assembly order in confined spaces. Do not compensate for an incorrect interface direction or length with forced folds. Confirm insulation thickness, total width and marked edge for the exact model.狭小空间布线需明确转弯、固定点和拆装顺序，不能用强行折线弥补接口方向或长度误差。选配时，绝缘厚度、总宽度及标识边按准确型号确认。

Equipment control-board connections设备控制板连接

Document pin-row views, mating direction and nearby wire support. Check access to the latch and contacts after assembly. Define termination form, connectors and stripping dimensions together.板端连接要给出针排视图、插合方向和近端线缆支撑；检查装配后是否仍能操作锁扣并查看接点。选配时，端接形式、连接器与剥线尺寸成套确定。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Specify radius and duty separately for fixed folds and dynamic bending.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，固定折弯与动态弯折分别规定半径和使用边界。

Board service and module removal板卡检修与模块拆装

Keep latches and cable ends accessible for board service. Review removal paths and cable allowance alongside the controlled pinout. Record conductor spacing, count and individual area separately.板卡检修要保留锁扣与连接端的操作空间，拆装路径和线缆余量应与受控针脚表一起确认。选配时，分别记录导体间距、芯数及每芯截面。

Internal connections in racks and multi-board equipment机架与多板卡内部连接

In multi-board equipment, define slots, board orientation and pin mapping at each end. Keep ribbon folds and retention clear of module-removal paths. Confirm insulation thickness, total width and marked edge for the exact model.多板卡设备需要清楚定义插槽、板间方向和各端针位，排线的折返与固定不能妨碍模块抽出。选配时，绝缘厚度、总宽度及标识边按准确型号确认。
