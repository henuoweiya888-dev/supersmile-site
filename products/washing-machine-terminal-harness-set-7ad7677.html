APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Routing boundaries around hot equipment热区设备的布线边界

Separate continuous temperature, peaks and exposure time around hot equipment. Review wire, terminals and protective parts against their own limits. Confirm conductor construction and insulation material from the full PTFE wire specification.热区布线需要区分连续温度、峰值和暴露时间，线材、端子及保护件应按各自限制评审。选配时，用完整PTFE线材规格确认导体结构及绝缘材料。

Instrument inputs and measurement leads仪器输入与测量引线

Start measurement leads from signal type, input conditions and grounding. Select pairs and shields for the actual measurement range and noise target. Record insulation dimensions, temperature conditions and voltage basis separately.测量引线以信号类型、输入条件和接地方式为起点，线对与屏蔽选择需要对应实际量程和噪声目标。选配时，分别记录绝缘尺寸、温度条件与电压依据。

Shield layers and end terminations屏蔽层与端部接续

Define shield coverage, drain wires and grounding method. Treat shield-continuity checks and system interference testing as separate tasks. Stripping and termination tooling must control strand damage, recession and insulation retention.屏蔽选择需要明确覆盖结构、排流线和接地方式，屏蔽连续性检查与整机抗干扰试验应分别定义。选配时，剥皮和端接工装需控制伤股、回缩与绝缘保持。

Contact and fine-wire termination端子与细线端接

Contact selection depends on conductor construction, insulation diameter and tooling limits. Set wire preparation and crimp inspection for the actual contact part. Review heat-shrink, molding and fixing for material compatibility and thermal conditions.端子选择同时取决于导体结构、绝缘外径和工具范围；备线尺寸与压接检查应落到实际接点型号。选配时，热缩、包胶与固定方式按材料相容和热条件评审。

Routing and retention in confined spaces狭小空间的布线与固定

Define bends, retention points and assembly order in confined spaces. Do not compensate for an incorrect interface direction or length with forced folds. Confirm conductor construction and insulation material from the full PTFE wire specification.狭小空间布线需明确转弯、固定点和拆装顺序，不能用强行折线弥补接口方向或长度误差。选配时，用完整PTFE线材规格确认导体结构及绝缘材料。

Internal machine connections and service设备机内连接与维护

Keep internal connections clear of working zones and moving parts. Allow appropriate cable length between supports, interfaces and service paths. Record insulation dimensions, temperature conditions and voltage basis separately.设备机内连接要避开加工区与运动部件，并在固定点、接口和检修路径之间保留合理线缆余量。选配时，分别记录绝缘尺寸、温度条件与电压依据。
