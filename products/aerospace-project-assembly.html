Build to a Controlled Mission Definition

Configuration, mass, environment, workmanship and evidence under one controlled definition

An aerospace harness is an installed system component. Connector family and wire gauge are only the beginning: every branch, shield, clamp, splice, backshell, label and test point must relate to the approved design, installation environment and acceptance plan.

    Aerospace Project Assembly Questions

    Does this page mean Super Smile is an approved flight-hardware supplier?

    No. The page describes a category available for project review. Customer, platform, quality-system, personnel, process and part approvals must be defined and completed before any flight-use claim.

    Can you build from an existing aerospace harness sample?

    A sample supports dimensional and interface study, but it cannot reveal all conductor specifications, derating, shield topology, prohibited materials, hidden splices or qualification history. Approved drawings and engineering confirmation remain necessary.

    Are MIL-DTL-38999-style connectors enough to make a harness aerospace grade?

    No. Connector approval, exact part number and source are only part of the system. Wire, accessories, routing, workmanship, documentation, testing and platform approval also apply.

    Which workmanship standard can be used?

    The customer or program must flow down the applicable revision and any addendum, tailoring or drawing-specific requirements. We review the requested standard and capability before quotation rather than selecting one unilaterally.

    What testing can be included?

    Routine checks can cover configuration, dimensions, workmanship and continuity. Resistance, insulation, dielectric, signal-integrity, environmental or functional tests are added only when method, hardware compatibility, specimen quantity and limits are defined.

    What is needed for an initial capability review?

    Send the platform and installation zone, drawing and revision, connector and wire specifications, circuit/shield schedule, geometry and mass constraints, environment, workmanship and inspection requirements, quantity, documentation package and qualification or source-inspection plan.

    Start from the controlled aircraft or spacecraft interface drawing and approved connector specification.

    02 Qualification boundary

    Identify which purchased components require qualification and what evidence the finished project assembly needs.

    03 Circuit and segregation

    Define power, signal and RF circuits, shield bonds and required separation in the equipment.

    Agree wire construction, finish, lot traceability and restrictions on substitutions before purchasing.

    Specify vibration, thermal range, fluids, mass and routing constraints for the actual installation.
