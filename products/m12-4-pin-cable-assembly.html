CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Circular interfaces concentrate the connections at the cable ends, with threaded coupling securing the mating position. Selection considers the interface keying, pin orientation, end dimensions and access needed to operate the coupling.圆形接口便于把接线点集中在电缆两端，螺纹连接用于固定插合位置。选配这类组件时，应同时考虑接口键位、针脚方向、端头长度以及锁紧操作所需空间。

Are all four-core M12 interfaces interchangeable?四芯M12接口都可以直接互换吗？
