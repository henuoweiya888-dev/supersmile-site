CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the automotive multi-connector corrugated-conduit main harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以汽车多接头波纹护管主线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

02

Main-trunk routing form主干布线形式

The connections follow a main-trunk form, with the overall route, end positions and service allowance arranged around equipment datums.连接以主干布线形式组织，可围绕设备固定基准安排整体走向、端头位置和可拆卸余量。

03

Corrugated covering form波纹管形态防护

The external covering has a corrugated form, with openings and fixing arranged around route bends and breakouts.外部覆盖采用波纹管形态，可围绕走线弯曲和分支位置安排端口与固定。

04

Multiple connector positions多端头连接位置

Multiple ends provide separate connection positions. Layout and spacing can follow the module arrangement.多个端头提供分别接入的位置，线束展开方向与各端头间距可围绕模块布局定义。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Interfaces within engine-bay layouts发动机舱内的接口布置

Map heat sources, fluid exposure and service routes together. Allow assembly space around connector ends and cable supports. Record full part numbers for housings, contacts, seals and secondary locks.发动机舱连接要把热源、油液暴露和检修路径放在同一张布线图中，端头与固定点也需留出装配空间。选配时，同时记录壳体、端子、线封与二次锁的完整料号。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Check mating drawings, keying and orientation for the exact series.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，按具体系列核对对插图、键位和接口方向。

Equipment panels and circular interfaces设备面板与圆形接口

Start with the cutout, panel thickness, mounting direction and rear clearance. Coordinate contact arrangement and cable exit with the complete assembly view. Use contact application documents to define conductor range, insulation support and crimp tooling.面板连接以开孔、板厚、装配方向和后方净空为起点；接点排列与电缆出口还需要对应完整总装视图。选配时，用端子应用文件确定导体范围、绝缘支撑与压接工装。

Relays and auxiliary power ends继电器与辅助供电端

Separate coil and load circuits at relay leads. Define terminals, conductor size and protection devices together from the schematic. List specified components and proposed alternatives separately in the approved BOM.继电器引线需要区分线圈与负载回路，端子定义、线径和保护元件由原理图共同确定。选配时，指定物料与拟议替代分别列入认可清单。

Enclosure entries and cable retention机箱穿线与入口固定

Match cable entries to actual diameter, panel thickness and fixing method. Keep jacket retention independent of the internal termination. Record full part numbers for housings, contacts, seals and secondary locks.线缆入口需匹配实际外径、板厚和固定方式；护套夹持与内部端接之间应保留独立的支撑路径。选配时，同时记录壳体、端子、线封与二次锁的完整料号。

Vehicle service connections车辆检修转接位置

Provide access for mating and service tools. Record end-face views and cable retention after service; appearance alone does not establish interchangeability. Check mating drawings, keying and orientation for the exact series.可拆卸尾线应保留插拔与工具空间，并记录端视图和维修后的固定方式，避免只凭外观确定替换关系。选配时，按具体系列核对对插图、键位和接口方向。
