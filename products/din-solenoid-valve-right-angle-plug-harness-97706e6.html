Industrial sensors and actuators工业传感器与执行器

Overmolding can protect compact interfaces and cable exits on sensors, valves, switches and actuators exposed to handling, vibration or contamination. Assess jacket compatibility, hardness and appearance together.包胶可保护传感器、阀、开关与执行器上的紧凑接口和出线，使其适应操作、振动或污染。选配时，材料与护套相容性、硬度和外观一起评估。

Automation and production equipment自动化与生产设备

Molded connectors provide repeatable handling and strain relief for equipment modules, fixtures and replaceable station cables. Define exit angle, transition and parting positions in the end geometry.成型接口为设备模块、工装与可更换工位电缆提供可重复操作和应力消除。选配时，3D端头定义出线角度、过渡段和分型位置。

Appliances and commercial equipment电器与商用设备

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

What is the difference between overmolding and using a separate boot?包覆成型与安装独立护套有什么区别？

Overmolding forms material directly around the insert and cable in a tool, creating a controlled integrated geometry. A separate boot is assembled afterward and may not provide the same retention, sealing or dimensional control.包覆成型在模具中直接围绕嵌件与线缆形成材料，得到受控一体几何；独立护套在之后装配，其保持、密封与尺寸控制可能不同。

Which molding material should be used?应该使用哪种包胶材料？

The choice depends on hardness, flexing, temperature, chemicals, abrasion, flame requirements, colour, cable-jacket compatibility and processing temperature. A material name alone is not enough.选择取决于硬度、弯折、温度、化学、耐磨、阻燃、颜色、护套兼容与加工温度，单一材料名称不足以完成选型。

Can an existing mold be reused?可以复用现有模具吗？

Possibly, after insert position, cable diameter, interface clearance, exit direction, parting line, gate and required performance are compared. Similar appearance does not guarantee fit.可以审核，但需要比较嵌件位置、线径、接口空间、出线方向、分型线、浇口与性能要求，外形相似不代表能够适配。
