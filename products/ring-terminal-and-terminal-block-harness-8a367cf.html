CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the ring-terminal and terminal-block harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以环形端子与端子排组合分支线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Branched end layout分支端头布局

Conductors separate toward different ends, creating individual connection points. Branch lengths and directions can follow the equipment modules.导线向不同端头分出，形成独立接入位置。各支路可围绕设备模块分别安排长度与方向。

02

Ring-terminal end环形端子端头

A ring-terminal end provides a mounting-hole connection. Lead direction, support and insulation coverage can follow the stud position.末端采用带安装孔的环形端子形式，可围绕螺柱位置安排接线方向、端头支撑与绝缘覆盖。

03

Terminal-block connection form端子排接入形式

Leads gather at terminal-block or wiring-board connection points, allowing circuits to be organized by terminal number.多根引线汇集到端子排或接线板连接位置，便于按端子编号组织不同回路。

04

End transition layout端头转接与组合

The assembly organizes a transition or combined connection, with end directions, transition sections and fixing arranged around the equipment positions.组件以转接或组合形式组织连接，可围绕两端设备位置安排端头方向、过渡段与固定。

05

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

What does turnkey supply include?交钥匙供货包含什么？

The exact scope is project-specific. It can include engineering review, sourcing, customer-supplied parts, harness assembly, accessories, labels, inspection, records, kitting and packaging. The quotation and approved documents define the boundary.准确范围按项目确定，可以包含工程审核、采购、客供件、线束组装、附件、标签、检验、记录、配套与包装。报价与批准文件定义边界。

Can you work with customer-supplied parts?可以使用客户提供的物料吗？

Yes, when receiving condition, quantities, ownership, shortage handling, unused return, traceability and responsibility for part suitability are agreed before production.可以，但生产前需约定收料状态、数量、所有权、短缺处理、余料退回、追踪与物料适用责任。

How are substitutions handled?替代件怎样管理？

Alternatives are compared technically and commercially, then released only through the agreed customer approval route. A temporary deviation is recorded separately from the standard BOM.替代件经过技术与商务比较，只能通过约定客户审批路径放行；临时偏差与标准BOM分开记录。
