CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the actuator large rectangular plug 5-wire repair pigtail form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以执行器大矩形插头五线修复尾线的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Rectangular interface form矩形接口外形

The rectangular interface form allows end position and service clearance to follow the mating face, latch and cable exit.接口采用矩形轮廓，可围绕插入面、卡扣与线束出口安排端头位置和拆装空间。

02

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

03

Pigtail connection form尾线连接形式

A lead section extends behind the connector, allowing length, further termination and support to follow the equipment connection.插接端后保留引线段，便于按设备接线位置确定长度、后续端接与支撑。

04

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

05

Current path and end termination载流与端部连接

Select conductors and terminations from continuous and peak loads, installed length and temperature rise. Confirm lug bore, contact surface, insulation coverage and fixing as a set.依据持续与峰值负载、安装长度和温升要求选择导体与端接；端子孔径、接触面、绝缘覆盖及固定方式需要成套确认。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Can the same harness fit every VGT or VNT actuator?同一线束能适配所有 VGT 或 VNT 执行器吗？

No. Match the exact actuator, mating connector, circuit assignment and installation. A family name or similar housing is only a starting point.不能。需要匹配准确执行器、对插件、回路分配和安装条件，系列名称或相似壳体只是识别起点。

Does replacing the lead remove the need for calibration?更换连接线就不需要标定了吗？

No such assumption should be made. Wiring repair and actuator calibration are different operations; follow the equipment-specific service procedure.不能这样推断。接线维修与执行器标定是不同操作，应遵循具体设备的维修程序。

Can you make a cable from an existing sample?可以按现有样品制作连接线吗？

Yes, subject to authorization and confirmation of the interfaces, circuit, materials, dimensions and inspection requirements. Unknown pin functions need documentation.可以，但需具备使用授权，并确认接口、回路、材料、尺寸和检验要求。未知针脚功能仍需文件依据。
