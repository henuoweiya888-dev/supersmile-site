Configuration & Project Review

01 Cable exit load

Define expected pull, bend and twist directions before shaping the strain relief.

02 Molded or separate component

Distinguish an integral molded boot from a separate snap-in bushing or cord grip.

03 Material interface

Confirm cable jacket, mold resin and connector insert materials with actual samples.

04 Support geometry

Set taper, supported length, exit angle and the minimum available bend space.

05 Panel fit

For separate parts, confirm panel thickness, hole geometry and the actual cable diameter range.

06 Mechanical validation

Agree pull, flex and twist tests on the complete assembly and its installed fixture.

Send the part number, drawing or sample to confirm the replacement configuration and sourcing plan.
