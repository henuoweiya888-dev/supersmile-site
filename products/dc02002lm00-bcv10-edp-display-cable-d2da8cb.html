CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Review the low-profile ends, covered section and cable exits against the target board and panel documentation.围绕图示屏线的低矮端头、包覆段和出线方向，按目标主板与面板资料确认连接。

01

Two low-profile ends两个低矮连接端

Both ends have a low-profile form in the photograph. Confirm the board or panel reference, contact face and insertion direction for each end from connector drawings.实拍可见两端采用低矮接口结构。每端对应的板端或屏端型号、接触面和插入方向需由连接器图纸确认。

02

Flat covered routing section扁平包覆走线段

Match the covered section to internal clearance, fixing points and assembly sequence. Confirm material, thickness and shielding from component documentation.包覆走线段需要与设备内部间隙、固定点和装配顺序配合。材料、厚度与屏蔽方式按物料资料确认。

03

eDP signal and power mappingeDP信号与电源分配

Use board and panel documentation to define main-link, auxiliary-communication, power and ground assignments. Contact count alone does not establish the mapping of another display cable.依据主板与面板资料确认主链路、辅助通信及电源接地针位，不能只按针数套用其他屏线的连接表。

04

Cable exits and bend allowance出线方向与弯曲余量

Confirm end orientation, covered-section transitions and bend allowance. Review any moving enclosure parts separately to avoid transferring loads to connector contacts.确认端头方向、包覆过渡位置和弯曲余量。机壳活动部位需另行评审，避免把拉力传至连接器接点。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These directions concern equipment using an eDP display link. Establish the internal interface, routing and cable fit from actual equipment documentation and sample review.以下为采用 eDP 显示链路时可讨论的设计与装配方向。内部接口、安装路径及屏线适配性以实际设备资料和样品确认为依据。

Board-to-panel display links板端至屏端连接

Where the target board and display panel use eDP, confirm the complete pin table, lanes, power and ground from both sets of documentation. Set length and end orientation from the enclosure; the cable code alone does not establish system fit.目标主板与显示面板采用 eDP 接口时，应按双方资料确认完整针脚表、信号通道、供电与接地。线长和端头方向随实际机壳确定，不凭代号推定整机兼容。

Display-board connection review显示板连接端评审

Review connector series, contact face, insertion direction and retention around the display board. Confirm cable-to-board assembly clearance and removal space from the target eDP interface documentation and actual enclosure.显示板附近的低矮连接端需核对系列、接触面、插入方向和保持方式。线缆与板端的装配间隙及拔插空间，需按目标 eDP 接口资料与实际机壳确认。

HMI display enclosures人机界面显示机壳

For an HMI that uses an eDP display link, coordinate panel clearance, board position, lead fixing and assembly sequence. Define touch, backlight and other circuits from equipment data; their presence on this cable is not assumed.在人机界面设备采用 eDP 显示链路时，协调屏端空间、主板位置、走线固定和装配顺序。触摸、背光及其他回路分别按设备资料定义，不默认由本屏线承载。

Display-equipment service matching显示设备维修匹配

For display-equipment replacement work, verify system revision, panel reference, connectors and the complete pin mapping. External display ports or screen size do not establish internal-cable fit; confirm the interfaces and routing before assembly.维修更换需要核对整机版本、面板型号、连接器及完整针脚关系。外部显示接口或屏幕尺寸不能证明内部屏线适配，应在装机前完成接口与走线确认。

Display-link sample validation显示链路样品验证

Validate sample display behavior on the target board and panel as well as checking continuity and contact mapping. Record test conditions, length, shield grounding and acceptance limits; this page does not assign an unverified transmission specification.屏线样品除导通与针位检查外，还需按项目要求在目标主板和面板上确认显示表现。测试条件、线长、屏蔽接地与验收限值应记录，本页不提供未经验证的传输指标。

Low-profile display-cable routing薄型屏线布置

Plan bend allowance, fixing positions and strain relief for the covered cable section and low-profile ends. Review assembly sequence and moving parts in the actual enclosure, keeping installation loads away from the panel connector.薄型屏线的包覆段与端头需要安排弯曲余量、固定位置和应力释放。结合实际机壳检查装配顺序及运动部位，避免把屏端接头作为牵拉固定点。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Can it replace a similar-looking LVDS display cable?可以直接替换外形相近的LVDS屏线吗？

Confirm eDP and LVDS link and pin-mapping requirements separately. Similar connector outlines or contact counts do not establish interchangeability. No protocol-conversion function is claimed for this assembly.eDP 与 LVDS 的链路及针位要求须分别确认。接头外形和针数相近不能证明可互换，本组件不声明提供协议转换功能。

Do DC02002LM00 or BCV10 identify a confirmed system fit?DC02002LM00或BCV10代号能确定整机适配吗？

Confirm board and panel references, system revision, both connectors and the complete pin table. A code and appearance help identify the example; establish system fit from documentation and sample validation.还需核对主板与面板型号、整机版本、两端连接器和完整针脚表。型号代号与外观可以辅助识别，装机适配须按实际资料及样品确认。

What should be checked during sampling?样品阶段需要检查什么？

Check interfaces, mapping, continuity, length and assembly first. Then verify display behavior, shield grounding and mechanical conditions in the target equipment to agreed project criteria, recording the test conditions and limits.先核对接口、针位、导通、长度及装配，再按项目要求在目标设备上检查显示表现、屏蔽接地和机械工况。测试条件与限值应形成受控记录。

Start with board and panel documentation从主板与面板资料开始

Share board and panel references, connectors, pin tables, target length, covering and routing requirements, and the display conditions to validate in the sample.请提供主板与面板型号、连接器和针脚表、目标长度、包覆与走线要求，以及样品需验证的显示工况。
