PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is a cluster pigtail the same as a complete dashboard harness?仪表盘短引线等同于整套仪表台线束吗？

No. A pigtail covers a defined cluster interface; a cross-car harness can include switches, HVAC, displays, junctions and body-controller branches. Specify the exact breakpoints and vehicle variant.不等同。短引线只覆盖指定仪表接口；横向总线束可能包含开关、空调、显示、配电与车身控制器分支。应明确断开点及车型变型。

Can matching cluster plugs be interchanged?仪表插头能对插，就可以互换吗？

No. Confirm cavity view, pin map, power domains, network protocol, cluster and controller references, and software configuration. Plug fit alone does not prove displayed information or warning behavior.不能。还需确认孔位视图、针脚表、供电域、网络协议、仪表与控制器料号以及软件配置。能插上不能证明显示或警示行为正确。

Can you reproduce an airbag or SRS branch from an old sample?可以按旧样复制安全气囊或 SRS 分支吗？

Not from a sample alone. SRS circuits are safety critical and require OEM-approved drawings, component traceability, controlled process and separately defined validation. We would first review whether that scope is appropriate for the project.不能只凭旧样。SRS 回路属于安全关键部件，需要整车方批准图纸、元件追溯、受控工艺和单独验证。我们会先审核项目范围是否适合承接。

Share mating interfaces, a circuit table, target lengths or a sample for the instrument cluster terminal harness. We can use them to review configuration and sample requirements.提供仪表盘插接端子线束的对插接口、回路表、目标长度或参考样品，我们据此梳理选配与样品确认事项。
