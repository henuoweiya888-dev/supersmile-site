CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the truck rectangular main plug with circular branch harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以卡车矩形主插头带圆形分支连接线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Branched end layout分支端头布局

Conductors separate toward different ends, creating individual connection points. Branch lengths and directions can follow the equipment modules.导线向不同端头分出，形成独立接入位置。各支路可围绕设备模块分别安排长度与方向。

02

Circular connection end圆形连接端

The end uses a circular interface form, with connection positions planned around mating direction, retention and cable exit.端头具有圆形接口形式，可围绕对插方向、端部固定和线缆出口规划连接位置。

03

Rectangular interface form矩形接口外形

The rectangular interface form allows end position and service clearance to follow the mating face, latch and cable exit.接口采用矩形轮廓，可围绕插入面、卡扣与线束出口安排端头位置和拆装空间。

04

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

05

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is truck harness one standard product?卡车线束是一种标准产品吗？

No. It is a broad category that includes many complete and zone-specific assemblies. Vehicle platform, year, wheelbase, cab, powertrain, installed options and body equipment determine the released configuration.不是。它是包含整车线束与多种区域分线束的大类，车辆平台、年份、轴距、驾驶室、动力总成、选装与上装共同决定放行配置。

Can a harness be copied from an existing sample?可以按照现有样品复制线束吗？

A sample is useful for geometry, connector and circuit tracing, but hidden materials, damaged areas, field repairs, option content and electrical intent still need documentation or controlled validation.样品有助于还原几何、连接器和回路，但隐藏材料、损伤区域、现场维修、选装内容与电气意图仍需文件或受控验证。

Can 12 V and 24 V truck harnesses use the same design?12 V 与 24 V 卡车线束可以共用同一设计吗？

Not by assumption. Load current, device rating, fuse strategy, wire size, relay or controller behavior, connector limits and voltage-drop budget must be reviewed for the actual system.不能直接假设。必须针对实际系统审核负载电流、设备额定值、保险策略、线径、继电器或控制器行为、连接器限制与压降预算。
