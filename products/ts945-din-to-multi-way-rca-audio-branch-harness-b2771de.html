PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does an XLR connector always mean balanced audio?使用 XLR 接口就一定是平衡音频吗？

No. XLR describes a connector family. The equipment circuits, pin assignment, cable pair and shield termination determine whether the path is balanced.不一定。XLR 描述的是接口家族，是否平衡取决于设备电路、针脚定义、线对与屏蔽端接。

Is a TRS cable always stereo?TRS 线一定是立体声吗？

No. TRS can carry stereo unbalanced audio, mono balanced audio, insert send/return or a proprietary circuit. Confirm both equipment endpoints and the pin map.不一定。TRS 可以承载非平衡立体声、平衡单声道、插入发送/返回或专用回路，必须确认两端设备和针脚。

Can microphone cable be used for loudspeakers?麦克风线可以用作扬声器线吗？

It should not be assumed. Loudspeaker circuits can require much larger conductors and different current, temperature and connector considerations than low-level microphone paths.不能直接假设可以。扬声器回路通常需要更大的导体，并具有不同的电流、温度与接口要求。
