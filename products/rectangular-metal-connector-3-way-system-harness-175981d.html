APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Rectangular interfaces and module layouts矩形接口与模块排列

Check insert orientation, latch access and module order in rectangular interfaces. Identify power and signal circuits separately in the contact table. Confirm the Harting family, frame and installation order of every module.矩形重载接口需要核对插芯朝向、锁扣操作和模块顺序，动力与信号回路应在接点表中分别标明。选配时，确认Harting系列、框架及每个模块的安装顺序。

Equipment panels and circular interfaces设备面板与圆形接口

Start with the cutout, panel thickness, mounting direction and rear clearance. Coordinate contact arrangement and cable exit with the complete assembly view. Define housing size, latch and panel fixing around assembly clearance.面板连接以开孔、板厚、装配方向和后方净空为起点；接点排列与电缆出口还需要对应完整总装视图。选配时，外壳尺寸、锁扣和面板固定按总装空间定义。

Internal machine connections and service设备机内连接与维护

Keep internal connections clear of working zones and moving parts. Allow appropriate cable length between supports, interfaces and service paths. Assign power, signal and ground contacts to the actual conductors and modules.设备机内连接要避开加工区与运动部件，并在固定点、接口和检修路径之间保留合理线缆余量。选配时，动力、信号和接地接点对应实际线径与模块。

Equipment power-distribution branches设备电源分配支路

Select power branches from actual loads, circuit protection and route length. Include termination, retention and service positions in the wiring definition. Document cable entry, backshell, housing ground and cable shield separately.供电分支以实际负载、回路保护和线长为选配依据，端头、固定和检修位置也需列入布线定义。选配时，入口、尾壳、壳体接地与电缆屏蔽分别记录。

Terminal wiring inside control cabinets控制柜内的端子配线

Coordinate terminal positions, duct exits and service access within the cabinet. Wire labels should correspond to the circuit table and equipment ports. Confirm the Harting family, frame and installation order of every module.柜内连接需协调端子位置、线槽出口和可维护空间，线号应能对应回路表与设备端口。选配时，确认Harting系列、框架及每个模块的安装顺序。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Define housing size, latch and panel fixing around assembly clearance.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，外壳尺寸、锁扣和面板固定按总装空间定义。

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Do equal housings mean the internal modules match?外壳相同是否意味着内部模块相同？

Heavy-duty shell shape does not establish matching internal modules. Confirm the Harting family, frame and installation order of every module.重载外壳外形不能证明内部模块一致。确认Harting系列、框架及每个模块的安装顺序。

Why confirm module order and keying as a set?模块顺序和键位为什么要成套确认？

Confirm insert sequence and keying as a set. Define housing size, latch and panel fixing around assembly clearance.插芯序列与键位需成套确认。外壳尺寸、锁扣和面板固定按总装空间定义。

Can ground contacts and housing ground be recorded as one item?接地接点和壳体接地可以合并记录吗？

Record earth contacts and housing grounding separately. Assign power, signal and ground contacts to the actual conductors and modules.接地接点和壳体接地需分开记录。动力、信号和接地接点对应实际线径与模块。
