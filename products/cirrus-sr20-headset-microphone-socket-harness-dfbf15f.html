PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does this page mean Super Smile is an approved flight-hardware supplier?这个页面是否表示超斯迈尔已经是获批飞行硬件供应商？

No. The page describes a category available for project review. Customer, platform, quality-system, personnel, process and part approvals must be defined and completed before any flight-use claim.不是。本页面说明该类别可进入项目审核。客户、平台、质量体系、人员、工艺与零件批准必须另行定义和完成，之后才能作出任何飞行用途声明。

Can you build from an existing aerospace harness sample?可以依据现有航空线束样品开发吗？

A sample supports dimensional and interface study, but it cannot reveal all conductor specifications, derating, shield topology, prohibited materials, hidden splices or qualification history. Approved drawings and engineering confirmation remain necessary.样品有助于尺寸与接口研究，但不能揭示全部导体规范、降额、屏蔽拓扑、禁用材料、隐藏接续或鉴定历史，仍需批准图纸与工程确认。

Are MIL-DTL-38999-style connectors enough to make a harness aerospace grade?使用类似 MIL-DTL-38999 的连接器就足以称为航空级线束吗？

No. Connector approval, exact part number and source are only part of the system. Wire, accessories, routing, workmanship, documentation, testing and platform approval also apply.不足。连接器批准、准确型号与来源只是系统的一部分；线材、附件、走线、工艺、文件、测试与平台批准同样适用。
