Loader Frame Looped Branch Harness装载机车架环形分支线束

Loader Frame Looped Branch Harness can be configured around equipment interfaces and routing. Off-road machines combine mobile-vehicle wiring with industrial actuators, hydraulic systems and exposed articulation. Harness design must follow the actual machine zone, movement envelope, duty cycle and service method.装载机车架环形分支线束可围绕设备接口与安装路径进行配置。非道路机械把移动车辆布线、工业执行器、液压系统与裸露铰接结构结合在一起。线束设计必须服从实际机器分区、运动包络、工作循环与维修方式；单靠连接器密封无法弥补错误走线、无支撑重量、积水、分支过硬或端子与线封组合不匹配。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Hydraulics and actuators液压与执行机构

Allocate valve, sensor and drive circuits from machine drawings. Use machine model and serial range to identify the configuration.阀组、传感器和驱动回路按设备图分配。选配时，机型与序列范围用于识别准确配置。

Cab controls驾驶室控制

Keep joystick, display and switch branches serviceable. List controller, valve-bank and sensor interfaces separately.手柄、显示与开关支路兼顾维护接近性。选配时，控制器、阀组和传感器接口分别成表。

Articulated and moving structures铰接和移动结构

Provide slack, clamps and abrasion protection for full travel. Select local materials for hydraulic oil, mud/water and temperature.按完整行程安排余量、夹持和防磨。选配时，液压油、泥水与温度用于选择局部材料。

Measurement equipment and signal interfaces测量设备与信号接口

For positioning or monitoring equipment, confirm interfaces and signal types first, then review length, fixing and outdoor conditions. Review articulation, lifting and frame routes over the full travel.定位或监测设备的连接先确认设备接口与信号形式，再核对线长、固定点和户外环境要求。选配时，铰接运动、提升机构与机架走线按完整行程审核。

Starting and auxiliary distribution起动与辅助配电

Review current, protection and engine thermal zones together. Use machine model and serial range to identify the configuration.电流、保护与发动机周边热区一起审核。选配时，机型与序列范围用于识别准确配置。

Service replacement harnesses维修与替换线束

Recover dimensions by serial range/sample and verify circuits separately. List controller, valve-bank and sensor interfaces separately.按设备序列和原样件恢复尺寸，再核对电路。选配时，控制器、阀组和传感器接口分别成表。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is a construction vehicle harness a single standard loom?工程车辆线束是一根标准线束吗？

No. It is a broad family covering complete machines and many cab, engine, chassis, boom, valve, lighting, attachment and service assemblies. Platform, serial range and options determine the released version.不是。它是覆盖整机以及驾驶室、发动机、底盘、臂架、阀组、照明、属具与维修组件的广泛家族，平台、序列号范围与选装决定放行版本。

Can the same harness be used on an excavator and loader?挖掘机与装载机可以共用同一线束吗？

Only if their approved interfaces, circuits, loads, movement, environment and geometry are actually identical. Similar controllers or connectors do not establish interchangeability.只有当批准接口、回路、负载、运动、环境与几何确实相同时才可能共用；相似控制器或连接器不能证明互换。

What must be checked at a boom or articulation point?臂架或铰接点必须检查什么？

Fixed and moving ends, full travel, twist, bend radius, support spacing, unsupported mass, rub points, hydraulic-hose interaction and service positions should all be reviewed.需要审核固定端与运动端、完整行程、扭转、弯曲半径、支撑间距、无支撑重量、摩擦点、与液压管相互作用及维修位置。
