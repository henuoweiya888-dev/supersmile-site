CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the marine circular plug & ferrule harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以船用圆形插头与管形端子线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Circular connection end圆形连接端

The end uses a circular interface form, with connection positions planned around mating direction, retention and cable exit.端头具有圆形接口形式，可围绕对插方向、端部固定和线缆出口规划连接位置。

02

End transition layout端头转接与组合

The assembly organizes a transition or combined connection, with end directions, transition sections and fixing arranged around the equipment positions.组件以转接或组合形式组织连接，可围绕两端设备位置安排端头方向、过渡段与固定。

03

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

04

Terminal connection section端子接入部位

Leads use terminal-form ends at the connection points. Orientation, fixing and insulation coverage can follow the equipment assembly.引线以端子形式接入相应连接位置，可按设备装配方式安排端头方向、固定和绝缘覆盖。

05

Circular mating interface圆形接口匹配

Match the series, keying, contact count, mating direction and locking method. Similar shell outlines do not establish compatible dimensions or contact assignments.接口选型需核对系列、键位、针数、插合方向与锁紧方式；相近外形不能替代对插尺寸和接点定义。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does flame retardance mean the circuit stays operational in fire?阻燃是否意味着火灾中保持通电？

Flame retardance is not fire-resistant circuit integrity. Specify the flame standard, test method and required classification.阻燃不等于耐火保持通电。指定阻燃标准、试验方法及要求等级。

Does flame retardance also mean halogen-free and low-smoke?阻燃是否同时代表无卤和低烟？

Flame retardance is not halogen-free or low-smoke performance. Define single-cable or bunched testing to avoid mixing their scopes.阻燃不等于无卤或低烟。明确单根或成束试验，避免混用适用范围。

Can classifications from different flame-test systems be treated as equivalent?不同阻燃体系的等级能直接等同吗？

Do not equate classifications from different test systems. Match insulation, jacket and test specimen to the supplied construction.不同试验体系等级不得直接等同。绝缘、护套与试样规格对应实际供货结构。
