CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the circular plug multi-branch robot harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以圆形插头多分支机器人线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Branched end layout分支端头布局

Conductors separate toward different ends, creating individual connection points. Branch lengths and directions can follow the equipment modules.导线向不同端头分出，形成独立接入位置。各支路可围绕设备模块分别安排长度与方向。

02

Circular connection end圆形连接端

The end uses a circular interface form, with connection positions planned around mating direction, retention and cable exit.端头具有圆形接口形式，可围绕对插方向、端部固定和线缆出口规划连接位置。

03

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is a drag-chain cable automatically suitable for a robot arm?拖链电缆可以直接用于机器人手臂吗？

Not necessarily. Linear bending and multi-axis torsion impose different loads. The actual path, bend radius, torsion and clamp layout must be reviewed.不一定。直线弯折与多轴扭转产生的载荷不同，必须审核真实路径、弯曲半径、扭转和固定布局。

What information is needed for a quotation?报价需要提供哪些资料？

Provide drawings or samples, pin definitions, circuit functions, robot or axis layout, motion path or video, stroke, bend radius, torsion, environment, quantity and testing needs.建议提供图纸或样品、针脚定义、回路功能、机器人或运动轴布局、运动路径或视频、行程、弯曲半径、扭转、环境、数量和测试需求。

Can you match an existing robotic harness?可以匹配现有机器人线束吗？

A sample can establish dimensions, interfaces and visible construction. Electrical function, hidden components and the real motion profile still require customer confirmation.样品可用于确认尺寸、接口和可见结构，但电气功能、隐藏元件与真实运动曲线仍需客户确认。
