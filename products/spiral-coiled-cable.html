Configuration & Project Review

01 Contracted and extended size

Specify contracted length, working extension, coil diameter and straight tails independently.

02 Restoring force

Define acceptable restoring force and travel so the coil does not load the device or operator.

03 Circuit function

Confirm power, signal and data requirements; coiled shape does not imply a particular protocol or data rate.

04 Material and conductor

Choose conductor construction, jacket and reinforcement for the required coil memory and environment.

05 End support

Set connector angle, cable-tail routing and strain relief at both coil transitions.

06 Motion verification

Agree extension cycles, electrical checks and recovery criteria at the intended operating temperature.

Send the part number, drawing or sample to confirm the replacement configuration and sourcing plan.
