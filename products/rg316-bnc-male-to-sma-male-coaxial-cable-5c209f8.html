CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the rg316 bnc male to sma male coaxial cable form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以RG316 BNC公头转SMA公头同轴线的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Male connector end公端插接形式

The connector end uses a male form. Installation aligns insertion direction and end protection with the mating location.连接端采用公端形式，装配时围绕对应插接位置确认插入方向与端头保护。

02

BNC-form endBNC形式端头

The BNC-form end can be arranged around the equipment panel and cable route, with clearance for mating.BNC形式的连接端可配合设备面板和线缆走向布置，插接操作空间需保留。

03

Compact coaxial interface end小型同轴接口端

The compact coaxial-form end fits a compact interface layout. Arrange bends and end support to avoid pulling directly on contacts.小型同轴形式的端头可用于紧凑接口布局，线缆转弯与端头支撑需避免直接牵拉接点。

04

End transition layout端头转接与组合

The assembly organizes a transition or combined connection, with end directions, transition sections and fixing arranged around the equipment positions.组件以转接或组合形式组织连接，可围绕两端设备位置安排端头方向、过渡段与固定。

05

Signal path信号路径

Define signal type, pair allocation, shield continuity and termination as well as the connector shape. Establish transmission performance from the actual cable, length and system validation.在接口外形之外确认信号类型、线对分配、屏蔽连续性和端接方法；传输能力应按实际线材、长度及整机验证结果确定。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Can an RF cable be selected from connector photos?可以只凭连接器照片选择 RF 电缆吗？

No. Photos help identify a family, but exact interface, gender, polarity, impedance, frequency, cable type, length and environment still require drawings, part numbers or equipment data.不可以。照片只有助于识别系列，精确界面、性别、极性、阻抗、频率、线缆类型、长度与环境仍需图纸、型号或设备资料。

What is the difference between 50-ohm and 75-ohm coax?50Ω 与 75Ω 同轴线有什么区别？

They use different conductor and dielectric geometry and serve different systems. Fifty ohms is common in RF and test equipment; 75 ohms is common in video and broadcast. The cable and both interfaces must match the equipment impedance.它们使用不同导体与介质几何，服务于不同系统。50Ω 常见于 RF 与测试设备，75Ω 常见于视频与广播；线缆和两端界面都必须匹配设备阻抗。

Does a higher-frequency connector guarantee the complete cable rating?使用更高频率连接器就能保证整条电缆频率吗？

No. The usable assembly band is limited by the cable, both connectors, termination quality, transitions, length, routing and acceptance limits. The complete assembly must be reviewed and, where required, measured.不能。组件可用频段受线缆、两端连接器、端接质量、过渡、长度、路由与接收限值共同限制，需要审核完整组件，必要时进行测量。
