04 / FAQ

Before You Order

Is a turbo actuator harness the same as a complete actuator?

No. The harness provides an electrical connection, while the complete actuator and individual sealing or motor parts have different functions and ordering references.

What information should be sent for compatibility review?

Send the turbo or actuator model, equipment or engine reference, clear connector photos, the current part or sample and the required function.

Can the harness length or protection be changed?

Length, protection and identification can be reviewed for a harness project after the connector, pin definition, environment and materials are confirmed.
