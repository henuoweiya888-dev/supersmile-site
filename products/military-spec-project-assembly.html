Military-Spec Project Assembly

Overview

Military-spec project cable assemblies connect power, controls, sensors, communications and mission equipment in ground, naval, airborne and deployable systems. The term describes a controlled requirement set—not a universal quality grade. Exact component part numbers, approved sources, workmanship, environment, inspection and acceptance evidence must be defined for each program.

Translate the Mission Requirement into a Controlled Build

From exact component identity to field-support evidence

Begin with the platform, installation zone and released data. Then reconcile connector series, contacts, wire, shielding, routing, environment, workmanship, inspection and configuration records. Rugged appearance is never a substitute for a controlled requirement.

    Military-Spec Project Assembly Questions

    Does ‘military-spec’ mean the complete assembly is qualified?

    No. Qualification applies to the exact product, specification, class, source and scope supported by current evidence. A project assembly must separately satisfy its drawing, contract, workmanship, inspection and platform approval requirements.

    Can you build with MIL-DTL-38999 connectors?

    Projects using this family can be reviewed when the exact series, class, slash-sheet PIN, insert, contact, keying, backshell, mating half and approved-source requirement are supplied. The connector name alone is not enough.

    Can an existing sample be reverse engineered?

    A sample supports dimensions and interface study, but it may not reveal wire designation, derating, shield topology, hidden splices, sealing materials, approved sources or qualification history. Released engineering data and authority are still required.

    Do you test every assembly to MIL-STD-810?

    No. MIL-STD-810 contains methods and tailoring guidance. Environmental testing is performed only when the responsible authority defines the applicable method, procedure, severity, mounting, operating state, samples and acceptance criteria.

    What routine acceptance tests are available?

    The baseline can include configuration, visual, dimensional and continuity checks. Resistance, insulation, dielectric, shield, bonding, data, RF or functional checks are added when the assembly is compatible and the test method and limits are released.

    What information is needed for a capability review?

    Send the platform and zone, drawing revision, exact component identities and source restrictions, circuit and shield schedule, electrical loads and signals, route and support, environmental profiles, workmanship and inspection criteria, traceability, quantity and required data deliverables.
