CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Consider the sensing element, leads and instrument as one measurement chain, separating the visible connection form from measurement configuration defined for the project.把感温元件、引线与仪表作为同一测量链路考虑；以下区分可见连接形式与需在项目中定义的测量配置。

01

Gathered leads and separate ends集中引线与分散端头

A gathered lead section and holed metal ends form a multi-end connection layout for Pt100 project identification and port mapping. Define end functions, element supply scope and temperature zones separately.集中束线段与带孔金属端部组成多端头连接形式，可围绕Pt100测温项目安排引线标识与端口对应。端部用途、元件供货范围和温区需分别明确。

02

Holed metal end sections带孔金属端部

Mounting holes are visible in the metal ends. Match their sensor-mounting, electrical-entry or retention function to the equipment drawing.金属端部可见安装孔，具体用于感温安装、电气接入还是固定，应与设备图纸中的端部功能对应。

03

Lead IDs and sensing points线号与测点对应

Lead identification can follow the sensing-point and acquisition-channel map. Keep the lead group from each RTD element identifiable.引线上的标识位置可与测点及采集通道表对应，同一温阻元件的引线组需保持可识别。

CONFIGURATION结构与选配

Construction & customization options结构与定制配置

The table describes the construction and available configuration choices. Materials, dimensions, circuits and inspection requirements are agreed for each project.以下说明展示结构与可配置内容。具体材料、尺寸、电路和检验要求按项目确认。

RTD element and input specification温阻元件与输入规范Provide the Pt100 element specification, characteristic curve and instrument-input documents. Confirm element tolerance and supply scope in the project BOM; lead appearance does not establish them.提供Pt100元件规范、特性曲线与仪表输入资料；元件的容差等级和供货范围按项目BOM确认，不由连接线外观赋予。
Two-, three- or four-wire scheme二、三或四线制Define the actual wiring scheme and port view from the instrument-supported connection. Overall lead count does not establish the wiring of each RTD channel.按仪表支持的接法定义实际线制与端口视图；不要由外部引线总数直接推断每个温阻通道的接法。
Lead resistance and matching引线电阻与匹配Review the effects of lead length, size and termination resistance. Three-wire compensation depends on lead-resistance matching; four-wire connections define excitation and voltage-sensing paths separately.确认线长、截面及端接电阻对测量的影响。三线补偿需考虑引线电阻匹配，四线接法分别定义激励和电压测量路径。
Sensing points, channels and map测点、通道与连接表Record element positions, lead IDs, plug or end mapping and acquisition channels. Distinguish multiple leads from one element from independent channels at different sensing points.逐路记录感温元件位置、引线编号、插头或端头对应及采集通道，分清同一元件的多根引线与不同测点的独立通道。
Holed ends and mounting contact带孔端部与安装接触Use drawings to define metal-end hole dimensions, function and retention. Establish whether the sensing element is at that position, defining mechanical mounting and electrical contacts separately.用图纸确认金属端部的孔径、功能及固定方式，明确感温元件是否位于该部位；机械安装与电气接点分别定义。
Lead protection and temperature zones引线保护与温区Select insulation, protection and retention for sensing, transition and instrument areas. Review heat-source clearance, exposure and shielding; element range does not establish the entire lead temperature capability.按测点、过渡段和仪表区分别选择引线绝缘、保护与固定，确认热源距离、介质暴露和屏蔽安排，不把元件量程当作整条线的耐温。
Complete measurement-chain acceptance整条测量链路验收Agree wiring, resistance and installation checks, plus any temperature-comparison or calibration scope. Verify measurement accuracy across the element, leads, instrument and installation together.约定接线、电阻与安装检查，以及所需温度比对或校准范围；测量精度由元件、引线、仪表和安装共同验证。
APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These directions cover temperature sensing points, thermal zones and instruments. Define sensor type, temperature exposure and measurement acceptance for the specific equipment project.以下为围绕温度测点、热区与仪表的连接方向。传感器类型、使用温区及测量验收按具体设备项目确认。

Peripheral zones of thermal equipment热工设备外围温区

For RTD measurement around thermal equipment, define sensing position, element exposure and lead route. The Pt100 element, mounting contact and instrument wiring scheme all affect the result; select peripheral leads for their own temperature zone rather than the furnace interior.热工设备外围采用温阻测量时，先确定测点位置、元件温区和引线路径。Pt100元件、安装接触与仪表线制共同影响测量结果，外围引线不能按炉内温度直接选型。

Feedback connections for controlled chambers温控腔体反馈连接

For RTD feedback from controlled chambers, choose two-, three- or four-wire connections to match the acquisition module and distinguish internal from external lead conditions. Changes to lengths, connectors or wiring require a new review of lead resistance and system acceptance.温控腔体的温阻反馈需按采集模块选择二、三或四线连接，并区分腔内与腔外引线条件。更改线长、接头或接线方式后，重新评估引线电阻与系统验收要求。

Contact sensing on equipment surfaces设备表面接触测温

For surface sensing points, define the element position, mounting-hole purpose and contact method before arranging lead support. The outline of a metal end with a hole does not establish whether it mounts a sensor or serves as an electrical termination.表面测点需明确感温元件所在位置、安装孔用途和接触方式，再安排引线的应力支撑。带孔金属端部的外形不能单独说明它是感温安装件还是电气端接件。

RTD acquisition and recording channels温阻采集与记录通道

At acquisition instruments, align RTD type, wiring scheme and channel records. Three-wire arrangements require attention to lead-resistance matching; four-wire arrangements distinguish excitation and sensing ends. Use the instrument documents to define the actual connections.温阻引线接入采集仪器时，需要让传感器型号、线制和通道表保持对应。三线方案关注引线电阻匹配，四线方案区分激励与测量端，具体接法以仪器资料为准。

Appliance temperature control and service interfaces家电温控与检修接口

For appliance projects using RTDs, route sensing leads and service interfaces around heating or cooling modules. Define whether the sensing element is included with the lead assembly, then confirm thermal-zone protection, the connection map and service lengths.采用温阻传感器的家电项目，可围绕加热或冷却模块安排测温引线及检修接口。先区分感温元件与连接线的供货范围，再确认热区保护、连接表和维护长度。

Multipoint temperature feedback in laboratory equipment实验装置多点温度反馈

For multipoint RTD feedback in laboratory equipment, record each sensing point, element and acquisition channel. Make lead IDs, branch lengths and removable interfaces practical for servicing, with accuracy and calibration defined for the complete measurement chain.实验装置的多点温阻反馈需要逐路记录测点、元件和采集通道。引线编号、分支长度及可拆位置应便于模块维护，整体精度与校准由完整测量链路的验收要求定义。

Leads and installation structure引线与安装结构

Configure lengths, conductor size, branches and ends around sensing and module positions. Confirm the function, dimensions and protection of holed metal sections in drawings.按测点与模块位置配置长度、截面、分支和端头；带孔金属部位的用途、安装尺寸与保护方式用图纸确认。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Are two-, three- and four-wire RTD connections interchangeable?二、三、四线温阻接法能直接互换吗？

Match the instrument excitation and sensing scheme. Two-wire readings include lead effects, three-wire arrangements depend on matching, and four-wire arrangements separate excitation and sensing. Validate the complete chain after changing the connection.需与仪表的激励和测量接法匹配。二线读数包含引线影响；三线关注引线匹配；四线分开激励与测量，改接后仍需按整条测量链路验证。

Is the ring-shaped metal end the sensing element?环形金属端部是否就是感温元件？

The photograph does not establish the internal element position. Use drawings to distinguish sensor mounting, electrical ends and ordinary retention, and define whether the Pt100 element is supplied with the harness.照片不能说明内部元件位置。需用图纸区分感温安装部位、电气端头和普通固定件，并明确Pt100元件是否随线束一同提供。

Can accuracy remain unchanged after altering lead length?更改引线长度后，精度还能直接沿用吗？

Review lead and termination resistance, wiring scheme, instrument compensation and installation again. Define dimensional approval separately from temperature calibration; element grade alone does not establish complete-chain accuracy.需要重新评估引线及端接电阻、线制、仪表补偿和安装条件。组件尺寸确认与温度校准分开约定，不能把原元件等级直接当作整条测量链路精度。

Start with sensing points and instrument inputs从测点与仪表输入开始

Share sensor and instrument models, sensing temperature zones, connection maps, mounting and target lengths. For RTDs, include the wiring scheme; for thermocouples, include type, materials and reference-junction requirements.提供传感器及仪表型号、测点温区、连接表、安装方式和目标长度；温阻补充线制，热电偶补充分度、材料与参考接点要求。
