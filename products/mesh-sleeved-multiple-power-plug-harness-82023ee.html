APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Robotics and repeated-motion equipment机器人与重复运动设备

Flexible protective braid organizes cables around moving arms and tooling while branch, bend and attachment points remain matched to the motion path. Separate bundle management, abrasion, heat protection and conductive shielding goals.柔性防护编织整理运动臂与工具周围电缆，分支、弯曲和固定点仍需匹配运动路径。选配时，先分清束线、耐磨、热防护与导电屏蔽目标。

Machine tools and industrial equipment机床与工业设备

Abrasion, chips, oil and service contact drive braid, conduit and transition choices around cabinets, spindles, sensors and auxiliary systems. Set segmented coverage around abrasion zones, breakouts and service clearance.机柜、主轴、传感器与辅助系统周围的磨损、切屑、油污和检修接触决定编织、波纹管与过渡结构。选配时，分段覆盖围绕摩擦区、分支出口和维修空间布置。

Connections near engine heat sources发动机附件热区连接

Near heating elements or engine auxiliaries, review the temperature limits of wires, braided covering, contacts and fixing parts separately. Record measurement locations and clearance from heat sources.靠近加热元件或发动机附件时，需要分别核对导线、编织护层、端子及固定件的使用温度；也要保留测温位置与热源距离。

Instrumentation and noise-sensitive systems仪器与噪声敏感系统

Conductive braid and controlled terminations can support shielding for measurement, sensor and communication circuits where the grounding scheme is defined. Use bending and temperature conditions to select braid and support.在接地方案明确时，导电编织与受控端接可支持测量、传感与通信回路的屏蔽。选配时，弯曲与温度条件用于选择编织材料和支撑结构。

Outdoor, marine and process equipment户外、船舶与工艺设备

Moisture, salt, chemicals and sunlight require braid material and underlying cable jackets that are compatible with the complete exposure, not just an attractive outer weave. Separate bundle management, abrasion, heat protection and conductive shielding goals.湿气、盐分、化学品与日照要求编织材料和内部电缆护套共同适配完整环境，而不仅是外层织物看起来耐用。选配时，先分清束线、耐磨、热防护与导电屏蔽目标。

Commercial equipment and visible harnesses商用设备与可见线束

Neat abrasion-resistant braid supports organized, serviceable visible cable runs in kiosks, appliances, packaging equipment and production fixtures. Set segmented coverage around abrasion zones, breakouts and service clearance.整洁耐磨编织适用于售卖设备、电器、包装设备与生产工装中可见且需要检修的布线路径。选配时，分段覆盖围绕摩擦区、分支出口和维修空间布置。

Select braid from the real protection goal从真实防护目标选择编织

The project first separates abrasion, bundle management, appearance, heat exposure and electrical shielding. Material and construction are then chosen for the required functions instead of treating every braid as equivalent.项目首先区分耐磨、束线管理、外观、热暴露与电气屏蔽，再针对所需功能选择材料和结构，避免把所有编织视为同一种产品。

Define shielding coverage and termination定义屏蔽覆盖与端接

For EMI projects, braid coverage, overlap, branch continuity, connector backshell, drain path and grounding location are recorded. Shielding performance requires a defined test method and complete installed configuration.EMI项目记录编织覆盖、搭接、分支连续、接口尾壳、排流路径与接地点。屏蔽性能需要明确测试方法和完整装机配置。

Manage branches and moving transitions处理分支与运动过渡

Braid size, branch breakout, overlap, end fixation and bend path are coordinated so the covering follows movement without bunching, opening or loading the connector exit.协调编织规格、分支引出、搭接、端部固定与弯曲路径，使防护层随运动变化而不堆积、张开或拉扯接口出线。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does every braided harness provide EMI shielding?所有编织线束都能提供EMI屏蔽吗？

No. PET, nylon and other textile braids mainly provide organization and mechanical protection. EMI shielding requires conductive material, defined coverage, termination, grounding and an agreed validation method.不能。PET、尼龙等纺织编织主要提供束线与机械防护。EMI屏蔽需要导电材料、明确覆盖、端接、接地和约定验证方法。

What information is needed for a quotation?报价需要提供哪些资料？

State the protection goal, braid material if mandatory, minimum and maximum bundle diameters, branches, temperature, chemicals, movement, termination, colour, quantity and required tests.请说明防护目标、指定编织材料、最小与最大束径、分支、温度、化学、运动、端接、颜色、数量与所需测试。

Can braid be added to an existing harness?可以给现有线束增加编织吗？

Often yes, when connector dimensions, removable parts, branch geometry and installation access allow the selected braid to pass and terminate correctly. A representative sample should be reviewed.通常可以，但接口尺寸、可拆部件、分支几何与安装空间必须允许所选编织正确通过并端接，建议审核代表性样品。
