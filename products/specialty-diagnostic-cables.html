03 / Scope

Brand and EV Boundary

Brand names identify compatibility targets and do not imply authorization. EV-related products in this catalogue are low-voltage diagnostic or service connections, not high-voltage traction harnesses.

04 / FAQ

Before You Order

Does a brand name mean the cable is an official product?

No. Brand and model references describe an intended compatibility target. They do not imply endorsement, affiliation or authorization.

Are the EV cables in this series high-voltage harnesses?

No. The current EV-related records are low-voltage diagnostic, gateway or service-tool connections. High-voltage harness capability is not claimed here.

Why are model year and generation important?

Gateway architecture, connector versions, pin routes and tool support may change. Exact generation information reduces the risk of a mechanically fitting but functionally incorrect cable.
