Configuration & Project Review

01 Micro-D configuration

Confirm contact count, plug/socket, shell finish, jackscrew hardware and mating drawing.

02 Wire termination type

Separate insulated wire-lead versions from solder cups, PCB tails and short solid-wire terminations.

03 Literal part identity

Check the printed MIL code and manufacturer cross-reference; do not generate unlisted combinations from a coding matrix.

04 Wire and length

Specify wire standard, color, lead length, circuit map and the required free-end preparation.

05 Mechanical support

Review backshell, potting, shield bond and bend clearance for the installed assembly.

06 Evidence at delivery

Agree traceability and inspection records; a MIL reference does not itself prove current QPL or finished-assembly qualification.
