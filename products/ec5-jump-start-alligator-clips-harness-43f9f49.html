PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Can battery cable size be selected from starter current alone?电池线径只按起动电流选择可以吗？

No. Loop length, allowable voltage drop, peak duration and repetition, continuous charging or auxiliary load, temperature, bundling, conductor construction, termination resistance and protection all affect selection.不可以。回路长度、允许压降、峰值持续时间及重复频率、持续充电或辅助负载、温度、成束、导体结构、端接电阻与保护都会影响选择。

Can you build from an existing battery cable sample?可以依据现有电池线样品开发吗？

Yes, a sample helps confirm length, routing and terminal geometry, but conductor area, material grade, electrical duty, joint specification and validation limits still require customer data or engineering confirmation.可以，样品有助于确认长度、走线与端子几何，但导体截面积、材料牌号、电气工况、接点规范与验证限值仍需客户资料或工程确认。

Is a soldered lug better than a crimped lug?焊接线鼻一定比压接线鼻好吗？

Not inherently. A properly engineered crimp provides a controlled mechanical and electrical interface. Solder can wick into strands and create a stiff transition; either process must be specified and validated for the application.不一定。正确工程化的压接能够形成受控机械与电气界面。焊锡可能沿线股渗入并形成硬过渡；无论哪种工艺都必须针对应用明确并验证。
