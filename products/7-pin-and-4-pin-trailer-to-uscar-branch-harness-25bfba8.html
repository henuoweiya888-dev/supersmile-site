Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Trailer lighting links拖车灯光连接

Separate lighting functions by regional format and tow-vehicle circuits. Confirm regional connector format with the tow-vehicle configuration.按地区制式和牵引车回路区分灯光功能。选配时，目标地区接口制式和牵引车配置一起确认。

Brake and axle branches制动与车轴分支

Define braking, sensors and motion clearance separately. Plan lighting, braking, charging and auxiliary circuits separately.制动、传感器及运动空间分别定义。选配时，灯光、制动、充电及辅助回路分别规划。

Auxiliary power and body equipment辅助电源与上装

Give charging, liftgate and equipment loads separate protection budgets. Select conductors from circuit current, body length and return paths.充电、尾板或设备负载需要各自保护预算。选配时，回路电流、车体长度和回路线共同选线。

Doors and sliding structures车门与滑动结构

Provide slack and protection through opening, closing and sliding travel. Match external connectors, fixing and abrasion protection to frame motion.余量和防磨按打开、关闭与滑动全过程安排。选配时，外部接头、固定与耐磨配合车架运动。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is a trailer harness a universal product?拖车线束是通用产品吗？

No. It is a broad family of tow-interface, chassis, lighting, brake, axle, auxiliary and equipment harnesses. Market, connector, voltage, trailer geometry, loads and installed options determine the released version.不是。它是由牵引接口、底盘、灯光、制动、车轴、辅助与设备线束组成的广泛家族，市场、连接器、电压、拖车几何、负载与安装选项共同决定放行版本。

Can two seven-pin trailer plugs use the same pinout?两个七针拖车插头一定使用相同针脚定义吗？

Not by assumption. Seven-position interfaces can belong to different standards and vehicle classes. Mating family, viewing direction, pin assignment, voltage, current and circuit function must be verified.不能直接假设。七位接口可能属于不同标准与车辆级别，必须核对对插家族、视图方向、针脚定义、电压、电流与回路功能。

Does a sealed connector make the complete harness waterproof?使用密封连接器就能让整根线束防水吗？

No. Housing, terminal, seal, cavity plug, mating part, cable exit, splice construction, covering, drainage, routing and assembly quality must work together under a defined exposure and test condition.不能。壳体、端子、线封、堵头、对插件、出线、接续结构、覆盖层、排水、走线与装配质量必须在规定暴露和测试条件下共同工作。
