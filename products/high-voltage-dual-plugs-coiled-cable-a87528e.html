Conductor, insulation and material choices导体、绝缘与选材要点

01

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

Coiled or spiral form卷绕或螺旋形态

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Power equipment and larger conductor connections电力设备与大截面连接

Review system voltage, load, temperature rise and accessories together. Relate installation clearance and test boundaries to the complete system. Record operating voltage, transient peaks and applicable specifications separately.电力连接需共同评审系统电压、负载、温升与附件，安装空间和试验边界也应对应完整系统。选配时，分别记录工作电压、瞬态峰值与适用规范。

Equipment power-distribution branches设备电源分配支路

Select power branches from actual loads, circuit protection and route length. Include termination, retention and service positions in the wiring definition. Review conductor area for load, temperature rise and installation derating.供电分支以实际负载、回路保护和线长为选配依据，端头、固定和检修位置也需列入布线定义。选配时，导体截面按负载、温升和安装降额审核。

Layer construction of multicore cables多芯线缆的层结构

Document conductors, fillers, shields and jacket separately. Set overall dimensions and breakout length for the assembly space. Define the material and dimensions of insulation, shield and jacket layers.多芯结构需分别记录芯线、填充、屏蔽和护套，外形尺寸与端接展开长度应满足总装空间。选配时，明确绝缘、屏蔽和护套各层材料及尺寸。

Terminal wiring inside control cabinets控制柜内的端子配线

Coordinate terminal positions, duct exits and service access within the cabinet. Wire labels should correspond to the circuit table and equipment ports. Agree boundaries for dielectric, partial-discharge or other project-specific tests.柜内连接需协调端子位置、线槽出口和可维护空间，线号应能对应回路表与设备端口。选配时，约定耐压、局放或其他项目要求的试验边界。

Vehicle battery and power-system projects车辆电池与动力系统项目

Define system voltage, load, interlock and accessory requirements in vehicle power-system projects before selecting cable construction and interface validation. Record operating voltage, transient peaks and applicable specifications separately.车辆动力系统连接需先界定系统电压、负载、互锁与附件要求，再确定线缆结构及接口验证范围。选配时，分别记录工作电压、瞬态峰值与适用规范。

Cable entry and exit positions at power cabinets电力柜体的进出线位置

Coordinate separation clearance, cable retention and service routes at power cabinets. Select cables and accessories for the specific system conditions. Review conductor area for load, temperature rise and installation derating.电力柜的进出线需要协调隔离间距、固定和维护路径，线缆与附件的选择应依据具体系统条件。选配时，导体截面按负载、温升和安装降额审核。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does a large diameter establish a high-voltage rating?粗直径能证明电缆高压额定吗？

Large diameter does not establish high-voltage rating. Record operating voltage, transient peaks and applicable specifications separately.粗直径不能证明高压额定。分别记录工作电压、瞬态峰值与适用规范。

Is test voltage the same as continuous operating voltage?测试电压就是持续工作电压吗？

Record test voltage separately from working voltage. Review conductor area for load, temperature rise and installation derating.测试电压与工作电压分开记录。导体截面按负载、温升和安装降额审核。

Can raw-cable inspection replace finished-assembly validation?原缆检验能替代端接后组件验证吗？

Raw-cable evidence does not replace terminated-assembly validation. Define the material and dimensions of insulation, shield and jacket layers.裸电缆证据不能替代端接后组件验证。明确绝缘、屏蔽和护套各层材料及尺寸。
