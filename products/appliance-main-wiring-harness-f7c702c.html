Develop from an appliance or reference sample依据设备或参考样品开发

An appliance or existing loom can establish fit, route and visible interfaces. Hidden circuits, current, temperature rating, safety parts and acceptance requirements still need customer confirmation before replication.设备实物或现有线束可以帮助确认装配、路径和可见接口，但隐藏回路、电流、温度等级、安全件和验收要求仍需客户确认后才能复制。

Separate power, heat and sensitive control circuits区分动力、热区与敏感控制回路

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is an appliance harness a fixed product model?电器设备线束是固定型号吗？

No. It is a broad family covering different appliances, loads, temperature zones, connectors, branches and assembly methods. The final harness is defined by the actual appliance and approved documents.不是。它覆盖不同电器、负载、温区、接口、分支与装配方式，最终结构由真实设备和批准文件确定。

What information is needed for a quotation?报价需要提供哪些资料？

Provide the circuit diagram or pin table, appliance layout, component and mating-interface data, current and temperature information, branch dimensions, assembly restrictions, quantity and required inspections. A physical sample can supplement incomplete drawings.建议提供电路图或针脚表、设备布局、器件与对插资料、电流和温度信息、分支尺寸、装配限制、数量与检验要求。图纸不完整时可补充实物样品。

Can you reproduce an existing appliance harness from a sample?可以按现有电器线束样品复制吗？

A sample can define visible materials, dimensions and interfaces, but hidden circuits, conductor ratings, safety components and acceptance criteria still require confirmation before production.样品可以用于确认可见材料、尺寸和接口，但隐藏回路、导体额定、安全件与验收标准仍需在生产前确认。
