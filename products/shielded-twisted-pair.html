ENGINEERING OVERVIEW工程概览

Protect the balanced pair from conductor geometry to enclosure bond从线对几何到机壳搭接，保护完整平衡通道

A shielded twisted-pair cable is a balanced transmission structure surrounded by one or more screens. Pair geometry controls differential performance; the screen manages coupling and common-mode current only when its connection to connectors and enclosures is intentional. Cable category, protocol name or foil coverage alone does not define a completed channel.

屏蔽双绞线是由一对或多对平衡线对及屏蔽层构成的传输结构。线对几何决定差分性能；屏蔽只有在与连接器和机壳形成明确连接时，才能管理耦合与共模电流。线缆类别、协议名称或铝箔覆盖率都不能单独定义完整通道。

01

SYSTEM DEFINITION

系统定义

Preserve the balanced pair保持平衡线对

Balanced geometry begins with the pair

平衡性能从线对几何开始

Conductor diameter, insulation, center spacing, twist lay and pair uniformity influence impedance, attenuation and balance. Untwisting too far at a connector or changing pair assignment creates a local discontinuity even when every pin is electrically continuous. Industrial Ethernet, single-pair Ethernet, CAN, RS-485, fieldbus and low-level sensor circuits have different physical-layer requirements; the exact protocol and equipment interface must be named.

导体直径、绝缘、中心距、绞距与线对一致性会影响阻抗、衰减和平衡。连接器处解绞过长或更换线对分配，即使每个针脚都导通，也会造成局部不连续。工业以太网、单对以太网、CAN、RS-485、现场总线和低电平传感器具有不同物理层要求，必须明确准确协议与设备接口。

Screen notation describes different constructions

屏蔽标识对应不同结构

U/FTP, F/UTP, S/FTP and related notations distinguish overall and individual-pair screening. Foil offers broad coverage with low diameter; braid improves mechanical robustness and low-frequency current capacity; combined constructions serve different EMC targets. Coverage percentage alone is not enough. Transfer impedance, coupling attenuation, bend performance, drain wire and connector termination method may be important project inputs.

U/FTP、F/UTP、S/FTP 等标识用于区分总屏蔽与单对屏蔽。铝箔可在较小外径下提供较高覆盖，编织层具有更好的机械稳定性与低频电流能力，复合结构则服务不同 EMC 目标。仅有覆盖率仍不够，转移阻抗、耦合衰减、弯曲性能、排流线与连接器端接方式都可能成为项目输入。

02

CONSTRUCTION + INSTALLATION

结构与安装

Complete the shield path闭合屏蔽路径

Connector and enclosure bonding complete the shield path

连接器与机壳搭接闭合屏蔽路径

A screen stopped on a long pigtail behaves differently from a low-impedance 360-degree termination. Shielded RJ45, M12, D-sub, circular and custom interfaces each require compatible backshells, clamps or glands. Whether the screen bonds at one end, both ends or through a controlled network depends on frequency, safety, grounding architecture and equipment design. The cable assembly should preserve the customer’s EMC strategy rather than invent one at the workbench.

屏蔽层通过长引线接地，与低阻抗 360° 环接具有不同高频表现。屏蔽 RJ45、M12、D-sub、圆形与定制接口分别需要兼容后壳、夹具或格兰。屏蔽在单端、双端还是通过受控网络连接，取决于频率、安全、接地架构与设备设计。线缆组件应保持客户的 EMC 策略，而不是在工位上临时决定。

Routing preserves separation and bend geometry

布线路径保持间距与弯曲几何

Maintain separation from motor leads, contactors, drives and other noise sources where the equipment design requires it. Avoid crushing, tight ties, sharp bends and uncontrolled parallel runs. In a moving route, confirm that foil, braid, pair lay and jacket are designed for the motion duty. Cable tray, cabinet entry, shield gland and service loop should be shown on the installation drawing so laboratory performance is not lost during assembly.

按设备设计要求，与电机线、接触器、变频器和其他噪声源保持间距，避免挤压、过紧扎带、急弯与无控制的长距离平行敷设。运动路径还需确认铝箔、编织、绞距与护套是否适合该运动工况。桥架、柜体入口、屏蔽格兰与维修余量应在安装图中表示，避免实验室性能在装配中丢失。

03

EVIDENCE + RELEASE

证据与放行

Prove the configured channel证明实际配置通道

Validate the channel, not only continuity

验证完整通道，而不只是导通

Continuity and shorts are necessary production checks but cannot prove a high-speed balanced link. Depending on the specified system, evidence may include conductor resistance, resistance unbalance, impedance, insertion loss, return loss, near- and far-end crosstalk, shield continuity or connector shielding effectiveness. IEC 61156 contains cable families for balanced digital communications, while connector-screen tests such as IEC 60512-23-3 address a different boundary. Use the method required by the project.

导通与短路检查是必要生产项目，却不能证明高速平衡链路。依据系统要求，证据可能包括导体电阻、电阻不平衡、阻抗、插入损耗、回波损耗、近端与远端串扰、屏蔽连续性或连接器屏蔽效能。IEC 61156 覆盖平衡数字通信电缆系列，而 IEC 60512-23-3 等连接器屏蔽试验针对不同边界，应采用项目规定的方法。
