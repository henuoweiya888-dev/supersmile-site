Transmission Harness变速箱线束

Automatic Transmission Ribbon Cable自动变速箱扁平排线线束

Automatic Transmission Ribbon Cable can be configured around equipment interfaces and routing. Transmission harnesses carry power, commands and feedback between a transmission control unit, mechatronic assembly, solenoids, speed or position sensors, range selector and the vehicle-side loom. The category includes external gearbox leads, sealed through-case interfaces and, where specified, oil-wet internal branches.自动变速箱扁平排线线束可围绕设备接口与安装路径进行配置。变速箱线束在变速箱控制单元、机电液模块、电磁阀、转速或位置传感器、挡位选择器与整车线束之间传输电源、指令和反馈。本小类包括箱体外部引线、穿壳密封接口，以及按项目要求制作的油浸式内部支路。

CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the automatic transmission ribbon cable form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以自动变速箱扁平排线线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Conductor layout and orientation排布与安装方向

Confirm conductor spacing, sequence, bend direction and insertion face to prevent reversed mapping. Plan fixing and abrasion protection around the available installation space.确认导体间距、排列顺序、弯折方向和插入面，避免翻转造成针位错误。扁平结构的固定与防磨需要结合设备空间安排。

02

Conductor and insulation selection导体与绝缘选配

Select conductors and insulation from circuit loads, diameter limits, bending and exposure. A material name does not replace ratings or validation results.从电路负载、外径限制、弯曲方式与环境条件选择导体和绝缘体系；材料名称不应直接代替额定参数或验证结果。

03

Interface and function review接口与功能确认

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is an external transmission lead the same as an internal mechatronic harness?箱外变速箱引线等同于箱内机电液线束吗？

No. They have different installation, fluid exposure, connector and cleanliness requirements. Define the breakpoints and whether a through-case interface belongs to the assembly.不等同。安装位置、油液暴露、连接器和洁净度要求都可能不同。必须明确断开点，以及穿壳接口是否属于供货范围。

Can a matching gearbox plug prove interchangeability?变速箱插头能对插就能互换吗？

No. Compare transmission and controller references, cavity view, circuit assignment, terminal system, sealing parts and software variant. A mating shell does not validate shifting behavior.不能。还要比较变速箱与控制器料号、孔位视图、回路分配、端子体系、密封件与软件变型。能插上不等于换挡行为正确。

Does continuity testing prove the feedthrough will not leak?导通测试能证明穿壳接口不漏油吗？

No. Continuity tests electrical mapping. Fluid containment needs the correct approved seals and installation, followed by the project-specific leak or pressure test.不能。导通只检查电气映射。油液密封需要正确的批准密封件和安装，并按项目规定进行渗漏或压力试验。
