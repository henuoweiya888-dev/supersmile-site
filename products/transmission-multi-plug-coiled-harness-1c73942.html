CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the transmission multi-plug coiled harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以变速箱多组插头盘绕线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

02

Coiled or spiral form卷绕或螺旋形态

The cable is arranged in a coiled or spiral form, with straight tails and ends placed for storage or connection positions.线缆以卷绕或螺旋形态排列，直线尾段与端头可以结合收纳或连接位置安排。

03

Multiple connector positions多端头连接位置

Multiple ends provide separate connection positions. Layout and spacing can follow the module arrangement.多个端头提供分别接入的位置，线束展开方向与各端头间距可围绕模块布局定义。

04

Current path and end termination载流与端部连接

Select conductors and terminations from continuous and peak loads, installed length and temperature rise. Confirm lug bore, contact surface, insulation coverage and fixing as a set.依据持续与峰值负载、安装长度和温升要求选择导体与端接；端子孔径、接触面、绝缘覆盖及固定方式需要成套确认。

Interface and function review接口与功能确认

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is an external transmission lead the same as an internal mechatronic harness?箱外变速箱引线等同于箱内机电液线束吗？

No. They have different installation, fluid exposure, connector and cleanliness requirements. Define the breakpoints and whether a through-case interface belongs to the assembly.不等同。安装位置、油液暴露、连接器和洁净度要求都可能不同。必须明确断开点，以及穿壳接口是否属于供货范围。

Can a matching gearbox plug prove interchangeability?变速箱插头能对插就能互换吗？

No. Compare transmission and controller references, cavity view, circuit assignment, terminal system, sealing parts and software variant. A mating shell does not validate shifting behavior.不能。还要比较变速箱与控制器料号、孔位视图、回路分配、端子体系、密封件与软件变型。能插上不等于换挡行为正确。

Does continuity testing prove the feedthrough will not leak?导通测试能证明穿壳接口不漏油吗？

No. Continuity tests electrical mapping. Fluid containment needs the correct approved seals and installation, followed by the project-specific leak or pressure test.不能。导通只检查电气映射。油液密封需要正确的批准密封件和安装，并按项目规定进行渗漏或压力试验。
