CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the fuel pump 4-wire plug pigtail form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以燃油泵四线插头尾线的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

02

Pigtail connection form尾线连接形式

A lead section extends behind the connector, allowing length, further termination and support to follow the equipment connection.插接端后保留引线段，便于按设备接线位置确定长度、后续端接与支撑。

03

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

04

Current path and end termination载流与端部连接

Select conductors and terminations from continuous and peak loads, installed length and temperature rise. Confirm lug bore, contact surface, insulation coverage and fixing as a set.依据持续与峰值负载、安装长度和温升要求选择导体与端接；端子孔径、接触面、绝缘覆盖及固定方式需要成套确认。

05

Conductor and insulation selection导体与绝缘选配

Select conductors and insulation from circuit loads, diameter limits, bending and exposure. A material name does not replace ratings or validation results.从电路负载、外径限制、弯曲方式与环境条件选择导体和绝缘体系；材料名称不应直接代替额定参数或验证结果。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

01

Fuel-pump power燃油泵供电

Select conductors/protection from drive load and connection location. Distinguish fuel pumps, injectors and sensors by exact model.驱动负载与连接位置确定导体和保护。选配时，燃油泵、喷油器与传感器按准确型号区分。

02

Injector branches喷油器分支

Define circuits from cylinder order and controller drive method. Wire drive, feedback, power and reference ground separately.缸序与控制器驱动方式共同定义回路。选配时，驱动、反馈、供电与参考地分开接线。

Pressure and level signals压力与液位信号

Separate low-level feedback from pump-current paths. Define exposure locations as splash, vapor or immersion.低电平反馈与泵电流路径分开处理。选配时，介质接触位置明确为飞溅、蒸气或浸泡。

04

Fuel-level connections燃油液位连接

Do not arbitrarily combine sensor reference ground with pump power. Match sealing, temperature and safety requirements to the installation zone.传感器参考地与泵供电不可随意合并。选配时，密封、温度与安全要求对应实际安装区。

Common-rail control branches共轨控制分支

Align controller revision, cylinder order and drive definitions. Distinguish fuel pumps, injectors and sensors by exact model.控制器版本、缸序和驱动定义应相互对应。选配时，燃油泵、喷油器与传感器按准确型号区分。

Pump-end service pigtails维修泵端尾线

Review connector, sealing and local media exposure together. Wire drive, feedback, power and reference ground separately.接头、密封和局部介质接触需共同检查。选配时，驱动、反馈、供电与参考地分开接线。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is a fuel-system harness the same as a fuel line?燃油系统线束等同于燃油管吗？

No. The harness carries electrical power and signals to pumps, injectors, valves and sensors. Hoses, rails and pipes carry fuel and require separate fluid-system engineering and verification.不等同。线束向泵、喷油器、阀和传感器输送电源与信号；软管、油轨和管路输送燃油，需另行开展流体系统设计与验证。

Can a connector that fits an injector be used directly?喷油器插头能插上就可以直接用吗？

No. Confirm the injector and controller part numbers, cavity view, driver strategy, circuit assignment, terminal system and installed environment. A shared shell shape does not prove electrical compatibility.不能。还需确认喷油器与控制器料号、孔位视图、驱动策略、回路分配、端子体系和安装环境。外壳形状相同不证明电气兼容。

Does a sealed connector make the whole harness fuel-resistant?密封接头能让整根线束耐燃油吗？

No. Fuel exposure must be defined by liquid, vapor or mist, duration and temperature. Wire insulation, seals, protection and the mated connector all need suitable material evidence and an agreed test if a resistance claim is required.不能。应明确是液体、蒸气还是油雾，以及接触时长和温度。若需耐受声明，线材绝缘、密封、防护及对插连接器均需对应材料证据和约定试验。
