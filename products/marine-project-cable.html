ENGINEERING OVERVIEW工程概览

Map every route from dry accommodation to exposed machinery spaces从干燥舱室到暴露机械区，逐段定义电缆路径

IEC 60092-350 provides general construction and test requirements for shipboard power, control and instrumentation cables with specified extruded insulation, primarily for fixed installations including vibration. It does not cover every data, coaxial, optical or frequently flexed marine cable. The vessel, classification society, flag, equipment specification and exact route remain project inputs.

IEC 60092-350 为规定挤包绝缘的船用动力、控制与仪表电缆提供通用结构和试验要求，主要面向包括振动在内的固定安装；它并不覆盖所有数据、同轴、光纤或频繁弯曲船用电缆。船舶、船级社、船旗、设备规范与准确路径仍是项目输入。

SYSTEM DEFINITION

系统定义

Map the vessel route绘制船舶路径

Classify the circuit and route zone

先划分回路与路径区域

Separate power, lighting, control, alarm, instrumentation, communication and special circuits. Then map accommodation, control room, engine room, machinery deck, exposed deck and submerged or splash zones. Temperature, vibration, oil, fuel, salt mist, water, UV, mechanical protection and fire exposure differ by location. One cable family rarely answers every route on a vessel or offshore unit.

应区分动力、照明、控制、报警、仪表、通信与特殊回路，再划分居住区、控制室、机舱、机械甲板、露天甲板以及浸水或飞溅区域。不同位置的温度、振动、油液、燃料、盐雾、水、紫外、机械防护与火灾暴露并不相同，一种线缆系列很少能覆盖整艘船或海上平台的所有路径。

Salt, oil and moisture reach the interfaces

盐、油与潮气最终都会到达接口

Jacket resistance matters, but ingress often begins at glands, connectors, splices, branch seals and damaged supports. Select materials against the actual fluid and temperature, then define drip loops, gland orientation, sealing stack, cable support and service access. Tinned copper, compatible metals and controlled shield termination may reduce corrosion risk, but material names do not replace an assembled-interface validation plan.

护套耐受性很重要，但进水常从格兰、连接器、接头、分支密封和受损支撑处开始。应依据真实介质与温度选材，并定义滴水弯、格兰方向、密封叠层、电缆支撑与维修空间。镀锡铜、相容金属和受控屏蔽端接可降低腐蚀风险，但材料名称不能代替组件界面验证。

02

CONSTRUCTION + INSTALLATION

结构与安装

Engineer exposed interfaces设计暴露接口

Engine-room heat and vibration require installed review

机舱热与振动需要按安装状态审核

Ambient heat, nearby exhaust or motors, current-generated heat and restricted ventilation can create different conductor temperatures. Vibration and machinery movement load clamps, glands and terminations even when the cable itself is fixed. Confirm support spacing, bend radius, expansion allowance, armour or mechanical protection, and separation from hot surfaces. A standard’s fixed-installation scope should not be presented as proof of frequent-flex life.

环境热、邻近排气或电机、电流自发热与受限通风会形成不同导体温度。即使线缆固定，振动与机械运动仍会对夹具、格兰和端接施加载荷。应确认支撑间距、弯曲半径、伸缩余量、铠装或机械防护，以及与热表面的隔离。固定安装标准不能被当作频繁弯曲寿命的证明。

03

EVIDENCE + RELEASE

证据与放行

Release project-specific evidence放行项目特定证据

Fire, smoke, halogen and EMC are separate project decisions

阻燃、烟密度、无卤与 EMC 应分别定义

Marine projects may specify flame propagation, fire resistance, low smoke, halogen gas, electromagnetic compatibility and circuit segregation. Each property has its own method and acceptance boundary. Instrumentation and communication circuits may need pair, screen and bonding controls in addition to fire performance. The cable schedule should connect every circuit and route to its exact standard, construction, approval evidence and installation note.

船舶项目可能规定火焰蔓延、耐火、低烟、卤酸气体、电磁兼容与回路隔离，每项性能都有独立方法和验收边界。仪表与通信回路除防火性能外，还可能需要线对、屏蔽与搭接控制。电缆清册应把每条回路和路径连接到准确标准、结构、认可证据与安装说明。
