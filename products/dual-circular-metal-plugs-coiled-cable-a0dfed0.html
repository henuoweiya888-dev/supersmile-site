CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the dual circular metal plugs coiled cable form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以双圆形金属插头弹簧线的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Circular connection end圆形连接端

The end uses a circular interface form, with connection positions planned around mating direction, retention and cable exit.端头具有圆形接口形式，可围绕对插方向、端部固定和线缆出口规划连接位置。

02

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

03

Coiled or spiral form卷绕或螺旋形态

The cable is arranged in a coiled or spiral form, with straight tails and ends placed for storage or connection positions.线缆以卷绕或螺旋形态排列，直线尾段与端头可以结合收纳或连接位置安排。

04

Circular mating interface圆形接口匹配

Match the series, keying, contact count, mating direction and locking method. Similar shell outlines do not establish compatible dimensions or contact assignments.接口选型需核对系列、键位、针数、插合方向与锁紧方式；相近外形不能替代对插尺寸和接点定义。

05

Current path and end termination载流与端部连接

Select conductors and terminations from continuous and peak loads, installed length and temperature rise. Confirm lug bore, contact surface, insulation coverage and fixing as a set.依据持续与峰值负载、安装长度和温升要求选择导体与端接；端子孔径、接触面、绝缘覆盖及固定方式需要成套确认。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does a soft feel establish continuous-motion capability?手感柔软能证明持续运动额定吗？

Soft feel does not establish continuous-motion ratings. Confirm fine-strand construction, gauge and supply model from material documents.手感柔软不等于持续运动额定。细股结构、线规与来源型号须由材料文件确认。

Are bending, carrier motion and torsion the same duty?弯折、拖链和扭转是同一工况吗？

Confirm bending, drag-chain and robot torsion duties separately. Select jacket material, hardness and diameter for installation and termination.弯折、拖链和机器人扭转分开确认。护套材质、硬度和外径按安装与端接选择。

Can fine-strand conductors use any crimp process?细股导体可以沿用任意压接工艺吗？

Match fine-strand conductors to terminals and crimp process. Define bending or torsional conditions and the corresponding cycle target.细股导体需匹配端子和压接工艺。明确弯折或扭转条件及对应循环目标。
