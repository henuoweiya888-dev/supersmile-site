APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Provide the series and full housing and mating-part numbers.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，提供系列名称及壳体、对插端完整料号。

Equipment power-distribution branches设备电源分配支路

Select power branches from actual loads, circuit protection and route length. Include termination, retention and service positions in the wiring definition. Match contact gender, material and plating to the specific contact documents.供电分支以实际负载、回路保护和线长为选配依据，端头、固定和检修位置也需列入布线定义。选配时，接点公母、材料与镀层按具体端子文件配套。

Equipment control-board connections设备控制板连接

Document pin-row views, mating direction and nearby wire support. Check access to the latch and contacts after assembly. Check polarization, latch and access rather than pitch alone.板端连接要给出针排视图、插合方向和近端线缆支撑；检查装配后是否仍能操作锁扣并查看接点。选配时，核对防误插、卡扣和操作空间，而不只看间距。

Housing and contact combinations壳体与接点配套

Define housings, contacts and locking parts as a matched series. Color and a similar outline can aid identification but cannot replace mating drawings. Review current and conductors against actual multi-circuit loading and installed temperature.壳体、接点与锁止件需按具体系列成套定义，外壳颜色或相近轮廓只能帮助识别，不能代替对插图纸。选配时，按实际多路负载和安装温度审核电流与导体。

Leads for small electronic modules小型电子模块的引线

Define contact mapping, fine-wire processing limits and enclosure clearance for compact modules. Locate support close to the cable ends. Provide the series and full housing and mating-part numbers.小型模块的接口定义需要覆盖针位、细线加工范围和壳内空间，靠近端头的支撑位置尤其重要。选配时，提供系列名称及壳体、对插端完整料号。

Equipment panels and circular interfaces设备面板与圆形接口

Start with the cutout, panel thickness, mounting direction and rear clearance. Coordinate contact arrangement and cable exit with the complete assembly view. Match contact gender, material and plating to the specific contact documents.面板连接以开孔、板厚、装配方向和后方净空为起点；接点排列与电缆出口还需要对应完整总装视图。选配时，接点公母、材料与镀层按具体端子文件配套。
