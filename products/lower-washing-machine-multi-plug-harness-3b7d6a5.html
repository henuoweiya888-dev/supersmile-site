CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the lower washing-machine multi-plug harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以洗衣机下部多插头线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

02

Multiple connector positions多端头连接位置

Multiple ends provide separate connection positions. Layout and spacing can follow the module arrangement.多个端头提供分别接入的位置，线束展开方向与各端头间距可围绕模块布局定义。

03

Current path and end termination载流与端部连接

Select conductors and terminations from continuous and peak loads, installed length and temperature rise. Confirm lug bore, contact surface, insulation coverage and fixing as a set.依据持续与峰值负载、安装长度和温升要求选择导体与端接；端子孔径、接触面、绝缘覆盖及固定方式需要成套确认。
