Configuration & Project Review

01 Electrical layer

Specify TTL UART, RS232, RS422 or RS485 before choosing an endpoint. A similar connector does not establish compatible signal levels.

02 Signals and supply

Define I/O voltage, VCC output or target supply, ground, handshake lines and pin mapping separately. Review the exact part datasheet.

03 Host and drivers

Identify the host OS, VCP or D2XX workflow, application protocol and any MPSSE library requirements. A UART cable is not a substitute for every SPI or JTAG adapter.

04 Target connection

Record connector keying, contact assignment, cable length, shield path, strain relief and any open-wire termination.

05 Electronic configuration

Confirm the genuine conversion device, programmed identity and serial configuration with the customer. Record any authorized EEPROM changes.

06 Active verification

Define enumeration, communication, loopback or target-function checks alongside continuity and visual inspection. Retain the released hardware and configuration revisions.
