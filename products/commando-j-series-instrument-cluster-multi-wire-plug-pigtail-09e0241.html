CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the commando/j series instrument cluster multi-wire plug pigtail form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以Commando／J系列仪表盘多线插头尾线的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

02

Pigtail connection form尾线连接形式

A lead section extends behind the connector, allowing length, further termination and support to follow the equipment connection.插接端后保留引线段，便于按设备接线位置确定长度、后续端接与支撑。

03

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

04

Current path and end termination载流与端部连接

Select conductors and terminations from continuous and peak loads, installed length and temperature rise. Confirm lug bore, contact surface, insulation coverage and fixing as a set.依据持续与峰值负载、安装长度和温升要求选择导体与端接；端子孔径、接触面、绝缘覆盖及固定方式需要成套确认。

05

Conductor and insulation selection导体与绝缘选配

Select conductors and insulation from circuit loads, diameter limits, bending and exposure. A material name does not replace ratings or validation results.从电路负载、外径限制、弯曲方式与环境条件选择导体和绝缘体系；材料名称不应直接代替额定参数或验证结果。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is a cluster pigtail the same as a complete dashboard harness?仪表盘短引线等同于整套仪表台线束吗？

No. A pigtail covers a defined cluster interface; a cross-car harness can include switches, HVAC, displays, junctions and body-controller branches. Specify the exact breakpoints and vehicle variant.不等同。短引线只覆盖指定仪表接口；横向总线束可能包含开关、空调、显示、配电与车身控制器分支。应明确断开点及车型变型。

Can matching cluster plugs be interchanged?仪表插头能对插，就可以互换吗？

No. Confirm cavity view, pin map, power domains, network protocol, cluster and controller references, and software configuration. Plug fit alone does not prove displayed information or warning behavior.不能。还需确认孔位视图、针脚表、供电域、网络协议、仪表与控制器料号以及软件配置。能插上不能证明显示或警示行为正确。

Can you reproduce an airbag or SRS branch from an old sample?可以按旧样复制安全气囊或 SRS 分支吗？

Not from a sample alone. SRS circuits are safety critical and require OEM-approved drawings, component traceability, controlled process and separately defined validation. We would first review whether that scope is appropriate for the project.不能只凭旧样。SRS 回路属于安全关键部件，需要整车方批准图纸、元件追溯、受控工艺和单独验证。我们会先审核项目范围是否适合承接。
