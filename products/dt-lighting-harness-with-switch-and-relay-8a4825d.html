CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the dt lighting harness with switch & relay form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以DT接头灯具开关继电器线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Relay connection section继电器连接部位

Ends or branches are organized around relay connection points. Routing can distinguish control-side and switched-circuit paths.端头或分支围绕继电器连接位置组织，线束布局可区分控制端与受控回路的走向。

02

Switch connection end开关连接端

Leads connect at switch or button locations, with routing arranged for panel mounting, operation and rear wiring space.引线接向开关或按钮连接位置，布置时可兼顾面板安装、操作方向与背部接线空间。

03

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

04

Protection and control circuits保护与控制回路

Define loads, control logic, protection-device positions and service access before conductor and branch selection. Housing shape alone does not establish ratings or control functions.先核对负载、控制逻辑、保护器件位置与维修可达性，再安排线径和分支。不能仅凭外壳形状判断保护额定或控制功能。

05

Interface and function review接口与功能确认

Do not interchange constant-voltage and constant-current drivers by connector恒压与恒流驱动不能按接口互换

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Fixtures and light strips灯具与灯带

Define wiring from the driver, channels and pad/connector interface. Define the circuit from the fixture/strip driver and interface.按驱动方式、通道和焊盘或插接端定义接线。选配时，灯具或灯带的驱动与接口决定起始电路。

Signs and distributed lighting标识与分布照明

Consider end voltage drop, segmented connections and labels on long branches. Map dimming, color channels and data paths separately.长支路应考虑末端压降、分段接续和标签。选配时，调光、颜色通道与数据回路分别映射。

Equipment auxiliary lighting设备辅助照明

Include switches, drivers and lamps in the complete circuit. Evaluate end voltage drop using trunk and branch loads.将开关、驱动器与灯组连接纳入完整回路。选配时，主干与各支路负载共同计算末端压降。

Zoned architectural lighting建筑分区照明

Configure zone lengths, channels and end voltage for the driver layout. Configure splices and connectors for indoor, damp or outdoor conditions.分区长度、通道及末端电压按驱动布局配置。选配时，接续及接头按室内、潮湿或户外环境配置。

Outdoor lamp groups户外灯组连接

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Can LED wire size be selected from wattage alone?LED 线径只按功率选择可以吗？

No. Supply voltage, current per channel, run length, complete return path, allowable drop, connector losses, ambient and local temperature, bundling and duty cycle all affect selection.不可以。供电电压、各通道电流、线长、完整回流路径、允许压降、连接器损耗、环境与局部温度、成束及工作循环都会影响选择。

Can one four-pin connector be used for every RGB system?四针连接器可以通用于所有 RGB 系统吗？

No. Systems can differ in voltage, common-anode or common-cathode arrangement, channel order, current, connector keying and control method. The exact pin map and mating device must be confirmed.不可以。系统在电压、共阳或共阴、通道顺序、电流、连接器防错与控制方式上都可能不同，必须确认确切针脚图与对接设备。

Does an IP67 connector make the whole LED harness IP67?使用 IP67 连接器就代表整条 LED 线束达到 IP67 吗？

No. The complete assembly boundary includes cable entry, wire seals, mating interface, branch transitions, splices, end caps and enclosure entries. The agreed test configuration must cover the finished assembly.不代表。完整组件边界还包括线缆入口、单线密封、对插界面、分支过渡、接头、端帽与机箱入口，约定测试配置必须覆盖最终成品。
