Application directions应用方向

Internal machine connections and service设备机内连接与维护

Keep internal connections clear of working zones and moving parts. Allow appropriate cable length between supports, interfaces and service paths. Specify the exact family, insert, housing and complete part numbers.设备机内连接要避开加工区与运动部件，并在固定点、接口和检修路径之间保留合理线缆余量。选配时，指定品牌内的具体系列、插芯、外壳和完整料号。

Shield layers and end terminations屏蔽层与端部接续

Define shield coverage, drain wires and grounding method. Treat shield-continuity checks and system interference testing as separate tasks. Check contact layout, keying, numbering view and insertion direction independently.屏蔽选择需要明确覆盖结构、排流线和接地方式，屏蔽连续性检查与整机抗干扰试验应分别定义。选配时，独立核对接点排列、键位、针号视图与插入方向。

Detachable circular connections可拆卸圆形连接

Check gender, keying, coupling and end-face views together. Equal contact counts do not replace confirmation of the exact series and part numbers. Match entry clamping range and shield termination to the cable construction.圆形接口需要同时核对公母、键位、锁紧方式和端视图，针数相同并不能替代完整系列与料号确认。选配时，入口夹持范围与屏蔽接续方式按线缆结构匹配。

Equipment panels and circular interfaces设备面板与圆形接口
