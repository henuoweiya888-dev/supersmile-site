PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is a control panel harness one standard product?控制面板线束是固定标准产品吗？

No. It is a category covering door looms, controller interconnects, terminal wiring, panel-to-machine interfaces, pre-installed sets and service branches. The finished assembly follows the actual panel data.不是。它覆盖柜门线束、控制器互连、端子布线、面板至设备接口、预装套件和检修分支等结构，最终成品依据实际面板资料定义。

What information should be provided first?首先需要提供哪些资料？

A panel layout, circuit or connection table, component models, terminal schedule, wire-number rule, hinge movement and delivery boundary are the most useful starting materials.面板布局、回路或接线表、器件型号、端子表、线号规则、铰链运动范围和交付边界，是最有帮助的起始资料。

Can you match an existing panel harness?可以匹配现有面板线束吗？

Yes. A sample or existing panel can be measured and mapped, but hidden jumpers, electrical function and mating references still require confirmation before production.可以。可对样品或现有面板进行测量和回路整理，但隐藏跳接、电气功能及对插资料仍需在生产前确认。
