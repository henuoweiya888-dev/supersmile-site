APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Leads for small electronic modules小型电子模块的引线

Define contact mapping, fine-wire processing limits and enclosure clearance for compact modules. Locate support close to the cable ends. Confirm actual AWG, strand count, conductor material and insulation diameter from wire documents.小型模块的接口定义需要覆盖针位、细线加工范围和壳内空间，靠近端头的支撑位置尤其重要。选配时，实际AWG、股数、导体材质和绝缘外径需由线材文件确认。

Equipment control-board connections设备控制板连接

Document pin-row views, mating direction and nearby wire support. Check access to the latch and contacts after assembly. Check pitch, cavity positions and conductor range for the full contact family.板端连接要给出针排视图、插合方向和近端线缆支撑；检查装配后是否仍能操作锁扣并查看接点。选配时，按完整端子系列核对间距、针位与导体适配范围。

Contact and fine-wire termination端子与细线端接

Contact selection depends on conductor construction, insulation diameter and tooling limits. Set wire preparation and crimp inspection for the actual contact part. Define processing and inspection separately for stripping, crimping or soldering.端子选择同时取决于导体结构、绝缘外径和工具范围；备线尺寸与压接检查应落到实际接点型号。选配时，分别确定剥皮、压接或焊接的工艺与检查方法。

Display-module and control-board connections显示模块与控制板连接

Check insertion direction, contact orientation and frame clearance at display modules. Mark fixed folds on the assembly drawing. Provide near-end support, installation bend clearance and a defined pull-test method.显示模块连接需核对插入方向、触点朝向与边框空间，并将固定折弯的位置标入装配图。选配时，设置近端支撑、安装弯曲空间和明确的拉力验收。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Confirm actual AWG, strand count, conductor material and insulation diameter from wire documents.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，实际AWG、股数、导体材质和绝缘外径需由线材文件确认。

Routing and retention in confined spaces狭小空间的布线与固定

Define bends, retention points and assembly order in confined spaces. Do not compensate for an incorrect interface direction or length with forced folds. Check pitch, cavity positions and conductor range for the full contact family.狭小空间布线需明确转弯、固定点和拆装顺序，不能用强行折线弥补接口方向或长度误差。选配时，按完整端子系列核对间距、针位与导体适配范围。
