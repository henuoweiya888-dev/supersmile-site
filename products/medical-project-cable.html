ENGINEERING OVERVIEW工程概览

Engineer the cable inside the equipment risk-control system把线缆纳入医疗设备风险控制体系

IEC 60601-1 addresses basic safety and essential performance of medical electrical equipment. A cable assembly is normally one component within that equipment boundary, not independently “IEC 60601 certified” by appearance or material. The equipment manufacturer’s risk management, applied-part classification, electrical architecture, EMC plan, cleaning method and usability requirements define the cable inputs.

IEC 60601-1 针对医用电气设备的基本安全与基本性能。线缆组件通常只是设备边界内的一个元件，不能凭外观或材料独立宣称“IEC 60601 认证”。设备制造商的风险管理、应用部分分类、电气架构、EMC 方案、清洁方法与可用性要求共同定义线缆输入。

01

SYSTEM DEFINITION

系统定义

Place the cable inside the equipment boundary把线缆放入设备边界

Start with the equipment and patient boundary

从设备与患者边界开始

Identify whether the cable carries mains power, protective earth, isolated power, measurement signals, actuator energy, communications or an applied-part connection. Record accessible conductive parts, connector touch points, isolation barriers and expected connection sequence. Creepage, clearance, leakage current and dielectric requirements are equipment-level safety decisions; the cable drawing must preserve the relevant insulation, pin spacing and protective conductors without claiming the cable alone establishes compliance.

应识别线缆承载市电、保护接地、隔离电源、测量信号、执行器能量、通信还是应用部分连接，并记录可触及导电部件、连接器触点、隔离屏障与预期插接顺序。爬电距离、电气间隙、漏电流与介电要求属于设备层级安全决策；线缆图纸必须保持相关绝缘、针间距与保护导体，但不能宣称线缆单独建立合规。

Low-level signals need a defined noise and grounding plan

低电平信号需要明确噪声与接地方案

ECG, temperature, pressure, SpO2, imaging, encoder and communication channels may use twisted pairs, coaxial paths, driven shields or controlled grounding. Pair assignment, shield coverage, connector backshell, cable capacitance and flex can influence signal quality. Keep sensitive channels separated from motors, heaters and switching power where the equipment design requires it. EMC evidence belongs to the configured equipment and accessories, not to a generic shielded-cable label.

心电、温度、压力、血氧、成像、编码器与通信通道可能采用双绞线、同轴、驱动屏蔽或受控接地。线对分配、屏蔽覆盖、连接器后壳、线缆电容与弯曲都会影响信号质量。按设备设计要求，将敏感通道与电机、加热器和开关电源隔离。EMC 证据属于配置完成的设备与附件，而不是一个笼统“屏蔽线”标签。

02

CONSTRUCTION + INSTALLATION

结构与安装

Coordinate handling and signal integrity协同操作与信号完整性

Flexibility, touch and cleaning must coexist

柔性、触感与清洁要求必须共存

Hand-held probes, foot controls, carts and detachable accessories create repeated bending, pull, rolling and cleaning loads. Jacket choice can affect flexibility, surface friction, stain resistance, plasticizer migration and compatibility with specified disinfectants. Overmoulds, strain reliefs and connector keys should support the real handling sequence. A cleaning claim must name the agent, concentration, contact time and cycle count evaluated by the equipment manufacturer.

手持探头、脚踏控制、推车与可拆附件会产生重复弯曲、拉扯、滚压和清洁负载。护套选择会影响柔性、表面摩擦、抗污、增塑剂迁移以及与规定消毒剂的相容性。包覆成型、应力释放与连接器防错应支持真实操作顺序。清洁声明必须写明设备制造商评价的清洁剂、浓度、接触时间与循环次数。

Connector coding supports safe use and service

连接器编码支持安全操作与维护

Colour, keying, labels and mechanical incompatibility can reduce incorrect connections, but they must follow the device usability and service plan. Confirm exact mating references, pin view, latching force, retention, insertion cycles and replacement access. Patient-side, operator-side and internal connectors may need different protection. For home healthcare equipment, storage, transport, lay-user handling and environmental assumptions may add requirements beyond a controlled clinical room.

颜色、防错键、标签与机械不兼容可以降低误插风险，但必须服从设备可用性与维修方案。需要确认准确对插料号、针脚视图、锁紧力、保持力、插拔次数与更换空间。患者侧、操作者侧与内部连接器可能需要不同防护。家庭医疗设备还要考虑储存、运输、非专业用户操作及环境假设。

03

EVIDENCE + RELEASE

证据与放行

Preserve risk-control evidence保持风险控制证据

Traceability links the assembly to equipment validation

追溯把组件连接到设备验证

Controlled records should link cable, connector, terminal and material identities to drawing revision, tooling, crimp or solder criteria, electrical inspection and lot history. First-article approval can include dimensions, pinout, polarity, labels, pull or retention checks and agreed environmental tests. A later material or supplier substitution must be visible to the equipment manufacturer so risk analysis and verification remain valid.

受控记录应把线材、连接器、端子与材料身份连接到图纸版本、工装、压接或焊接判定、电气检查与批次历史。首件批准可包含尺寸、针脚、极性、标签、拉力或保持力检查及约定环境试验。后续材料或供应商替代必须对设备制造商可见，才能保持风险分析与验证有效。

Common questions常见问题

Can a cable assembly be called IEC 60601 certified?线缆组件能否称为 IEC 60601 认证？

Normally the standard applies to medical electrical equipment and systems. The cable is specified and controlled as a component within that equipment evaluation.

通常该标准适用于医用电气设备与系统，线缆作为其中元件被规定和控制。
