ENGINEERING OVERVIEW工程概览

Coordinate voltage class, insulation system, termination and installed stress协同定义电压等级、绝缘体系、端接与安装应力

Rated voltage, system earthing, fault duty, conductor temperature, installation method, short-circuit current and accessories define the cable system. IEC 60502-1 covers certain extruded-insulation power cables for fixed installations at 1 kV and 3 kV, while IEC 60502-2 addresses specified fixed-installation cables from 6 kV to 30 kV. Mobile heavy-duty cables may require a different product standard and project evidence.

额定电压、系统接地、故障工况、导体温度、安装方式、短路电流与附件共同定义电缆系统。IEC 60502-1 覆盖部分 1 kV 与 3 kV 固定敷设挤包绝缘电力电缆，IEC 60502-2 涉及 6 kV 至 30 kV 的规定固定敷设电缆。移动式重型电缆可能需要不同产品标准和项目证据。

01

SYSTEM DEFINITION

系统定义

Define the electrical system定义电气系统

Define the system voltage and fault conditions

明确系统电压与故障条件

Record phase-to-phase and phase-to-earth voltage, AC or DC service, earthing method, expected overvoltage, fault-clearing time and short-circuit current. The same nominal voltage may lead to different insulation and screen requirements in different networks. Equipment, vehicle, renewable-energy and mining applications can use the phrase high voltage differently, so the governing standard and exact voltage designation must appear on the drawing.

应记录相间与相对地电压、交流或直流、接地方式、预期过电压、故障切除时间和短路电流。相同标称电压在不同网络中可能对应不同绝缘与屏蔽要求。设备、车辆、新能源与矿山对“高压”的定义可能不同，因此图纸必须写明适用标准与准确电压标识。

Conductor size follows current and thermal installation

导体截面取决于电流与热环境

Continuous current, duty cycle, grouping, ambient temperature, ventilation, conduit, tray, soil or free-air installation all change ampacity. Voltage drop and short-circuit withstand provide additional constraints. Flexible high-strand conductors can support motion or service handling, but strand construction must be compatible with lugs and crimp barrels. Thermal calculations should use the exact cable construction and installed route, not a generic ampacity table.

持续电流、占空比、成束、环境温度、通风、导管、桥架、土壤或空气敷设都会改变载流量，压降与短路耐受又形成额外限制。高股数柔性导体可用于运动或维护操作，但绞线结构必须与接线端子和压接筒兼容。热计算应采用准确线缆结构与安装路径，不能只套用通用载流表。

02

CONSTRUCTION + INSTALLATION

结构与安装

Coordinate construction and accessories协同结构与附件

Insulation, screen and sheath form one dielectric system

绝缘、屏蔽与护套构成完整介电体系

Extruded insulation, semiconductive screens, metallic screen or armour, bedding and outer sheath each have a defined electrical or mechanical role. Clean preparation is critical because cuts, contamination, sharp screen edges and voids concentrate electric stress. Material compatibility with temperature, oil, moisture, UV, abrasion and flame requirements must be confirmed across the complete build. A thicker jacket does not correct an incorrect insulation system.

挤包绝缘、半导电屏蔽、金属屏蔽或铠装、垫层与外护套分别承担规定的电气或机械作用。备线清洁度非常关键，划伤、污染、尖锐屏蔽边缘和空隙会造成电场集中。整套结构需同时确认温度、油液、潮气、紫外、磨损与阻燃要求的材料相容性；加厚护套不能纠正错误绝缘体系。

Terminations and joints are part of the cable system

终端与中间接头属于电缆系统

Lugs, stress-control components, separable connectors, glands, shields and enclosure interfaces must match conductor, insulation diameter, screen construction and voltage class. Creepage, clearance, sealing, torque, bend radius and support are installation decisions, not cosmetic details. For mobile equipment, connector interlock, touch protection, coding and service sequence may also be safety inputs. Exact accessory part numbers and preparation dimensions belong in controlled work instructions.

端子、应力控制件、可分离连接器、电缆格兰、屏蔽与机壳界面必须匹配导体、绝缘外径、屏蔽结构和电压等级。爬电距离、电气间隙、密封、扭矩、弯曲半径与支撑都是安装决策，不是外观细节。移动设备还可能需要考虑连接器互锁、防触电、编码和维护顺序。准确附件料号与备线尺寸应写入受控作业文件。

03

EVIDENCE + RELEASE

证据与放行

Inspect the complete installation boundary检查完整安装边界

Inspection and testing must match the project boundary

检验与试验必须匹配项目边界

Incoming records confirm cable and accessory identity. Assembly inspection controls preparation, crimping or bolting, shielding, cleaning, torque, sealing and labels. Depending on product standard and project scope, evidence may include conductor resistance, insulation resistance, voltage tests, partial-discharge or sheath tests performed by qualified facilities. The test plan must state whether Super Smile supplies a finished cable assembly, an internal equipment lead or part of a larger field-installed system.

来料记录用于确认线缆与附件身份；装配检查控制备线、压接或螺栓连接、屏蔽、清洁、扭矩、密封与标签。依据产品标准和项目范围，证据可能包括导体电阻、绝缘电阻、耐压、局部放电或护套试验，并由具备条件的机构实施。试验计划必须说明超斯迈尔交付的是成品电缆组件、设备内部引线，还是更大现场系统的一部分。
