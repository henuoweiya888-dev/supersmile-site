Configuration & Project Review

01 Fine-pitch mating pair

Confirm pitch, row count, contact count, keying and the mating board connector drawing.

02 32AWG wire construction

Specify conductor strand construction, insulation and finished diameter rather than gauge alone.

03 Current and resistance

Check load and permitted resistance for the actual wire length and connector contact.

04 Termination and handling

Set strip length, termination tooling and handling controls appropriate to the small conductor.

05 Route and retention

Provide bend clearance, support and connector retention so the lead does not carry equipment movement.

06 Small-wire verification

Agree continuity, pull-force and visual inspection criteria for the complete assembly.

Send the part number, drawing or sample to confirm the replacement configuration and sourcing plan.
