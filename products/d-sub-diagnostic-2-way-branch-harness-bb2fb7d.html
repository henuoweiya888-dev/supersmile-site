PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Are DB9 and DE-9 the same connector?DB9 和 DE-9 是同一种接口吗？

DB9 is a common market name for the nine-contact connector, while DE-9 is the more accurate shell designation. Always confirm the mating face, contact sex, hardware and equipment interface rather than relying on the name alone.市场常把九针接口称为 DB9，而 DE-9 是更准确的外壳称呼。应核对插合面、触点性别、固定硬件和设备端接口，不能只依赖名称。

Can D-Sub cables with the same contact count be interchanged?针数相同的 D-Sub 电缆可以互换吗？

No automatic interchangeability exists. Pin map, contact sex, shell, screw hardware, shielding, cable and hidden components can differ even when the contact count is equal.不能直接互换。即使针数相同，针脚、触点性别、外壳、螺钉、屏蔽、线缆和隐藏元件也可能不同。

What is different about high-density D-Sub?高密度 D-Sub 有什么不同？

It places more contacts into a comparable shell size. Cavity pattern, pitch, terminals, wire range and tooling generally differ from standard-density versions.高密度结构在相近壳体尺寸内布置更多触点，其孔位、节距、端子、线径范围与工装通常不同于标准密度版本。
