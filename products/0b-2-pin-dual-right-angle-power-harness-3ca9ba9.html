APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Instrument inputs and measurement leads仪器输入与测量引线

Start measurement leads from signal type, input conditions and grounding. Select pairs and shields for the actual measurement range and noise target. Define shell size, push-pull family, keying and insert as one mating set.测量引线以信号类型、输入条件和接地方式为起点，线对与屏蔽选择需要对应实际量程和噪声目标。选配时，壳号、推拉系列、键位与插芯必须成套确定。

Handling clearance at push-pull instrument interfaces仪器推拉接口的操作空间

Allow room to grip and release a push-pull plug. Match collet range, cable diameter and boot bend; establish mating relationships from complete part numbers. Record contact gender and pin mapping from mating-face views.推拉式插头要留出握持与解锁空间，并匹配夹头范围、线缆外径和尾套弯曲；具体对插关系由完整料号定义。选配时，用插合面视图记录接点公母和针位映射。

External interfaces on portable equipment便携设备的外部接口

Consider handling, plug grip and cable bends together. Align the exit direction with the device position during use. The collet range must suit the actual cable diameter and boot configuration.便携设备连接要同时考虑操作手势、插头握持和尾部弯曲空间，线缆出口方向应与实际使用姿态协调。选配时，夹头范围须覆盖实际线缆外径及尾套配置。

Detachable circular connections可拆卸圆形连接

Check gender, keying, coupling and end-face views together. Equal contact counts do not replace confirmation of the exact series and part numbers. Define heat exposure, insulation and support for solder-cup or crimp termination.圆形接口需要同时核对公母、键位、锁紧方式和端视图，针数相同并不能替代完整系列与料号确认。选配时，按焊杯或压接方式确定热影响、绝缘和支撑。

Equipment panels and circular interfaces设备面板与圆形接口

Start with the cutout, panel thickness, mounting direction and rear clearance. Coordinate contact arrangement and cable exit with the complete assembly view. Define shell size, push-pull family, keying and insert as one mating set.面板连接以开孔、板厚、装配方向和后方净空为起点；接点排列与电缆出口还需要对应完整总装视图。选配时，壳号、推拉系列、键位与插芯必须成套确定。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Record contact gender and pin mapping from mating-face views.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，用插合面视图记录接点公母和针位映射。
