PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Are FAKRA and automotive LVDS the same thing?FAKRA 和车载 LVDS 是同一个概念吗？

No. FAKRA describes a coaxial connector family, while LVDS describes signaling. Determine whether the equipment requires coax, HSD differential wiring or another interface.不是。FAKRA 描述同轴接头系列，LVDS 描述信号方式。应确认设备需要同轴、HSD 差分连接还是其他接口。

Does matching color prove compatibility?颜色一致就代表兼容吗？

No. Confirm coding, contact interface, cable type and the electrical function on both sides. Color is an aid, not a complete specification.不代表。还需确认两端编码、接触界面、线缆类型和电气功能。颜色只是辅助识别，不是完整规格。

Can a passive adapter convert a camera protocol?无源转接线可以转换摄像头协议吗？

No. Protocol conversion requires compatible electronics. A cable adapter changes a physical connection and must preserve the required channel.不能。协议转换需要兼容的电子设备，线缆转接改变物理连接，并需保持所要求的通道特性。
