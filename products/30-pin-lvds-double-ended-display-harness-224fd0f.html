APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Record the JAE family, housing and contact part numbers together.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，同时记录JAE系列、壳体及端子件号。

Equipment control-board connections设备控制板连接

Document pin-row views, mating direction and nearby wire support. Check access to the latch and contacts after assembly. Define contact rows, polarization and mates from controlled end views.板端连接要给出针排视图、插合方向和近端线缆支撑；检查装配后是否仍能操作锁扣并查看接点。选配时，按受控端视图定义针排、极化及对插端。

Contact and fine-wire termination端子与细线端接

Contact selection depends on conductor construction, insulation diameter and tooling limits. Set wire preparation and crimp inspection for the actual contact part. Match termination method and tooling to conductor and insulation dimensions.端子选择同时取决于导体结构、绝缘外径和工具范围；备线尺寸与压接检查应落到实际接点型号。选配时，端接方式与导体、绝缘外径和工装相匹配。

Display-module and control-board connections显示模块与控制板连接

Check insertion direction, contact orientation and frame clearance at display modules. Mark fixed folds on the assembly drawing. Check locking, retention and installation envelope as separate requirements.显示模块连接需核对插入方向、触点朝向与边框空间，并将固定折弯的位置标入装配图。选配时，检查锁止、保持力及安装包络的不同边界。

Equipment power-distribution branches设备电源分配支路

Select power branches from actual loads, circuit protection and route length. Include termination, retention and service positions in the wiring definition. Record the JAE family, housing and contact part numbers together.供电分支以实际负载、回路保护和线长为选配依据，端头、固定和检修位置也需列入布线定义。选配时，同时记录JAE系列、壳体及端子件号。

Terminal wiring inside control cabinets控制柜内的端子配线

Coordinate terminal positions, duct exits and service access within the cabinet. Wire labels should correspond to the circuit table and equipment ports. Define contact rows, polarization and mates from controlled end views.柜内连接需协调端子位置、线槽出口和可维护空间，线号应能对应回路表与设备端口。选配时，按受控端视图定义针排、极化及对插端。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Do similar contact rows establish the same family?相似针排能确定同系列吗？

Similar contact rows do not establish same-series mating. Record the JAE family, housing and contact part numbers together.相似针排不确定同系列插合。同时记录JAE系列、壳体及端子件号。

Are contact retention and harness pull force the same check?端子保持力和线束拉力是同一检查吗？

Check contact retention separately from harness pull strength. Define contact rows, polarization and mates from controlled end views.端子保持与线束拉力分别核对。按受控端视图定义针排、极化及对插端。

How is an alternative interface verified?替代接口需要怎样的验证？

Alternatives require dimensional and mating verification. Match termination method and tooling to conductor and insulation dimensions.替代件需具体尺寸和插合验证。端接方式与导体、绝缘外径和工装相匹配。
