CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the circular plug multi-core flying leads pigtail form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以圆形插头多芯散线尾线的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Circular connection end圆形连接端

The end uses a circular interface form, with connection positions planned around mating direction, retention and cable exit.端头具有圆形接口形式，可围绕对插方向、端部固定和线缆出口规划连接位置。

02

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

03

Open lead ends开放引线端

An end or some branches remain as open leads, with later terminations and insulation preparation defined for the assembly method.一端或部分支路以开放引线形式保留，可结合装配方式定义后续端头和绝缘处理。

04

Pigtail connection form尾线连接形式

A lead section extends behind the connector, allowing length, further termination and support to follow the equipment connection.插接端后保留引线段，便于按设备接线位置确定长度、后续端接与支撑。

05

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Pumps and drive equipment泵与驱动设备

Define connections for media, thermal zones and equipment interfaces. Use the task, area classification and project standards to define review scope.根据介质、热区及设备接口定义电气连接。选配时，用途、区域分类和项目标准决定审核边界。

Field instruments现场测量仪表

Configure sensor supply, low-level signals and shields separately. Use actual fluids, gases, pressure and temperature for material conditions.传感器供电、低电平信号与屏蔽分别配置。选配时，实际油液、气体、压力和温度形成材料条件。

Process equipment and area-condition review工艺设备与区域条件评审

The project owner defines area conditions, media exposure and equipment documents before connectors, jackets and installation are reviewed. A context photo does not establish an explosion-protection rating.区域条件、介质暴露和设备文件需由项目方明确，再核对接头、护套与安装方式；场景照片不代表防爆等级。

Outdoor valve-bank interfaces室外阀组接口

Select connectors/jackets for temperature, rain and media exposure. Agree shielding/grounding separately from explosion-protection responsibility.温度、雨水与介质暴露共同影响接头及护套。选配时，屏蔽接地与防爆责任分开约定。

Equipment cabinet interconnects设备机柜互连

Allocate power, signals and earth separately with defined ports. Use the task, area classification and project standards to define review scope.供电、信号和接地分开布置并定义端口。选配时，用途、区域分类和项目标准决定审核边界。

Inspection and monitoring leads检修与监测引线

Do not assume monitoring leads have hazardous-area approval. Use actual fluids, gases, pressure and temperature for material conditions.监测连接不得默认具备危险区域使用资格。选配时，实际油液、气体、压力和温度形成材料条件。
