Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the ix double-ended network harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以IX接口双端网络线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

IX rectangular data interfacesIX矩形数据接口

Both ends use compact rectangular IX-form data interfaces. Match insertion direction and retention access to the equipment ports.两端采用紧凑矩形IX形式接口，插合方向和锁紧操作空间需与设备端配合。

Short two-ended patch layout双端短跳接布局

A short cable section joins both ends. Arrange patch length and bends around adjacent equipment or module positions.两端由短段线缆连接，可围绕相邻设备或模块位置安排跳接长度和弯曲。

Black connector exits meet a light-colored jacket. Plan fixing points and bend starts from the clearance behind each interface.黑色端头出口连接浅色护套线身，固定点和弯曲起点可按接口后方空间规划。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These connection directions concern equipment data interfaces. Confirm fit, circuits and performance from the actual equipment data.以下连接方向围绕设备数据接口展开，实际适配、回路与性能依据具体设备资料确认。

Controllers and industrial networks控制器与工业网络

Select cable construction from controller ports, network architecture and complete interface data. Confirm protocol and rate separately.依据控制器端口、网络架构和完整接口资料选择线缆结构；协议与速率另行确认。

Cabinet and panel data extensions机柜与面板数据延伸

Arrange data interfaces, length and strain support around rear-panel space. Keep ports and latches accessible.结合面板后方空间安排数据接口、长度和应力支撑，保留端口插拔与锁扣操作位置。

Links between network equipment网络设备间布线

Organize patching and routing around equipment ports. Define both link endpoints, pair mapping and shield grounding.按设备端口组织跳接与走线，明确链路两端、线对关系和屏蔽接地要求。

Robot-peripheral data interfaces机器人外围数据接口

Plan communication connections from peripheral-module ports. Review fixed and moving sections separately; cycle life needs exact cable and test evidence.按外围模块端口安排通信连接，固定与运动段分别审核；循环寿命需要具体线缆与试验依据。

Data links within automation cells自动化单元数据连接

Plan data paths between controllers and equipment modules. Check interfaces, route length and the equipment-defined communication validation.在控制器与设备模块之间规划数据路径，核对接口、布线长度及设备规定的通信验证方法。

Equipment service and network patching设备维护与网络跳接

Check both interfaces and pin definitions when replacing leads. Set patch length and labels for the port locations and validate the complete link.维护换线时核对两端接口和针脚定义，按端口位置安排跳接长度与标签，验证完整链路。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does a matching interface shape guarantee data compatibility?接口外形相同就能保证数据兼容吗？

No. Confirm the full family, pin and pair mapping, equipment communication, cable construction and actual link requirements.不能。还需确认完整系列、针脚与线对、设备通信方式、线缆结构和实际链路要求。

Does continuity acceptance establish network performance?导通合格是否代表网络通信性能合格？

No. Pairs, termination, impedance, shielding, length and both devices affect communication and need appropriate link validation.不能。通信还受线对结构、端接、阻抗、屏蔽、长度和两端设备影响，需要相应链路验证。

Does an RJ45 or M12 interface establish PoE capability?有RJ45或M12接口就可以承载PoE吗？

No. Confirm the power function, pin allocation, conductor and contact limits, temperature rise and equipment compatibility separately.不能。供电功能、针脚分配、导体与接点能力、温升及整机兼容需另行确认。
