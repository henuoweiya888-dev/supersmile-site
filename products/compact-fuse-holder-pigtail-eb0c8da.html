CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the compact fuse holder pigtail form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以小型保险丝座尾线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Fuse-holder connection section保险座连接部位

The assembly includes a fuse-holder connection section, separating supply, protected-side routing and service access in the layout.组件包含保险座相关连接部位，可将供电、受保护端和维护接近位置在布局中区分。

02

Pigtail connection form尾线连接形式

A lead section extends behind the connector, allowing length, further termination and support to follow the equipment connection.插接端后保留引线段，便于按设备接线位置确定长度、后续端接与支撑。

03

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

04

Protection and control circuits保护与控制回路

Define loads, control logic, protection-device positions and service access before conductor and branch selection. Housing shape alone does not establish ratings or control functions.先核对负载、控制逻辑、保护器件位置与维修可达性，再安排线径和分支。不能仅凭外壳形状判断保护额定或控制功能。

Equipment power protection设备供电保护
