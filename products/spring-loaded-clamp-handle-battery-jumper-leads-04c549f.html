CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the spring-loaded clamp handle battery jumper leads form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以弹簧夹柄电瓶搭接线的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Current path and end termination载流与端部连接

Select conductors and terminations from continuous and peak loads, installed length and temperature rise. Confirm the end interface, contact surface, insulation coverage and fixing as a set; check mounting-bore size where ring terminals are specified.依据持续与峰值负载、安装长度和温升要求选择导体与端接；端部接口、接触面、绝缘覆盖及固定方式需要成套确认；仅在采用环形端子时核对安装孔径。

02

Extension and retraction伸缩与回弹空间

Define retracted and extended lengths, coil diameter and straight tails from the working stroke. Repeated motion also requires review of retraction force, fixing points and cable fatigue.结合工作行程确认收缩长度、伸展长度、圈径和直线尾段；反复运动时还需审核回弹力、固定点与线材疲劳表现。

03

Interface and function review接口与功能确认

Application directions应用方向

Service starting connections维修启动连接

Battery replacement and service电瓶更换与维护

Arrange ends for polarity, access and insulation. Record peak current, duration and cooling intervals separately.极性、可接近性与绝缘共同影响端头安排。选配时，峰值、持续时间和冷却间隔分别记录。

Commercial-vehicle service work商用车辆维护作业

For commercial-vehicle starting assistance and battery service, confirm voltage system, contacts and working space before defining tool leads or a fixed assembly. Conductor material, area and length define circuit resistance.商用车辆的辅助启动与电瓶维护需确认电压体系、接点和操作空间，再定义工具引线或固定组件。选配时，导体材质、截面与总长度决定回路电阻。

Temporary auxiliary power and leads临时辅助电源与引线

Define load, duration, polarity identification and insulation coverage for temporary auxiliary-power leads. Cable outside diameter alone is not a selection basis. Review clamp contact, polarity and insulation together.临时辅助电源引线需明确负载、使用时长、极性标识和绝缘覆盖，避免仅按线缆外径选型。选配时，夹钳接触、极性及绝缘保护共同审核。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Are permanent battery leads and jumper cables the same?永久安装的电瓶线与救援跨接线一样吗？

No. Mounting, insulation, terminations and operating duty differ. A temporary clamp connection is not a substitute for a released permanent installation.不一样。固定、绝缘、端接和工作周期不同，临时夹钳连接不能替代已确认的永久安装方案。

Can current capacity be selected from AWG alone?只看 AWG 就能确定载流能力吗？

No. Review conductor material and area, length, ambient temperature, bundling, voltage drop, connections and the continuous or intermittent duty.不能。还需核对导体材料与截面、长度、环境温度、成束状态、压降、连接点以及连续或间歇工作要求。

Can you match a drawing or existing cable?可以按图纸或现有样品定制吗？

Yes. Provide dimensions, terminal references, polarity, installation photographs, electrical duty, quantity and required inspection. A sample establishes geometry but not every hidden requirement.可以。请提供尺寸、端子资料、极性、安装照片、电气工况、数量及检验要求。样品能提供几何参考，但不包含全部隐藏要求。
