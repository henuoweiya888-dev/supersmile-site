CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the df13 double-ended twisted wire terminal harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以DF13接口 · 双端绞线端子线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Twisted conductor form绞合导线形态

Conductors follow a twisted arrangement. Unwinding near the ends and fixing positions can follow the equipment route.导线以绞合形态排列，端头附近的展开长度与固定位置可以结合设备布线安排。

02

Terminal connection section端子接入部位

Leads use terminal-form ends at the connection points. Orientation, fixing and insulation coverage can follow the equipment assembly.引线以端子形式接入相应连接位置，可按设备装配方式安排端头方向、固定和绝缘覆盖。

03

Two-ended connection layout双端连接布局

Connection points sit at both cable ends, with the route arranged for equipment spacing and port orientation.线缆在两端设置连接位置，可结合设备间距和端口朝向安排整体走线。

04

Pairs and shield termination线对与屏蔽端接

Define pair allocation, shield construction, grounding path and termination coverage to avoid unintended breaks at branches or connections. Validate performance in the actual system.确认线对分配、屏蔽形式、接地路径和端接覆盖范围，避免屏蔽在分支或连接处意外中断。效果需在实际系统环境中验证。

05

Conductor and insulation selection导体与绝缘选配

Select conductors and insulation from circuit loads, diameter limits, bending and exposure. A material name does not replace ratings or validation results.从电路负载、外径限制、弯曲方式与环境条件选择导体和绝缘体系；材料名称不应直接代替额定参数或验证结果。

Equipment control-board connections设备控制板连接

Document pin-row views, mating direction and nearby wire support. Check access to the latch and contacts after assembly. Confirm the exact Hirose family, full part number and board or cable form.板端连接要给出针排视图、插合方向和近端线缆支撑；检查装配后是否仍能操作锁扣并查看接点。选配时，确认Hirose具体系列、完整料号及板端或线端形式。

Leads for small electronic modules小型电子模块的引线

Define contact mapping, fine-wire processing limits and enclosure clearance for compact modules. Locate support close to the cable ends. Include pitch, mating height and header clearance in dimensional review.小型模块的接口定义需要覆盖针位、细线加工范围和壳内空间，靠近端头的支撑位置尤其重要。选配时，把节距、插合高度与板端空间列入尺寸审查。

Shield layers and end terminations屏蔽层与端部接续

Define shield coverage, drain wires and grounding method. Treat shield-continuity checks and system interference testing as separate tasks. Process miniature contacts for the actual conductor, insulation diameter and termination method.屏蔽选择需要明确覆盖结构、排流线和接地方式，屏蔽连续性检查与整机抗干扰试验应分别定义。选配时，微型端子按真实导体、绝缘外径及端接方式加工。

Display-module and control-board connections显示模块与控制板连接

Check insertion direction, contact orientation and frame clearance at display modules. Mark fixed folds on the assembly drawing. Check the latch, shield shell and cable support in the assembled state.显示模块连接需核对插入方向、触点朝向与边框空间，并将固定折弯的位置标入装配图。选配时，锁扣、屏蔽壳和线缆支撑按总装状态验证。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Confirm the exact Hirose family, full part number and board or cable form.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，确认Hirose具体系列、完整料号及板端或线端形式。
