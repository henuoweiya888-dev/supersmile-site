CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Conductor, insulation and material choices导体、绝缘与选材要点

Use the marine ring-terminal power harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以船用环形端子电源线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Ring-terminal end环形端子端头

A ring-terminal end provides a mounting-hole connection. Lead direction, support and insulation coverage can follow the stud position.末端采用带安装孔的环形端子形式，可围绕螺柱位置安排接线方向、端头支撑与绝缘覆盖。

02

Current path and end termination载流与端部连接

Select conductors and terminations from continuous and peak loads, installed length and temperature rise. Confirm the end interface, contact surface, insulation coverage and fixing as a set; check mounting-bore size where ring terminals are specified.依据持续与峰值负载、安装长度和温升要求选择导体与端接；端部接口、接触面、绝缘覆盖及固定方式需要成套确认；仅在采用环形端子时核对安装孔径。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Vessel and deck-equipment environments船舶与甲板设备环境

Identify cabin, deck or machinery-space installation before reviewing humidity, salt exposure, fluids and required documentation. Define vessel use, installation area and any required classification-document scope.船舶连接要先确定舱内、甲板或机舱区域，再分别评审湿热、盐雾、油液和所需文件范围。选配时，定义船舶用途、安装区域及需要的船级文件范围。

Equipment junction boxes and entry positions设备接线盒与入口位置

Define entry orientation, water-trap risks and service access at junction boxes. Record the seal-to-jacket combination separately. Specify salt, humidity, fluids and temperature exposure separately.接线盒入口需要说明安装朝向、积水风险和检修空间；密封件与电缆护套的匹配关系单独记录。选配时，分别说明盐、湿热、油液与温度暴露条件。

Terminal wiring inside control cabinets控制柜内的端子配线

Coordinate terminal positions, duct exits and service access within the cabinet. Wire labels should correspond to the circuit table and equipment ports. Select conductor, jacket, shield and flame construction for the project.柜内连接需协调端子位置、线槽出口和可维护空间，线号应能对应回路表与设备端口。选配时，导体、护套、屏蔽及阻燃结构按项目组合选择。

Equipment power-distribution branches设备电源分配支路

Select power branches from actual loads, circuit protection and route length. Include termination, retention and service positions in the wiring definition. Approval documents must cover the exact model and supplied scope.供电分支以实际负载、回路保护和线长为选配依据，端头、固定和检修位置也需列入布线定义。选配时，认可文件须对应具体型号及实际供应范围。

Connections around stern propulsion equipment船尾动力设备连接

Define splash, vibration and service conditions around stern equipment. Coordinate cable exit, nearby retention and detachable interfaces. Define vessel use, installation area and any required classification-document scope.船尾设备的连接需明确飞溅、振动和维护条件，并协调出线方向、近端固定与可拆接口。选配时，定义船舶用途、安装区域及需要的船级文件范围。

Contact and fine-wire termination端子与细线端接

Contact selection depends on conductor construction, insulation diameter and tooling limits. Set wire preparation and crimp inspection for the actual contact part. Specify salt, humidity, fluids and temperature exposure separately.端子选择同时取决于导体结构、绝缘外径和工具范围；备线尺寸与压接检查应落到实际接点型号。选配时，分别说明盐、湿热、油液与温度暴露条件。
