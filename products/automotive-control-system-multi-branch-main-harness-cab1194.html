APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Off-highway equipment routing非道路机械的布线评审

Review service access, movement and contamination exposure on agricultural and construction equipment. Treat interface protection and cable retention as separate requirements. Identify the exact family, such as DT, DTM or DTP, and its contact size.农机和工程机械连接需结合检修可达性、运动范围与污物暴露，分别确认接口防护和线缆固定条件。选配时，分别识别DT、DTM、DTP等具体系列及接点尺寸。

Interfaces within engine-bay layouts发动机舱内的接口布置

Map heat sources, fluid exposure and service routes together. Allow assembly space around connector ends and cable supports. Confirm pin/socket arrangement, gender, keying and cavity views against mating drawings.发动机舱连接要把热源、油液暴露和检修路径放在同一张布线图中，端头与固定点也需留出装配空间。选配时，针孔、公母、键位与腔位视图按对插图确认。

Vehicle service connections车辆检修转接位置

Provide access for mating and service tools. Record end-face views and cable retention after service; appearance alone does not establish interchangeability. Select seals by insulation diameter and document unused-cavity plugs separately.可拆卸尾线应保留插拔与工具空间，并记录端视图和维修后的固定方式，避免只凭外观确定替换关系。选配时，线封按绝缘外径选择，未使用腔位的封堵单列。

Chassis and mobile-machine connections底盘与移动机械连接

Keep routing clear of moving parts, abrasion points and water traps. Define connector orientation and nearby retention with the installation route. Define wedge-lock assembly order and contact-retention inspection points.底盘和移动机械的走线需要避开运动件、磨损点与积水位置，接口朝向和近端固定应随安装路径一起定义。选配时，确定楔形锁装配顺序及端子保持检查点。

Contact and fine-wire termination端子与细线端接

Contact selection depends on conductor construction, insulation diameter and tooling limits. Set wire preparation and crimp inspection for the actual contact part. Identify the exact family, such as DT, DTM or DTP, and its contact size.端子选择同时取决于导体结构、绝缘外径和工具范围；备线尺寸与压接检查应落到实际接点型号。选配时，分别识别DT、DTM、DTP等具体系列及接点尺寸。

Enclosure entries and cable retention机箱穿线与入口固定

Match cable entries to actual diameter, panel thickness and fixing method. Keep jacket retention independent of the internal termination. Confirm pin/socket arrangement, gender, keying and cavity views against mating drawings.线缆入口需匹配实际外径、板厚和固定方式；护套夹持与内部端接之间应保留独立的支撑路径。选配时，针孔、公母、键位与腔位视图按对插图确认。
