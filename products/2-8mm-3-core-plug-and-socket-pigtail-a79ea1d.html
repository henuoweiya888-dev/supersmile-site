CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the 2.8mm 3-core plug-and-socket pigtail form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以2.8mm接口 · 三芯插头插座尾线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

02

Pigtail connection form尾线连接形式

A lead section extends behind the connector, allowing length, further termination and support to follow the equipment connection.插接端后保留引线段，便于按设备接线位置确定长度、后续端接与支撑。

03

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Interfaces within engine-bay layouts发动机舱内的接口布置

Map heat sources, fluid exposure and service routes together. Allow assembly space around connector ends and cable supports. Retain complete Yazaki family, housing and contact part-number suffixes.发动机舱连接要把热源、油液暴露和检修路径放在同一张布线图中，端头与固定点也需留出装配空间。选配时，保留Yazaki系列、壳体和端子件号的完整后缀。

Vehicle control-module leads车辆控制模块引线

Separate interface cavities, power circuits and signal circuits at controller leads. Set branch dimensions from the module and mounting-point positions. Confirm keying, cavity drawings and orientation against the vehicle mate.控制模块尾线需要清楚区分接口腔位、电源和信号回路；分支尺寸应对应模块与固定点的位置。选配时，按车辆对插件确认键位、针位图和接口方向。

Vehicle service connections车辆检修转接位置

Provide access for mating and service tools. Record end-face views and cable retention after service; appearance alone does not establish interchangeability. Match seals and secondary locks to the actual conductor and insulation diameter.可拆卸尾线应保留插拔与工具空间，并记录端视图和维修后的固定方式，避免只凭外观确定替换关系。选配时，线封、二次锁与实际导体及绝缘外径配套。

Equipment control-board connections设备控制板连接

Document pin-row views, mating direction and nearby wire support. Check access to the latch and contacts after assembly. Define retention, sealing and temperature acceptance for the actual configuration.板端连接要给出针排视图、插合方向和近端线缆支撑；检查装配后是否仍能操作锁扣并查看接点。选配时，保持、密封和温度要求按实际配置确定验收依据。

Vehicle lighting and body connections车载灯具与车身连接

Coordinate mating clearance, cable retention and body routing around lighting units. Define circuit functions from the target equipment wiring diagram. Retain complete Yazaki family, housing and contact part-number suffixes.灯具附近的引线需要协调插接空间、线缆固定和车身走线路径，电路功能以目标设备接线图为依据。选配时，保留Yazaki系列、壳体和端子件号的完整后缀。

Chassis and mobile-machine connections底盘与移动机械连接

Keep routing clear of moving parts, abrasion points and water traps. Define connector orientation and nearby retention with the installation route. Confirm keying, cavity drawings and orientation against the vehicle mate.底盘和移动机械的走线需要避开运动件、磨损点与积水位置，接口朝向和近端固定应随安装路径一起定义。选配时，按车辆对插件确认键位、针位图和接口方向。
