APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These directions concern low-voltage battery-side, auxiliary-supply and maintenance connections. Confirm actual equipment and interfaces; photovoltaic-string or starter-circuit suitability is not claimed.以下为低压电池侧、辅助供电与维护接入的项目方向。实际设备及接口需要确认，本示例不声明适用于光伏串侧或起动机回路。

Controller low-voltage battery side控制器低压电池侧

For the battery side of a solar controller, confirm the controller battery terminals, battery connections and protection requirements from equipment data. This direction concerns low-voltage battery connections; it does not establish a photovoltaic-string connection.太阳能控制器的电池侧接线需按控制器电池端、蓄电池端和保护要求确认。此方向讨论低压电池连接，不将图示接口作为光伏组件串侧接口使用。

Detachable battery-maintenance leads电池维护可拆引线

For detachable maintenance leads, define the equipment interface, polarity and operating duration. Confirm whether ring ends remain installed and whether the plug-in end needs a cover, then check access and handling during sampling.维护设备的可拆引线需要确认设备端接口、正负极和使用时长。环形端子是否长期固定、插接端是否需带盖，都应在安装要求与样品阶段明确。

DC accessory interface adaptation直流附件接口转接

Create a connection table from the input/output data of both devices for a DC accessory adapter. The XT60, SAE-style or barrel-interface form does not replace checks of polarity, permitted current, supply direction and circuit protection.接口转接应按两端设备输入输出资料建立回路表。XT60、SAE式接口或DC圆孔端的形状不能代替极性、允许电流、供电方向和保护配置确认。

Battery-side circuit protection电池侧回路保护

For a lead with a fuse holder, review the fuse, holder, conductors and equipment load as a complete circuit. Define protection-device location and service access; ratings depend on the approved components and electrical duty.带保险座的结构需要把熔断器、保险座、导体和设备负载作为整套回路评审。明确保护器件位置及维护空间，实际额定值以确认物料和项目工况为准。

Plug-in auxiliary distribution插接式辅助配电

Select the mating parts at both ends of an auxiliary-distribution lead from equipment documentation. Confirm XT60 and SAE-style interface polarity, retention and lead fixing, then check installation and service access; mating alone does not establish suitability.辅助配电中的两端对插件需按设备接口资料选配，确认XT60与SAE式接口的正负极、插合保持及线缆固定。安装空间与维护路径也需检查，不能仅以端头可插合判断适用。

Maintenance-lead sample checks维护引线样品检查

Check continuity, polarity, mating, fuse-holder access and behavior under the agreed electrical duty during sampling. Record the test limits and connected equipment; a connector that can mate does not by itself establish suitability for service.样品可按项目确认导通、极性、插合、保险座操作和约定电气负载下的表现。测试限值与接入设备应记录，不能只以连接器能插合判断可投入使用。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is this a photovoltaic-string cable?这类线可以直接用于光伏组件串侧吗？

This page concerns low-voltage battery-side and maintenance connections. Photovoltaic-string connectors, voltage, insulation and exposure require separate review; an SAE-style, XT60 or barrel-interface form does not establish those requirements.本页讨论低压电池侧与维护连接。光伏串侧的连接器、电压、绝缘及环境要求需独立评审，不能从 SAE、XT60 或DC圆孔端外观推定满足。

Can it be selected for a starter circuit?可以用于启动机回路吗？

Starter-current duty is outside the confirmed scope of this page. Supply the system, load and installation conditions for a separate starter-circuit design, component selection and validation.启动机大电流不在本页确认范围。需要起动回路时，应另行提供系统、负载及安装工况，独立设计、选料与验证。

Does mating alone establish equipment compatibility?接口能插合就能接入设备吗？

Verify the actual interface specification, polarity, supply direction, permitted load and protection. A fuse holder does not replace confirmation of the complete circuit rating and operating conditions.仍需核对接口实际规格、正负极、供电方向、允许负载及保护配置。带保险座也不能代替整套回路的额定和工况确认。

Define the battery-side or maintenance interface确认电池侧或维护设备的接口

Share controller or maintenance-equipment data, both interfaces, polarity, target voltage and load, length, terminations and protection for configuration and sample review.请提供控制器或维护设备资料、两端接口与极性、目标电压及负载、线长、端子和保护配置，以便开展选配和样品评审。
