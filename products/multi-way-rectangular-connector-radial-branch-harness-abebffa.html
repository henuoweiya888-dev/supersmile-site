Branched end layout分支端头布局

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Branch joining and joint protection分支汇接与接点保护

Define each conductor, stacking direction and joint geometry before selecting tooling, insulation protection and inspection methods. Record conductor materials, individual branch areas and the welding combination.分支汇接需定义每根导体、叠放方向和接头几何，再选择工装、绝缘保护与检验方法。选配时，记录导体材质、各支路截面和焊接组合。

Contact and fine-wire termination端子与细线端接

Contact selection depends on conductor construction, insulation diameter and tooling limits. Set wire preparation and crimp inspection for the actual contact part. Confirm joint geometry, compressed dimensions and subsequent insulation boundaries.端子选择同时取决于导体结构、绝缘外径和工具范围；备线尺寸与压接检查应落到实际接点型号。选配时，确认接头几何、压缩尺寸和后续绝缘边界。

Equipment power-distribution branches设备电源分配支路

Select power branches from actual loads, circuit protection and route length. Include termination, retention and service positions in the wiring definition. Match the welder, tooling and process window to the actual material combination.供电分支以实际负载、回路保护和线长为选配依据，端头、固定和检修位置也需列入布线定义。选配时，焊机、工装及工艺窗口须对应实际材料组合。

Battery and removable power connections电池与可拆电源连接

Define polarity, continuous and peak load, and permitted connection conditions. Record auxiliary signals separately from the main power path. Define pull, resistance and cross-section checks for the project.电池连接要明确极性、持续与峰值负载以及允许插拔条件，辅助信号回路与主电源路径应分别记录。选配时，按项目确定拉力、电阻及截面检查方法。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Record conductor materials, individual branch areas and the welding combination.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，记录导体材质、各支路截面和焊接组合。

Vehicle control-module leads车辆控制模块引线

Separate interface cavities, power circuits and signal circuits at controller leads. Set branch dimensions from the module and mounting-point positions. Confirm joint geometry, compressed dimensions and subsequent insulation boundaries.控制模块尾线需要清楚区分接口腔位、电源和信号回路；分支尺寸应对应模块与固定点的位置。选配时，确认接头几何、压缩尺寸和后续绝缘边界。

Does a joint photograph establish ultrasonic processing?焊点照片能确认使用超声波工艺吗？

A joint photo does not establish ultrasonic welding. Record conductor materials, individual branch areas and the welding combination.焊点照片不能证明超声波工艺。记录导体材质、各支路截面和焊接组合。

Can copper-aluminium combinations use the copper-wire welding process?铜铝组合可以沿用铜线焊接工艺吗？

Copper/aluminum or dissimilar joints need dedicated process evidence. Confirm joint geometry, compressed dimensions and subsequent insulation boundaries.铜铝或异材组合需专用工艺依据。确认接头几何、压缩尺寸和后续绝缘边界。

Do weld-quality checks also define insulation protection?焊接质量检查包含绝缘防护吗？

Inspect joint load capability separately from insulation protection. Match the welder, tooling and process window to the actual material combination.接头负载和绝缘防护分开检验。焊机、工装及工艺窗口须对应实际材料组合。
