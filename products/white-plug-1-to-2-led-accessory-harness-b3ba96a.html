PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

What is the difference between a pigtail and a branch harness?尾线和分支线束有什么区别？

A pigtail usually has a connector or terminal system at one end and free or prepared leads at the other. A branch harness divides one or more circuits into two or more controlled tails. Some products combine both structures.尾线通常一端带连接器或端子系统，另一端为开口或已处理引线；分支线束把一个或多个回路分成两条以上受控尾端，有些产品会同时包含两种结构。

What information is needed for a quotation?报价需要提供哪些资料？

Provide connector and mating part numbers, pin table, branch drawing with datums, each tail length, conductor sizes, loads, splice and sealing requirements, environment, quantity and inspections.建议提供连接器与对插件型号、针脚表、带基准的分支图、各尾端长度、导体规格、负载、接点与密封要求、环境、数量和检验项目。

Can you reproduce a pigtail from a sample?可以依据样品复制尾线吗？

Yes, but hidden pin routes, conductor ratings, plating, internal splices and acceptance criteria still require confirmation. A sample is evidence, not a complete specification.可以，但隐藏针脚、导体额定、镀层、内部接点与验收标准仍需确认。样品是开发依据，不是完整规格。
