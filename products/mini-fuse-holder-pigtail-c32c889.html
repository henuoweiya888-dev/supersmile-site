CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the mini fuse holder pigtail form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以迷你保险丝座尾线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Fuse-holder connection section保险座连接部位

The assembly includes a fuse-holder connection section, separating supply, protected-side routing and service access in the layout.组件包含保险座相关连接部位，可将供电、受保护端和维护接近位置在布局中区分。

02

Pigtail connection form尾线连接形式

A lead section extends behind the connector, allowing length, further termination and support to follow the equipment connection.插接端后保留引线段，便于按设备接线位置确定长度、后续端接与支撑。

03

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

04

Protection and control circuits保护与控制回路

Define loads, control logic, protection-device positions and service access before conductor and branch selection. Housing shape alone does not establish ratings or control functions.先核对负载、控制逻辑、保护器件位置与维修可达性，再安排线径和分支。不能仅凭外壳形状判断保护额定或控制功能。

05

Interface and function review接口与功能确认

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Fuse holders and protected branches保险座与受保护支路

Review the fuse, holder and conductor together. Match fault conditions and time-current requirements to the system design. Specify fuse brand, full type, part number and required ratings.保护支路需将保险丝、保险座和导体一起审核；故障条件与时间电流依据应对应系统设计。选配时，指定熔断件品牌、完整类型、料号和所需额定。

Interfaces within engine-bay layouts发动机舱内的接口布置

Map heat sources, fluid exposure and service routes together. Allow assembly space around connector ends and cable supports. Select the holder and contacts for actual wire size and mounting conditions.发动机舱连接要把热源、油液暴露和检修路径放在同一张布线图中，端头与固定点也需留出装配空间。选配时，保险座和端子按实际线径与安装状态选型。

Equipment power-distribution branches设备电源分配支路

Select power branches from actual loads, circuit protection and route length. Include termination, retention and service positions in the wiring definition. Review interrupting capacity and time-current behavior against system fault conditions.供电分支以实际负载、回路保护和线长为选配依据，端头、固定和检修位置也需列入布线定义。选配时，用系统故障条件审核开断能力及时间电流特性。

Battery and removable power connections电池与可拆电源连接

Define polarity, continuous and peak load, and permitted connection conditions. Record auxiliary signals separately from the main power path. Define load, ambient temperature, conductors and branch protection together.电池连接要明确极性、持续与峰值负载以及允许插拔条件，辅助信号回路与主电源路径应分别记录。选配时，负载、环境温度、导体与支路防护共同确定。

Relays and auxiliary power ends继电器与辅助供电端

Separate coil and load circuits at relay leads. Define terminals, conductor size and protection devices together from the schematic. Specify fuse brand, full type, part number and required ratings.继电器引线需要区分线圈与负载回路，端子定义、线径和保护元件由原理图共同确定。选配时，指定熔断件品牌、完整类型、料号和所需额定。

Cabinet service and electrical checks控制柜检修与电气核对

Service work needs traceable circuits, contacts and wire labels. Agree the measurement method and link the results to acceptance records. Select the holder and contacts for actual wire size and mounting conditions.控制柜检修需要能追踪回路、接点与线号，约定测量方法后再把结果对应到验收记录。选配时，保险座和端子按实际线径与安装状态选型。
