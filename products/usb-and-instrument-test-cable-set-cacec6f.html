Conductor and insulation selection导体与绝缘选配

Select conductors and insulation from circuit loads, diameter limits, bending and exposure. A material name does not replace ratings or validation results.从电路负载、外径限制、弯曲方式与环境条件选择导体和绝缘体系；材料名称不应直接代替额定参数或验证结果。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Can ordinary copper wire replace compensation wire?普通铜线可以替代补偿导线吗？

Ordinary copper wire cannot replace specified compensating cable. Record measurement signal, range, bandwidth and noise targets.普通铜线不能替代指定补偿导线。记录测量信号、量程、带宽及噪声目标。

Can a low-noise designation be treated as a measurement result?低噪声名称能直接作为测量结果吗？

A low-noise label needs measurement conditions. Define pairs, shielding, capacitance and leakage for the instrument input.电缆低噪声名称需有测量条件。线对、屏蔽、电容与漏电要求按仪器输入定义。

Can a shield be connected freely to the instrument reference ground?屏蔽可以任意接到仪器参考地吗？

Do not arbitrarily connect shields to instrument reference ground. Confirm special conductors or compensation wire with exact material documents.屏蔽与仪器参考地不得随意并接。特殊导体或补偿导线须用准确材料文件确认。
