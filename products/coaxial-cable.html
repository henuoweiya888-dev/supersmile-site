Selection & Project Review

01 Application Boundary

System impedance, frequency, loss, power, cable construction, connector interface, termination and validation must be defined together.

02 Project Inputs

Provide equipment ports, impedance, frequency band, signal or power, length, cable limits, route, environment and test evidence.

03 Release Records

Release records cable and connector part numbers, impedance, length, strip and termination controls, labels, environment and agreed electrical evidence.

04 Interface Review

Confirm the exact mating interface, pin map, dimensions and installation orientation.

05 Sample Acceptance

Agree the sample configuration and electrical, mechanical or environmental checks required by the project.

06 Change Control

Record changes to materials, drawings and process instructions before repeat manufacture.

Send the part number, drawing or sample to confirm the replacement configuration and sourcing plan.
