Selection & Project Review

01 Application Boundary

SAE J1128, the ISO 19642 series and customer specifications define different construction and validation frameworks. The exact type and revision must be identified for each project.

02 Project Inputs

Provide current, duty, voltage, route length, temperature zones, fluids, abrasion, terminal system, colour, marking and required cable specification.

03 Release Records

Release records the exact cable source and designation, conductor and insulation criteria, colour and marking, cut and strip dimensions, terminal pairing and agreed harness validation.

04 Interface Review

Confirm the exact mating interface, pin map, dimensions and installation orientation.

05 Sample Acceptance

Agree the sample configuration and electrical, mechanical or environmental checks required by the project.

06 Change Control

Record changes to materials, drawings and process instructions before repeat manufacture.

Send the part number, drawing or sample to confirm the replacement configuration and sourcing plan.
