PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Can similar-looking Molex families be interchanged?外形接近的Molex系列能互换吗？

Do not assume interchangeability between similar-looking series. Provide the series and full housing and mating-part numbers.近似外形系列不可默认互换。提供系列名称及壳体、对插端完整料号。

Why check polarization when the position count matches?针数相同为什么还要核对极化？

Equal pin count does not establish equal polarization. Match contact gender, material and plating to the specific contact documents.针数相同不代表极化相同。接点公母、材料与镀层按具体端子文件配套。

Can a single-contact rating define the complete harness rating?单接点额定能直接作为整条线束额定吗？

Review single-contact ratings separately from loaded multiposition housings. Check polarization, latch and access rather than pitch alone.单接点额定与整壳多路负载分别核对。核对防误插、卡扣和操作空间，而不只看间距。
