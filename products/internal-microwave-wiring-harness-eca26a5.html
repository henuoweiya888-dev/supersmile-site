APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Equipment power-distribution branches设备电源分配支路

Select power branches from actual loads, circuit protection and route length. Include termination, retention and service positions in the wiring definition. Check current documents for the specified AWM style or other wire standard.供电分支以实际负载、回路保护和线长为选配依据，端头、固定和检修位置也需列入布线定义。选配时，按指定AWM式样或其他线材标准核对有效文件。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Match conductor gauge, stranding and plating to the termination process.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，导体线规、绞合和镀层须匹配端接工艺。

Conductor construction and termination fit导体结构与端接适配

Match conductor area and stranding to load and terminal limits. Insulation diameter also affects tooling, seals and cable retention. Tie voltage, temperature and insulation construction to the exact wire model.导体截面与股线结构需要对应实际负载和端子范围，绝缘外径还会影响工具、密封与夹持选择。选配时，电压、温度与绝缘层结构对应准确线材型号。

Relays and auxiliary power ends继电器与辅助供电端

Separate coil and load circuits at relay leads. Define terminals, conductor size and protection devices together from the schematic. Include flame-test method, marking and traceability in material acceptance.继电器引线需要区分线圈与负载回路，端子定义、线径和保护元件由原理图共同确定。选配时，阻燃方法、印字和追溯信息列入材料验收要求。

Routing boundaries around hot equipment热区设备的布线边界

Separate continuous temperature, peaks and exposure time around hot equipment. Review wire, terminals and protective parts against their own limits. Check current documents for the specified AWM style or other wire standard.热区布线需要区分连续温度、峰值和暴露时间，线材、端子及保护件应按各自限制评审。选配时，按指定AWM式样或其他线材标准核对有效文件。

Display-module and control-board connections显示模块与控制板连接

Check insertion direction, contact orientation and frame clearance at display modules. Mark fixed folds on the assembly drawing. Match conductor gauge, stranding and plating to the termination process.显示模块连接需核对插入方向、触点朝向与边框空间，并将固定折弯的位置标入装配图。选配时，导体线规、绞合和镀层须匹配端接工艺。
