CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Consider the sensing element, leads and instrument as one measurement chain, separating the visible connection form from measurement configuration defined for the project.把感温元件、引线与仪表作为同一测量链路考虑；以下区分可见连接形式与需在项目中定义的测量配置。

01

Miniature plug-and-socket ends微型双端插接

Miniature mating ends let the temperature extension be arranged as a removable section. Define mating and polarity from complete interface documents.两端设置微型插接端头，便于把测温延长段作为独立线段安排安装和拆卸。对插与极性按完整接口资料确认。

02

Continuous fine-cable section连续细线连接段

A continuous fine-cable section links the ends. Set length, supports and bends behind the ends around sensor and instrument positions.两端之间是一段连续细线，可围绕传感器到仪表的位置安排长度、固定和端头后方的弯曲空间。

03

Defined materials through the measurement path测量材料的连续定义

Define connector-contact and cable materials together within the thermocouple chain, including material transitions and the reference junction as well as end form.连接器触点与线材材料需一起纳入热电偶链路定义，避免只确认端头外形而遗漏材料转换和参考接点。

CONFIGURATION结构与选配

Construction & customization options结构与定制配置

The table describes the construction and available configuration choices. Materials, dimensions, circuits and inspection requirements are agreed for each project.以下说明展示结构与可配置内容。具体材料、尺寸、电路和检验要求按项目确认。

Thermocouple type and connection task热电偶分度与接入任务Record thermocouple type and sensor and instrument-input documents. Distinguish probe leads, extension leads and compensating sections. Use the matching material system for Type K; yellow appearance alone does not identify it.记录热电偶分度、传感器与仪表输入资料，区分探头引线、延长线与补偿段。K型项目按对应材料体系定义，不以黄色外观单独认定。
Polarity and channel mapping正负极与通道对应Define positive and negative leads, interface positions and channel IDs at each end. Confirm connector-contact materials and the convention used for polarity marks.逐端定义正负极、接口位置与测量通道编号，核对连接器触点材料和极性标识采用的规范。
Extension and compensating materials延长与补偿导线材料Select specified materials from the measuring junction to the reference junction for the thermocouple type and each temperature zone. Do not directly substitute ordinary copper for specified thermocouple extension or compensating material.从测温结点到参考接点的路径按分度与各段温度选择规定材料，不直接用普通铜线替代指定热电偶延长或补偿材料。
Reference junction and cold-junction compensation参考接点与冷端补偿Define material-transition locations, the reference-junction temperature zone and the instrument cold-junction compensation arrangement. Include connectors, terminal blocks and cabinet temperature differences in the measurement-chain review.明确异种材料转换位置、参考接点所在温区及仪表的冷端补偿安排；接头、端子排与柜内温差一并纳入测量链路评审。
Sensing junction and installation测温结点与安装方式Confirm junction position, exposed or sheathed construction, and grounding or isolation requirements against sensing contact, response needs and the actual installation.确认结点位置、露出或护套结构、接地或隔离要求，并与测点接触、响应要求及实际安装结构对应。
Length, insulation and thermal-zone route线长、绝缘与热区路径Define lengths, materials and temperature zones separately for probe and extension sections. Confirm bending, supports and strain relief; braided appearance does not establish insulation material or temperature rating.分别定义探头段和延长段长度、材料及温区，确认弯曲、固定和端头防拉扯；编织外观不代表已确认的绝缘材料或温度等级。
Sample and measurement acceptance样品与测量验收范围Check materials, polarity, connection maps and dimensions in the sample. If temperature comparison or calibration is required, agree equipment, method, measurement-chain scope and reporting responsibility separately.样品核对物料、极性、连接表与尺寸；如需温度比对或校准，另约定设备、方法、整条测量链路范围与报告责任。
APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These directions cover temperature sensing points, thermal zones and instruments. Define sensor type, temperature exposure and measurement acceptance for the specific equipment project.以下为围绕温度测点、热区与仪表的连接方向。传感器类型、使用温区及测量验收按具体设备项目确认。

Temperature sensing around thermal equipment热工设备外围测温

Around thermal equipment, select lead construction from the actual sensing zone, radiant heat and support route. Thermocouple type, junction form and instrument input define the measurement chain; insulation temperature capability does not establish the probe measurement range.热工设备外围的测温引线需按实际测点温区、热辐射与固定路径选择结构。热电偶分度、结点形式和仪表输入共同定义测量链路，不能把引线绝缘的耐温直接当作探头量程。

Laboratory furnaces and controlled chambers实验烘炉与温控腔体

Temperature connections for furnaces or controlled chambers need separate definitions for the sensing point, cable passage and external instrument area. Select extension or compensating materials for each section and record the reference-junction position within the system.烘炉或温控腔体的测温连接需区分腔内测点、穿出位置与腔外仪表区。按各段温度和接线方式安排延长或补偿材料，把参考接点位置纳入系统记录。

Heated surfaces and local sensing points加热表面与局部测点

For contact sensing on heated surfaces, define sensing position, junction contact and lead retention so cable loads do not disturb the contact. Confirm thermal response, grounding and electrical isolation from the probe and complete equipment design.加热表面的接触测温需先确定测点位置、结点接触方式和引线固定，避免引线受力改变接触状态。热惯性、接地与电气隔离按探头及整机设计确认。

Temperature recorders and acquisition instruments温度记录与采集仪器

When connecting thermocouples to recorders or acquisition instruments, align sensor-type settings, polarity and channel IDs. Define cold-junction compensation through the instrument and connection arrangement, then configure the lead for the approved measurement channel.热电偶连接记录仪或采集仪器时，分度设置、正负极性与通道编号需要一致。冷端补偿由仪表与接线结构共同定义，线缆只按确认后的测量通道进行配置。

Appliance temperature-control connections家电温控模块连接

For appliance projects using thermocouples, define the sensor, temperature-control module and removable interfaces separately. Plan materials, retention and junction requirements for leads passing through heated areas, the enclosure and service positions from equipment documents.家电采用热电偶测温的项目，需分别确认传感器、温控模块及可拆接口。引线经过加热区、机壳和维修位置时，各段材料、固定和接点要求按设备资料安排。

Thermal-zone feedback in laboratory equipment实验装置温区反馈

Where laboratory equipment uses thermocouple feedback, map thermal zones to sensing points and acquisition channels, allowing module removal and clear end identification. Sampling, compensation and temperature conversion are system functions; a cable set alone does not establish measurement accuracy.实验装置如采用热电偶反馈，可把多个温区按测点与采集通道对应，预留模块拆装和端头识别空间。采样、补偿和温度换算由系统定义，线组不单独代表整机测量精度。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Can an extension section use ordinary copper wire?热电偶延长段可以直接换普通铜线吗？

Use the specified materials for the thermocouple type and temperature zones between the measuring and reference junctions. Connections beyond the reference junction follow the instrument design; do not replace sections arbitrarily with copper.从测温结点到参考接点的路径需使用与分度及温区相符的规定材料。参考接点之后的连接由仪表设计决定，不能任意在原测量路径中改换铜线。

Do yellow ends establish Type K and polarity?黄色接头是否足以确认K型与正负极？

Also check material specifications, contact materials, polarity marks and the color-code convention. Appearance aids identification but does not replace the thermocouple type and polarity map.还需核对物料规范、触点材料、极性标识和所用色标规则。外观只帮助识别，不能代替分度与正负极连接表。

Does an extension lead provide cold-junction compensation?延长线本身是否包含冷端补偿？

The lead connects the specified materials with the correct polarity. The instrument and connection arrangement define the reference junction and cold-junction compensation, which need review with the sensor, terminations and temperature zones.延长线负责按规定材料和极性连接。参考接点与冷端补偿由仪表和接线结构定义，需连同传感器、端接和温区共同审核。

Start with sensing points and instrument inputs从测点与仪表输入开始

Share sensor and instrument models, sensing temperature zones, connection maps, mounting and target lengths. For RTDs, include the wiring scheme; for thermocouples, include type, materials and reference-junction requirements.提供传感器及仪表型号、测点温区、连接表、安装方式和目标长度；温阻补充线制，热电偶补充分度、材料与参考接点要求。
