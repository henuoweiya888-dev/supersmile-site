ENGINEERING OVERVIEW工程概览

Defined connections for surface process equipment为地面工艺设备定义每一处连接

Oil and gas equipment uses different cable duties within the same facility: a pump motor needs a power connection, while a pressure transmitter, valve-position switch or control solenoid may use a separate instrument or control circuit. A useful assembly is therefore defined by the exact equipment interface, cable construction, route and maintenance boundary. This page focuses on surface process equipment and packaged skids. Inquiries for automotive fuel harnesses, downhole cables, subsea umbilicals or power-transmission projects can be reviewed separately against their own requirements. We review each inquiry against its drawings, component data sheets and site requirements. Hazardous-area suitability, ingress protection and any claimed test result require evidence for the specified complete installation.

同一油气设施内会有不同电缆任务：泵电机需要供电连接，压力变送器、阀位开关或控制电磁阀则可能分别使用仪表或控制回路。因此，组件应由准确的设备接口、线缆结构、布线路径和维护边界共同定义。本页聚焦地面工艺设备与成套撬装；汽车燃油线束、井下电缆、海底脐带缆或输电项目也可按各自要求另行评估。每项询盘须依据图纸、元件数据表及现场要求审核。危险区域适用性、防护等级和任何测试结果，都须有针对指定完整安装状态的证据。

01

POWER + INSTRUMENTATION

供电与仪表

Equipment circuits设备回路

Pump power and auxiliary control are separate duties

泵组供电与辅助控制是不同任务

A pump skid may place motor power, temperature monitoring, valve control and local indications close together, but each path has its own voltage, load, conductor and termination limits. Map outgoing, return and protective-earth conductors from the approved equipment drawing. Then identify the smaller control and instrument circuits separately. Motor start and fault behavior can differ from steady running; the selected cable, protective device and terminals must be assessed as a complete circuit rather than inferred from a generic ‘oilfield cable’ label.

泵撬上可能同时布置电机供电、温度监测、阀门控制与就地指示，但每条路径的电压、负载、导体和端接限制不同。先从获认可的设备图中理清去程、回程及保护接地，再单独列出较小的控制与仪表回路。电机启动和故障工况可能与稳态运行不同，线缆、保护器件与端子应作为完整回路审核，不能凭“油田电缆”这一通称推断适用性。

Instrument leads begin at the device terminals

仪表引线从设备端子定义

Pressure, temperature, flow and position devices may use different analog, discrete or digital interfaces. Record the exact transmitter or switch model, supply requirement, terminal polarity, signal pair and permitted cable entry before choosing the lead. For a 4–20 mA or HART loop, follow the instrument manufacturer's wiring and shield instructions; a similar connector shell does not establish the same pinout or measurement behavior. Keep the instrument path and power path identifiable from the field device to the control end.

压力、温度、流量和位置器件可能分别采用模拟量、开关量或数字接口。选线前应记录准确的变送器或开关型号、供电要求、端子极性、信号线对及允许进线方式。4–20 mA 或 HART 回路应遵循仪表厂商的接线与屏蔽要求；接头外壳相似不能证明针脚或测量行为相同。仪表路径与动力路径从现场设备到控制端都应可辨。

02

CIRCUIT + SERVICE

回路与维护

Connection solutions连接解决方案

Keep power, control and measurement pin maps distinct

动力、控制与测量针脚表分别核对

Start with an endpoint schedule: device part number, mating connector, terminal number, conductor identity, shield path and cable length. A valve actuator may provide separate power and control terminals, while a transmitter has its own loop connections. Where one route carries more than one circuit, define insulation, segregation, shield termination and grounding from the equipment designer's documents. A continuity pass confirms a wire path only; it does not prove signal performance or the safety of the installed equipment.

先建立两端接口清单：设备料号、对插件、端子号、导体标识、屏蔽路径与线长。阀门执行机构可能分设动力和控制端子，变送器又有自己的回路接点。若一条路径容纳多种回路，应依据设备设计文件定义绝缘、分隔、屏蔽端接与接地。导通检查只确认导线路径，不能证明信号性能或安装后设备的安全性。

Design a serviceable equipment-side section

为现场设备定义可维护的连接段

A replaceable lead can end at a defined instrument, valve or junction-box interface, provided the mating part and wiring are specified. Mark each end so a maintenance technician can identify the circuit before disconnecting it. Branch length must allow access without leaving a loop where it can snag or rest against hot or moving equipment. A substitute connector is not accepted by appearance alone; check the exact contacts, seal, cable diameter, rating and pin map against the approved configuration.

可更换引线可以止于明确的仪表、阀门或接线箱接口，前提是对插件与接线已被定义。两端标识应让维护人员在拆卸前辨认回路。分支需留出操作空间，也不能形成会被钩挂或贴靠高温、运动部件的多余线圈。替代接头不能凭外观认可，须逐项核对触点、密封件、电缆外径、额定值及针脚表。

03

MATERIAL + ENTRY

材料与进线

Materials and interfaces线材与附件

Choose a jacket for the named oil and exposure

护套应对应具体油液与暴露条件

Oil mist, hydraulic fluid, cleaning agents, sunlight, abrasion and ambient temperature are different exposures. PVC, PUR and other jacket families contain many formulations; a material name alone cannot establish resistance to a specified fluid. Match the cable manufacturer's data to the actual medium, concentration, duration, temperature and installation method. Where a cable lies in a tray or conduit, check the required cable construction and site rules rather than borrowing ratings from a different product line.

油雾、液压油、清洗剂、日晒、磨耗及环境温度是不同暴露条件。PVC、PUR 等护套类别各有多种配方，材料名称本身不能证明对指定液体的耐受。应把线缆厂商数据与实际介质、浓度、接触时长、温度和敷设方式对应。若电缆进入桥架或导管，还须核对现场规定的结构与安装要求，不能套用另一系列产品的额定值。

Cable entries, seals and strain relief work as one interface

进线、密封与应力释放是同一个接口问题

A gland must match the enclosure thread, cable outside diameter, seal material and installation torque. The first bend and clamp position should not put pull or vibration directly into instrument terminals. Heat-shrink or a boot may protect a branch, but neither creates a complete-equipment ingress rating by appearance. For any classified hazardous location, the site designer must select and document the appropriate equipment and installation method under the applicable requirements; a generic cable gland or jacket cannot make an assembly Ex-approved.

格兰头须匹配箱体螺纹、电缆外径、密封材料和安装扭矩。首次弯折与夹持位置应避免拉力或振动直接传到仪表端子。热缩管或尾套可以保护分支，却不能凭外观形成整机防护等级。若安装点属于已划分的危险区域，须由现场设计方按适用要求选择并记录设备及安装方式；普通格兰头或护套不能让组件自动成为防爆产品。

04

SURFACE EQUIPMENT

地面设备

Industries and applications行业与应用

Surface pump stations and metering skids

地面泵站与计量撬装

Packaged surface equipment brings motors, valves, junction boxes and instruments into a compact route. The cable assembly may need short equipment tails, defined branch points and clear identification on both sides of the skid boundary. Confirm where the vendor package ends and site wiring begins; those interfaces drive connector selection, spare length and document ownership. A preterminated section can reduce field terminations when the package layout and acceptance plan are agreed before manufacture.

地面成套设备把电机、阀门、接线箱和仪表布置在紧凑范围内。电缆组件可能需要短设备尾线、明确的分支基准以及撬内外一致的标识。先确认设备供方范围与现场布线范围的交界，才能确定接头、检修余量及文件归属。若包装布局和验收计划在制作前明确，预端接段可减少现场接线工作。

Valve actuators and process instruments

阀门执行机构与工艺仪表

An electric actuator may separate power, position indication and command circuits inside its terminal compartment. A nearby pressure transmitter or limit switch adds its own cable-entry and polarity rules. Do not merge these into one pin map because the devices sit on the same pipe. Review each manufacturer's wiring document, mechanical entry, environmental rating and required grounding path, then define the cable tails as a matched set of equipment-side interfaces.

电动执行机构的接线腔内可能把动力、阀位反馈和命令回路分开；邻近的压力变送器或限位开关又各有进线与极性要求。它们安装在同一管段，也不能合并成一张未经区分的针脚表。应分别审核厂商接线文件、机械进线、环境额定与接地路径，再把各段引线定义成相互匹配的设备端接口。

05

ROUTE + IDENTIFICATION

路径与标识

Installation and service现场安装与维护

Predefine routing, labels and service access

预先定义路径、标识与检修空间

A useful build drawing specifies length datums, branch directions, clip points, cable tags and the first bend after each rigid termination. Route away from hot surfaces, moving mechanisms and sharp edges, using the selected cable maker's bend limits. Leave access to a gland or connector that may need inspection, but do not leave loose cable where it can be caught by equipment. Labels should identify the circuit and destination in the site's own naming scheme; generic wire colors are not a maintenance plan.

可执行的装配图应给出长度基准、分支方向、固定点、线标及刚性端接后的首次弯折位置。布线须避开热表面、运动机构和锐边，并遵循所选线缆厂商的弯曲限制。格兰头或接头要留出检查空间，多余电缆也不能松散地留在设备旁。标签应采用现场自己的回路和目的地命名，通用线色不能替代维护标识。

PROJECT EVIDENCE

项目证据

Project review项目审核

Release the specified assembly, not a generic promise

放行指定组件，不做笼统承诺

Before production, agree on the component list, approved drawing, pin map, dimensions, labels and inspection points. Continuity and polarity checks can establish basic wiring; insulation, shield, pull, ingress or functional testing require project-defined methods and limits. Record what was actually checked and against which revision. If the intended installation is in a classified hazardous area, the equipment and installation assessment belongs to the responsible design authority under the applicable rules; this product page makes no Ex, ATEX, subsea or blanket IP claim.

生产前应确认物料清单、认可图纸、针脚表、尺寸、标识与检验点。导通和极性检查可以确立基本接线；绝缘、屏蔽、拉力、防护或功能试验则要有项目定义的方法与限值。记录实际检查内容及对应版本。若安装在已划分的危险区域，设备和安装评估应由责任设计方按适用规则完成；本页面不宣称整类产品取得 Ex、ATEX、海底使用或统一 IP 等级。

Common questions常见问题

Does an oil-resistant cable withstand every petroleum fluid?耐油电缆能接触所有石油类液体吗？

No. Resistance depends on the exact jacket compound, named fluid, concentration, temperature and exposure time. Compare the chosen cable data sheet with the site conditions before approval.

不能。耐受性取决于准确的护套配方、介质、浓度、温度和接触时长；批准前要把所选线缆数据表与现场条件逐项对应。

Can these assemblies be installed in a hazardous area?这类组件可以安装在危险区域吗？

The site classification and applicable rules determine the equipment and installation requirements. A cable, gland or connector selected in isolation does not approve the finished assembly or plant installation for an Ex area.

现场分区和适用规则决定设备及安装要求。单独选用某款电缆、格兰头或接头，并不使成品组件或现场安装自动获得防爆适用性。

Can power and transmitter signals share one cable route?动力线和变送器信号线可以走同一路径吗？

Only after the equipment designer reviews circuit separation, cable construction, shielding, grounding and site installation rules. Similar routing does not mean the circuits should share a jacket or connector.

须由设备设计方审核回路分隔、线缆结构、屏蔽、接地及现场敷设规则；路径接近不等于应共用护套或接头。

What information is needed for a replacement equipment lead?按现有设备尾线制作替换件需要什么资料？

Provide the equipment and cable part numbers or a sample, both endpoint drawings, circuit and pin maps, length and branch dimensions, environmental conditions, site restrictions and acceptance criteria.

请提供设备与电缆料号或样品、两端接口图、回路与针脚表、长度和分支尺寸、环境条件、现场限制及验收准则。
