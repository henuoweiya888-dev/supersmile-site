CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Conductor, insulation and material choices导体、绝缘与选材要点

01

Circular connection end圆形连接端

The end uses a circular interface form, with connection positions planned around mating direction, retention and cable exit.端头具有圆形接口形式，可围绕对插方向、端部固定和线缆出口规划连接位置。

02

End transition layout端头转接与组合

The assembly organizes a transition or combined connection, with end directions, transition sections and fixing arranged around the equipment positions.组件以转接或组合形式组织连接，可围绕两端设备位置安排端头方向、过渡段与固定。

03

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

04

Coiled or spiral form卷绕或螺旋形态

The cable is arranged in a coiled or spiral form, with straight tails and ends placed for storage or connection positions.线缆以卷绕或螺旋形态排列，直线尾段与端头可以结合收纳或连接位置安排。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

External interfaces on portable equipment便携设备的外部接口

Consider handling, plug grip and cable bends together. Align the exit direction with the device position during use. Confirm fine-strand construction, gauge and supply model from material documents.便携设备连接要同时考虑操作手势、插头握持和尾部弯曲空间，线缆出口方向应与实际使用姿态协调。选配时，细股结构、线规与来源型号须由材料文件确认。

Routing and retention in confined spaces狭小空间的布线与固定

Define bends, retention points and assembly order in confined spaces. Do not compensate for an incorrect interface direction or length with forced folds. Select jacket material, hardness and diameter for installation and termination.狭小空间布线需明确转弯、固定点和拆装顺序，不能用强行折线弥补接口方向或长度误差。选配时，护套材质、硬度和外径按安装与端接选择。

Routing and retention on moving equipment移动设备的走线与固定

Define travel, speed and retention at both ends of moving connections. Set separate targets and validation conditions for bending and torsion. Define bending or torsional conditions and the corresponding cycle target.移动连接需明确动作行程、速度和两端固定，动态弯曲与扭转应分别设定目标和验证条件。选配时，明确弯折或扭转条件及对应循环目标。

Instrument inputs and measurement leads仪器输入与测量引线

Start measurement leads from signal type, input conditions and grounding. Select pairs and shields for the actual measurement range and noise target. Specify strand retention, contact fit and near-end strain protection together.测量引线以信号类型、输入条件和接地方式为起点，线对与屏蔽选择需要对应实际量程和噪声目标。选配时，细股保持、端子适配与近端应力保护共同规定。

Cable exits and nearby support电缆出口与近端支撑

Keep bending loads away from contacts through appropriate rear support. Review exit angle, retention distance and boot dimensions with the enclosure. Confirm fine-strand construction, gauge and supply model from material documents.端头后方的支撑要避免把弯折载荷直接传到接点，出线角度、固定距离和尾套尺寸需与机壳一起评审。选配时，细股结构、线规与来源型号须由材料文件确认。

Robot-arm and moving-module connections机械臂与移动模块连接

Coordinate cables on moving modules with joint motion and tool clearance. Review cable type and cycle targets against the actual movement profile. Select jacket material, hardness and diameter for installation and termination.移动模块的线缆安排需与关节动作和工具空间协调，线缆型号与循环目标应按实际运动谱评审。选配时，护套材质、硬度和外径按安装与端接选择。

Does a soft feel establish continuous-motion capability?手感柔软能证明持续运动额定吗？

Soft feel does not establish continuous-motion ratings. Confirm fine-strand construction, gauge and supply model from material documents.手感柔软不等于持续运动额定。细股结构、线规与来源型号须由材料文件确认。

Are bending, carrier motion and torsion the same duty?弯折、拖链和扭转是同一工况吗？

Confirm bending, drag-chain and robot torsion duties separately. Select jacket material, hardness and diameter for installation and termination.弯折、拖链和机器人扭转分开确认。护套材质、硬度和外径按安装与端接选择。

Can fine-strand conductors use any crimp process?细股导体可以沿用任意压接工艺吗？

Match fine-strand conductors to terminals and crimp process. Define bending or torsional conditions and the corresponding cycle target.细股导体需匹配端子和压接工艺。明确弯折或扭转条件及对应循环目标。
