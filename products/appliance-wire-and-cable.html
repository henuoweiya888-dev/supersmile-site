Internal wiring, AWM, flexible cord and the completed appliance have different specification and approval boundaries. The exact construction and use conditions must be identified.

02 Project Inputs

Provide the destination market, appliance zone map, circuit loads, temperatures, exposure, movement, wire or cord designation, terminal system and validation plan.

03 Release Requirements

Release records the exact wire or cord, conductor and insulation construction, marking, termination, routing, protection, traceability and agreed assembly checks.

04 Mating & Termination

Identify the mating part number and termination method before confirming the assembly drawing.

05 Sample Review

Compare the sample with the approved pin map, dimensions and material list.
