CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the idc & ffc terminated ribbon cable assembly set form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以IDC与FFC端子排线组件组的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Terminal-block connection form端子排接入形式

Leads gather at terminal-block or wiring-board connection points, allowing circuits to be organized by terminal number.多根引线汇集到端子排或接线板连接位置，便于按端子编号组织不同回路。

02

Terminal connection section端子接入部位

Leads use terminal-form ends at the connection points. Orientation, fixing and insulation coverage can follow the equipment assembly.引线以端子形式接入相应连接位置，可按设备装配方式安排端头方向、固定和绝缘覆盖。

03

Flat conductor arrangement扁平排列结构

The cable uses a flat arrangement, allowing compact routing around insertion direction, folds and end orientation.线缆采用扁平排列形态，可围绕插入方向、折转位置和端头朝向安排紧凑布线。

04

Grouped cable-set form组合线组形式

Several connection pieces are organized as a cable set, allowing each section to be assigned and identified by assembly sequence and module.多段连接件以线组形式组织，可按装配顺序和设备模块划分各段用途与标识。

05

Conductor layout and orientation排布与安装方向

Confirm conductor spacing, sequence, bend direction and insertion face to prevent reversed mapping. Plan fixing and abrasion protection around the available installation space.确认导体间距、排列顺序、弯折方向和插入面，避免翻转造成针位错误。扁平结构的固定与防磨需要结合设备空间安排。
