Wideband Sensor Harness宽域传感器连接线束

Wideband Sensor Harness can be configured around equipment interfaces and routing. Instrument and sensor harnesses connect sensing elements, transmitters, gauges, acquisition modules and controllers. They may carry low-level analog signals, digital communication, excitation power or mixed circuits.宽域传感器连接线束可围绕设备接口与安装路径进行配置。仪表与传感器线束连接传感元件、变送器、仪表、采集模块和控制器，可能承载低电平模拟信号、数字通信、激励电源或混合回路。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Test and measurement equipment测试与测量设备

Bench instruments, DAQ modules, test fixtures, reference sensors and replaceable test leads. Distinguish analog, switching and digital outputs by sensor model.台式仪表、DAQ采集模块、测试工装、参考传感器和可更换测试线。选配时，按传感器型号区分模拟量、开关量或数字输出。

Process and packaging machinery过程与包装机械

Photoelectric, proximity, temperature, pressure, position and weighing sensors distributed across a production machine. Define supply, reference ground and signal polarity from device drawings.分布在生产设备中的光电、接近、温度、压力、位置和称重传感器。选配时，供电、参考地和信号极性按设备图定义。

Water, energy and environmental monitoring水处理、能源与环境监测

Level, flow, pressure, conductivity and condition-monitoring instruments installed around pumps, tanks and treatment systems. Use separate wiring definitions for voltage, current, pulse and communication circuits.安装在泵、储罐与处理系统周围的液位、流量、压力、电导率及状态监测仪表。选配时，电压、电流、脉冲与通信回路采用各自接线定义。

Machine tools and motion equipment机床与运动设备

Encoders, limit sensors, tool probes, temperature points and condition-monitoring devices inside machines. Follow the system grounding scheme for shields and instrument references.机床内部的编码器、限位传感器、对刀探头、温度测点与状态监测装置。选配时，屏蔽端接与仪器参考地遵从系统接地方案。

Laboratory and analytical instruments实验室与分析仪器

Internal sensor interconnects, pumps, valves, detectors, acquisition boards and front-panel instruments. Distinguish analog, switching and digital outputs by sensor model.连接内部传感器、泵、阀、检测器、采集板与前面板仪表。选配时，按传感器型号区分模拟量、开关量或数字输出。

Automation and robotic cells自动化与机器人单元

Position, force, vision-trigger, safety and process sensors linked to local I/O and controllers. Define supply, reference ground and signal polarity from device drawings.将位置、力、视觉触发、安全与工艺传感器连接至现场I/O和控制器。选配时，供电、参考地和信号极性按设备图定义。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is an instrument and sensor harness a fixed product model?仪表与传感器线束是固定型号吗？

No. It is a family covering many sensors, instruments, signal types, interfaces and environments. The final harness is defined by the actual measurement system.不是。它覆盖多种传感器、仪表、信号类型、接口与环境，最终结构由真实测量系统定义。

What information is needed for a quotation?报价需要提供哪些资料？

Provide sensor and instrument models, manuals or drawings, signal type, supply, pin map, connector data, route, length, environment, quantity and required inspections.建议提供传感器与仪表型号、手册或图纸、信号类型、供电、针脚表、接口资料、路径、长度、环境、数量和检验要求。

Can you copy an existing sensor cable?可以按现有传感器线缆复制吗？

A sample can define dimensions and visible construction, but signal function, hidden components, compensation material and calibration responsibility must still be confirmed.样品可用于确认尺寸和可见结构，但信号功能、隐藏元件、补偿材料与校准责任仍需确认。
