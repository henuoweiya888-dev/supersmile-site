ENGINEERING OVERVIEW工程概览

Specify the insulation, the cable construction and the evidence separately分别定义交联绝缘、整线结构与验证证据

Cross-linked polyethylene (XLPE) and related cross-linked polyolefin compounds appear in hook-up wire, control cable and power-cable designs. Cross-linking changes the polymer network, but it does not specify conductor size, insulation thickness, jacket, shield, fire behaviour or approval. A useful inquiry starts with circuit voltage and current, installation temperature, routing, movement, surrounding media, termination and the exact standard required. The selected finished cable—not the material name alone—must support the requested duty.

交联聚乙烯（XLPE）及相关交联聚烯烃材料可用于设备引线、控制电缆和电力电缆。交联改变的是聚合物网络，却不能替代导体规格、绝缘厚度、护套、屏蔽、燃烧表现或认证定义。有效的询价应从回路电压和电流、安装温度、敷设路径、运动、接触介质、端接方式与适用标准开始。最终应由选定的成品线缆结构，而非单一材料名称，来满足工况。

What the XLPE name tells you—and what it does not

XLPE 名称能说明什么，不能说明什么

XLPE describes a cross-linked polyethylene insulation compound. Its network tends to retain shape and electrical separation at temperatures where an otherwise comparable thermoplastic may soften, but no universal operating temperature follows from the letters XLPE. A low-voltage hook-up wire, a tray control cable and a screened medium-voltage cable can all use cross-linked insulation while differing radically in wall, shielding, jacket and accessories. Ask for the exact manufacturer's datasheet and approved construction; do not transfer one catalogue's rating to another cable.

XLPE 指交联聚乙烯绝缘配方。其网络结构有助于在较高温度下保持形状和电气隔离，但“XLPE”四个字母并不自动给出统一工作温度。低压设备引线、桥架控制电缆与带屏蔽的中压电缆都可能使用交联绝缘，壁厚、屏蔽、护套及附件却完全不同。应索取准确厂家数据表和获认可的结构，不能把某一本目录的额定值挪到另一条线缆上。

Match conductor, wall and thermal duty

把导体、绝缘壁厚与热工况一起确定

Define copper or aluminium, cross-section or AWG, stranding, circuit length, current and allowable voltage drop before choosing a wire. Continuous conductor temperature, short-duration overload, installation temperature and terminal temperature are separate constraints; bundling and enclosure heat can reduce usable ampacity. An XLPE-insulated core may still be limited by its jacket, connector, seal or adjacent component. Some published products state 90 °C continuous service, while other cross-linked hook-up wires have different ratings—read the rating of the exact cable and installation, never infer it from the polymer label.

选线前要确定铜或铝导体、截面或 AWG、绞合、回路长度、电流及允许压降。导体连续温度、短时过载、安装温度和端子温度是不同约束；成束敷设与机柜热量还会降低可用载流量。即使线芯采用 XLPE，整体仍可能受护套、连接器、密封件或邻近元件限制。有些公开产品标示 90°C 连续使用，其他交联设备线的额定值则不同——应读取准确型号及安装条件，不能只看聚合物名称推定。

CONSTRUCTION + INSTALLATION

结构与安装

Build the complete cable system设计完整电缆系统

The production route and formulation matter

生产路线与配方同样重要

Cross-linking can be achieved by different controlled processes, and compounds may be formulated for different thermal, electrical or mechanical duties. The extrusion photograph illustrates insulation application, not a particular cross-linking process or a Super Smile production line. Project documents should identify the approved cable supplier, compound or part number, insulation thickness, colour coding and traceability. A request for low smoke, halogen-free, oil resistance or flame behaviour needs its own specification and evidence; none is guaranteed merely by choosing XLPE.

交联可通过不同受控工艺实现，材料配方也会针对不同热、电气或机械工况调整。配图只展示绝缘挤出场景，不代表特定交联工艺或超斯迈尔生产线。项目文件应记录已认可的线缆供应商、配方或料号、绝缘厚度、颜色标识与追溯方式。如需低烟、无卤、耐油或阻燃表现，应分别提出标准和证据；这些性能不会因为选用 XLPE 就自动成立。

A sound cable can fail at its end

线缆完好，端接处仍可能失效

The cable cutback, conductor preparation, connector or joint and strain relief form part of the electrical insulation system. Select a terminal for the actual conductor and strand class; preserve required creepage, clearance, screen continuity and sealing in the finished assembly. Heat-shrink or resin around a joint is not a substitute for a qualified accessory and installation method. The photograph shows a low-voltage field joint as an interface-workmanship example, not an approved XLPE joint design for this site or evidence that Super Smile made it.

剥切尺寸、导体处理、连接器或接头与应力消除共同构成电气绝缘系统。端子须匹配实际导体及绞合等级，成品要保持所需爬电距离、电气间隙、屏蔽连续性与密封。接头上的热缩管或树脂不能代替经过确认的附件与安装方法。配图为低压现场接头的工艺场景，不是本站认可的 XLPE 接头设计，也不代表超斯迈尔施工。

EVIDENCE + RELEASE

证据与放行

Release evidence for the actual design为实际结构提供放行证据

Verify the specific cable and the agreed assembly

验证准确线缆型号与约定组件

Approval starts with the identified cable and its relevant standard or customer drawing, not a generic materials list. Check marking, conductor, insulation, wall, colour and termination against the bill of materials; define continuity, insulation resistance, voltage withstand or other tests only where applicable and with agreed methods and limits. The instrument photograph illustrates the type of equipment used for insulation measurements, not a recorded result. Retain lot identity, sample approval, changes and inspection records so a repeat order can reproduce the approved configuration.

放行应从已识别的线缆型号及其适用标准或客户图纸开始，不能只凭一份笼统材料清单。按物料表核对印字、导体、绝缘、壁厚、颜色与端接；仅在适用时，按约定方法和限值规定导通、绝缘电阻、耐压或其他试验。仪器配图只说明绝缘测量设备类型，并非检测结果。保留批次身份、样品确认、变更及检验记录，才能在复单时重现获认可配置。

Common questions常见问题

Does XLPE automatically mean a 90 °C cable?采用 XLPE 就一定是 90°C 电缆吗？

No. Published XLPE products have different ratings. The complete construction, applicable standard, accessories and installed conditions set the usable limit.

不一定。公开的 XLPE 产品额定值并不相同，可用上限由完整结构、适用标准、附件与安装条件共同决定。

Can an XLPE core be used outdoors without further review?XLPE 线芯能不经审核直接用于户外吗？

No. UV, water, jacket, cable entry, support and installation approvals must be checked for the complete cable system.

不能。仍需对整条线缆系统核对紫外、水、护套、进线、支撑与安装许可条件。
