CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the power connector to ring-terminal braided harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以电源接头转环形端子编织线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Ring-terminal end环形端子端头

A ring-terminal end provides a mounting-hole connection. Lead direction, support and insulation coverage can follow the stud position.末端采用带安装孔的环形端子形式，可围绕螺柱位置安排接线方向、端头支撑与绝缘覆盖。

02

End transition layout端头转接与组合

The assembly organizes a transition or combined connection, with end directions, transition sections and fixing arranged around the equipment positions.组件以转接或组合形式组织连接，可围绕两端设备位置安排端头方向、过渡段与固定。

03

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

04

Terminal connection section端子接入部位

Leads use terminal-form ends at the connection points. Orientation, fixing and insulation coverage can follow the equipment assembly.引线以端子形式接入相应连接位置，可按设备装配方式安排端头方向、固定和绝缘覆盖。

05

Braided outer protection编织外层

A braid can organize the bundle and protect its surface. Define its material, coverage and end treatment around the routing path; distinguish insulating braid from metallic shielding.编织层可用于束线与表面防磨；材料、覆盖长度和端部收口需结合安装路径确定。绝缘编织与金属屏蔽编织应分别定义。
