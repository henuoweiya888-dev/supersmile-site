Configuration & Project Review

01 Joint and standard

Distinguish a wire-to-wire branch splice from a wire-to-terminal bond. Agree the validation standard for the actual joint.

02 Exact conductor stack

List every wire material, cross-section, strand construction, surface condition and branch orientation. Treat a changed combination as a review trigger.

03 Wire preparation

Define strip length, cleanliness, strand integrity and the allowed stack arrangement without assuming plated or lubricated wire behaves like bare conductor.

04 Tooling and weld window

Record the machine and tooling identity, force or pressure, amplitude, energy and monitoring limits developed for the actual joint.

05 Branch protection

Review nugget location, insulation clearance, strain relief and the selected protective system together with harness routing.

06 Joint verification

Agree mechanical strength, electrical resistance and environmental conditioning for the joint, with controlled sample preparation and traceable acceptance records.
