Selection & Project Review

01 Application Boundary

A truck harness is a vehicle architecture rather than a generic bundle of wires. Cab and chassis zones have different exposure, service access and mechanical movement. Every released branch therefore links circuit function, load, protection, connector system, route, support spacing, bend allowance, splice position, environmental zone and validation method.

02 Project Inputs

Provide vehicle maker, platform, model year and cab or wheelbase configuration; system voltage; equipment and controller part numbers; circuit and pin tables; load current and protection; connector BOM; zone and routing photographs; branch datums; clips, grommets and coverings; articulation or service loops; body-builder and trailer interfaces; labels; quantity; and the required electrical, mechanical or environmental checks.

03 Release Records

We convert approved vehicle information into controlled drawings, circuit schedules, connector and terminal BOMs, splice maps, route dimensions, covering instructions, labels and test programs. Sample fit is reviewed on the specified vehicle or fixture before repeat production. Continuity and workmanship checks confirm the manufactured harness;

04 Interface Review

Confirm the exact mating interface, pin map, dimensions and installation orientation.

05 Sample Acceptance

Agree the sample configuration and electrical, mechanical or environmental checks required by the project.

06 Change Control

Record changes to materials, drawings and process instructions before repeat manufacture.

Send the part number, drawing or sample to confirm the replacement configuration and sourcing plan.
