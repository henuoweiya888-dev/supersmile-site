ENGINEERING OVERVIEW工程概览

Design the cable, carrier and motion profile as one system把线缆、拖链与运动谱作为一个系统设计

A drag-chain cable is not identified by softness alone. Travel, speed, acceleration, bend radius, cycle count, orientation, fill and environmental exposure create the real duty. The cable carrier controls the path, but it does not remove tensile, torsional or abrasion loads caused by poor installation.

拖链电缆不能只凭“柔软”判断。行程、速度、加速度、弯曲半径、循环次数、安装方向、填充率与环境暴露共同构成真实工况。拖链负责约束路径，但不能消除错误安装造成的拉伸、扭转或磨损。

01

SYSTEM DEFINITION

系统定义

Define the motion envelope定义运动包络

Define the motion before choosing the cable

先定义运动，再选择线缆

Record travel length, cycle rate, acceleration, operating hours, minimum dynamic bend radius and whether the path is horizontal, vertical or unsupported. Continuous flexing and torsion are different loads: a robot wrist, linear axis and festoon system may require different conductor lay and reinforcement. A supplier cycle claim is meaningful only when its radius, speed, travel and installation conditions resemble the project.

应记录行程、循环频率、加速度、工作时长、最小动态弯曲半径，以及路径属于水平、垂直还是悬空。连续弯曲与扭转是两种不同负载；机器人手腕、直线轴和悬挂系统可能需要不同的导体绞合与加强结构。供应商循环寿命数据只有在半径、速度、行程和安装条件接近项目时才有意义。

Fine-stranded conductors need a coordinated core lay

细绞导体还需要协调的缆芯绞合

High strand count can improve flexibility, but conductor class alone does not define flex life. Insulation wall, core lay length, separators, fillers, shield construction and jacket must move together without concentrating strain. Power, servo, encoder, Ethernet and sensor circuits may share a carrier while requiring different pair geometry, shielding and current capacity. Hybrid constructions should be reviewed by circuit rather than described as one generic robot cable.

较高股数可以改善柔性，但导体等级本身不能定义弯曲寿命。绝缘壁厚、缆芯节距、隔离层、填充、屏蔽与护套必须协同运动，避免应变集中。动力、伺服、编码器、工业以太网和传感器回路可能共用一条拖链，却需要不同的线对几何、屏蔽和载流能力。混合结构应按回路逐项审核，不能笼统称为“机器人电缆”。

02

CONSTRUCTION + INSTALLATION

结构与安装

Build the moving cable system构建运动线缆系统

Carrier layout controls service life

拖链内部布置直接影响寿命

Keep cables free to move along the neutral axis, separate very different diameters or weights, and avoid crossing or tightly bundling adjacent runs. Observe the carrier maker’s bend radius, internal height, separators and fill guidance. Provide strain relief at the fixed and moving ends without clamping the flex zone. Hose, pneumatic line and cable interaction should be checked through the complete travel before releasing the assembly.

线缆应能沿中性轴自由移动，不同直径或重量的线缆应适当分隔，避免交叉或紧密捆扎。需要遵循拖链厂商对弯曲半径、内部高度、隔片和填充率的要求。固定端与运动端应设置应力释放，但不能夹住动态弯曲区。气管、液压管与电缆之间的相互作用应在全行程内确认。

Jacket and shield follow the installed environment

护套与屏蔽必须服从安装环境

PUR, TPE and other jacket compounds are selected against abrasion, oil, coolant, chips, temperature and cleaning agents, not by material name alone. A braided or foil shield must remain electrically useful after repeated motion; drain-wire fatigue and poor 360-degree termination can interrupt the EMC path. Confirm outer diameter, surface friction, cable weight and connector exit direction as part of the moving assembly.

PUR、TPE 等护套材料应根据磨损、油液、冷却液、切屑、温度和清洗剂选择，不能只看材料名称。编织或铝箔屏蔽在重复运动后仍需保持电气作用；排流线疲劳和不完整的 360° 端接会破坏 EMC 路径。外径、表面摩擦、线缆重量与连接器出线方向都应纳入运动组件审核。

03

EVIDENCE + RELEASE

证据与放行

Validate the installed cycle验证安装循环

Validate the actual cycle and inspect the failure mode

按真实循环验证，并检查失效模式

Prototype testing should reproduce the intended bend radius, travel, speed, acceleration, temperature and cable loading. Continuity monitoring can reveal intermittent opens, while conductor resistance, insulation, shield continuity and visual checks identify progressive damage. Record fixture geometry and cycle count so a later cable, terminal or route change can be assessed. General flexible-cable standards do not automatically certify a project-specific drag-chain life.

样件试验应复现预定弯曲半径、行程、速度、加速度、温度与载荷。在线导通监测可发现瞬断，导体电阻、绝缘、屏蔽连续性与外观检查则用于识别渐进损伤。应记录夹具几何与循环次数，便于后续线材、端接或路径变化时重新评估。通用柔性电缆标准不会自动证明某个项目的拖链寿命。

Common questions常见问题

Is a flexible cable automatically suitable for a drag chain?柔性电缆是否自动适合拖链？

No. Repeated-flex life depends on the complete construction and the actual bend radius, travel, speed, acceleration, installation and environment.

不是。重复弯曲寿命取决于完整结构，以及真实弯曲半径、行程、速度、加速度、安装与环境。
