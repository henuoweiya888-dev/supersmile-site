Configuration & Project Review

01 PTFE layer identity

Confirm whether PTFE is the insulation, jacket or coax dielectric before specifying the termination.

02 Conductor and interface

Define conductor alloy, plating, gauge and the actual connector or free-end preparation.

03 Strip method

Approve the stripping method and dimensions with limits for insulation cuts and conductor damage.

04 Thermal termination

Review soldering exposure or crimp conditions for the selected wire and connector combination.

05 Support and sealing

Select strain relief and sealing methods that suit the low-adhesion fluoropolymer surface.

06 Assembly conditions

Agree electrical, mechanical and temperature verification for the finished assembly rather than using a bulk-material rating.
