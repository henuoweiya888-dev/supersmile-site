Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the pictured ends and lead construction to define auxiliary-power connections, electrical duty and handling space.以图示端头及引线结构为依据，确认辅助电源接入位置、电气工况与操作空间。

01

SAE-style two-pole interface and clampsSAE式双极接口与夹钳

These connection forms are visible in the photograph. Define the equipment at each end, supply direction and permitted electrical duty from circuit documentation.以上结构可在实拍中识别。各端接入设备、供电方向和允许负载应由回路资料确定。

02

Red/black clamps and polarity红黑夹钳与极性

Red and black clamps distinguish the connection ends. Manufacturing documents must define polarity, contact locations, grip range and end insulation; color alone does not establish the system connection.红黑夹钳用于区分连接端。生产文件还需明确正负极、接触位置、夹持范围及端头绝缘，不能只凭颜色判定整机接法。

03

Lead length and handling space引线长度与操作空间

Set lead length from the actual connection distance and allow room for mating and clamp handling. A lead looped for storage does not establish repeated-extension or spring-retraction performance.按实际接点距离确定引线长度，预留插拔和夹钳操作空间；可盘绕收纳不等于具备反复拉伸或弹簧回缩性能。

04

Auxiliary-power circuit scope辅助电源回路边界

This example is scoped to auxiliary supply and battery-maintenance connections. Starter-motor current is outside the confirmed application scope. Confirm the load, voltage drop, component ratings and circuit protection for the project.本示例按辅助供电与电池维护连接讨论，启动机大电流不在已确认用途范围。负载、压降、器件额定和保护配置需要项目确认。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

The following directions concern auxiliary supply and battery-maintenance projects. Establish the connections, permitted duty and protection from equipment documentation and sample review.以下方向用于辅助供电与电池维护项目讨论。具体接法、允许负载及保护要求以设备资料与样品确认为依据。

Battery maintenance access电池维护接入

Plan access for battery-maintenance equipment around terminal polarity, clamp clearance and the charger interface. Charging method and permitted current come from the equipment documentation; the lead itself does not provide charging control.围绕电池维护设备的接入位置，确认正负极、夹钳操作空间和充电器接口。充电方式及允许电流由设备资料确定，线束本身不承担充电控制功能。

Vehicle accessory interfaces车载附件接口

For a vehicle accessory interface, confirm the plug or socket form, mating retention and lead length. Establish the accessory input and vehicle outlet requirements before selecting conductors, circuit protection and the connection arrangement.用于讨论车内附件的辅助供电接口时，核对插头或插座形式、插合保持力和线长。先确认附件输入及车辆电源口要求，再选择导体与保护配置。

DC accessory bench checks直流附件台架检查

Bench checks can cover continuity, polarity, terminal contact and the voltage-drop limits agreed for the project. Use a suitably controlled supply and confirm the permitted electrical duty from drawings and component data before testing.台架样品检查可围绕导通、极性、端头接触和项目要求的压降展开。测试电源须具备适合样品的控制条件，允许电气负载应先由图纸和器件资料确认。

Vehicle-service tool leads车辆维护工具引线

For small tools or accessories used during vehicle service, define the equipment input, connection points, operating duration and a secure lead position. This direction covers auxiliary supply review; starter-motor jump starting is outside its scope.车辆维护中的小型工具或附件接入，需要确认设备输入、接点、作业时长及安全放置位置。此方向用于辅助电源连接评审，不包含起动机跨接启动用途。

Auxiliary-circuit protection辅助回路保护配置

Review protection devices, conductors, terminations and the accessory load together, allowing access for service and replacement. Confirm whether a fuse holder is included, its component reference and its circuit position from the actual construction and approved drawing.辅助回路的保险器件、导体、端头和负载应配套评审，保留检修及更换空间。是否配置保险座、器件型号和串联位置，需按具体结构与确认图纸确定。

Accessory lead routing附件引线安装走向

Route auxiliary leads clear of sharp edges, moving parts and pinch points in the vehicle or service area. Confirm fixing points, bend allowance, mating clearance and installed length during sample fitting rather than inferring the installation from appearance.车内或维修工位的辅助引线需避开锐边、活动件和受压位置，预留插拔空间。固定点、弯曲余量与实际长度应在样品试装时核对，不凭产品外观确定安装位置。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is this a starter-motor jump lead?可以用于发动机跨接起动吗？

This page covers auxiliary supply and battery-maintenance connections. Starter-motor current is outside its confirmed scope. A jump-starting lead requires a separate system and electrical-duty review, followed by appropriate design, component selection and validation.本页的用途范围为辅助供电与电池维护连接，启动机大电流不在已确认范围。需要跨接起动线时，应独立提供系统和电流工况，另行设计、选料与验证。

Do clamps or a fuse holder establish a current rating?带有夹钳或保险座就能确定电流吗？

Establish electrical duty from the conductors, contacts, plug or socket, protection devices, length and operating conditions. Clamp size or fuse-holder appearance in a photograph does not provide an assembly rating.载流要求须结合导体、接点、插头或插座、保护器件、长度和使用条件确认。照片中的夹钳大小或保险座外形不能提供整套组件的额定值。

Does the lead provide charging or voltage conversion?连接线是否具有充电或升降压功能？

The example shows a connection assembly without a claim of charging control or voltage conversion. Before connecting maintenance equipment or an accessory, confirm equipment function, supply direction, polarity and input/output requirements.本示例展示连接结构，不声明具备充电控制或升降压功能。连接维护充电器或辅助附件前，需要核对设备功能、供电方向、极性和输入输出要求。
