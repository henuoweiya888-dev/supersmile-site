Selection & Project Review

01 Application Boundary

A lighting harness is not defined by a bulb socket alone. Lamp technology, optical function, circuit current, switching method, body controller, diagnostics, connector keying, route, thermal zone, sealing and destination-market requirements determine the released assembly. A connector that fits can still have the wrong pinout, ground strategy, load capacity or control architecture.

02 Project Inputs

Provide vehicle or equipment identity; model year and market; lamp positions and technologies; lamp, bulb, driver and mating-connector data; system voltage; circuit and pin tables; continuous, inrush and fault current; fuse, relay, BCM, PWM or LIN control; ground strategy; route and branch datums; temperature, water, salt, vibration and flex exposure; drawings or samples; labels; quantity; and required validation.

03 Release Records

Approved requirements become controlled drawings, pin and circuit tables, BOMs, cut lists, splice maps, branch datums, protection instructions, labels and test definitions. First articles are checked for mating, reach, lamp installation, current path, voltage drop and agreed functions before repeat production. Harness, lamp, vehicle configuration, fixture and inspection records remain linked by revision.

04 Interface Review

Confirm the exact mating interface, pin map, dimensions and installation orientation.

05 Sample Acceptance

Agree the sample configuration and electrical, mechanical or environmental checks required by the project.

06 Change Control

Record changes to materials, drawings and process instructions before repeat manufacture.

Send the part number, drawing or sample to confirm the replacement configuration and sourcing plan.
