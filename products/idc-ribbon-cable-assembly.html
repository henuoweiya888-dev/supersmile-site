Configuration & Project Review

01 Ribbon cable definition

Confirm conductor count, pitch, gauge and actual flat-cable article number.

02 IDC connector pair

Specify connector series, contact count, keying and the cable construction accepted by its IDC contacts.

03 Contact orientation

Define pin1, connector orientation, straight-through or crossed mapping and branch positions.

04 Termination tooling

Confirm connector-specific tooling, compression depth and process checks on representative cable.

05 Fold and strain relief

Set permitted folds, edge clearance and strain-relief components for the route.

06 Identity and inspection

Keep 3M product-identification numbers distinct from order codes; verify finished pin mapping and retention.

Send the part number, drawing or sample to confirm the replacement configuration and sourcing plan.
