CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the hd15-vga to 5-way bnc branch harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以HD15-VGA转五路BNC分支线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Branched end layout分支端头布局

Conductors separate toward different ends, creating individual connection points. Branch lengths and directions can follow the equipment modules.导线向不同端头分出，形成独立接入位置。各支路可围绕设备模块分别安排长度与方向。

02

D-Sub interface endD-Sub接口端

The D-Sub-form end allows equipment connection to be arranged around the mating face, fixing screws and rear cable route.端头采用D-Sub形式，可围绕插合面、固定螺钉和后部线缆走向安排设备连接。

03

BNC-form endBNC形式端头

The BNC-form end can be arranged around the equipment panel and cable route, with clearance for mating.BNC形式的连接端可配合设备面板和线缆走向布置，插接操作空间需保留。

04

End transition layout端头转接与组合

The assembly organizes a transition or combined connection, with end directions, transition sections and fixing arranged around the equipment positions.组件以转接或组合形式组织连接，可围绕两端设备位置安排端头方向、过渡段与固定。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Does an HDMI connector guarantee a particular resolution?使用 HDMI 接口就能保证某个分辨率吗？

No. The cable category and construction, length, source, display, operating mode and every adapter or switch in the path determine the supported link.不能。线缆类别与结构、长度、信号源、显示端、运行模式以及路径中的每个转接器或切换设备共同决定链路能力。

Can continuity testing prove a high-speed video cable?导通测试能证明高速视频线性能吗？

No. Continuity verifies pin routing, opens and shorts. High-speed performance may require representative equipment or defined signal-integrity measurements.不能。导通测试用于确认针脚、开路与短路；高速性能可能还需要代表性设备或规定的信号完整性测量。

Are active optical video cables directional?有源光纤视频线有方向性吗？

Many are. Source and display ends, power requirements, bend limits and service conditions must be confirmed for the selected design.很多有方向性。所选设计的信号源端与显示端、供电要求、弯曲限制和维修条件都必须确认。
