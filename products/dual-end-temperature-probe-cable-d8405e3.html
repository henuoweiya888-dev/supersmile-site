APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Instrument inputs and measurement leads仪器输入与测量引线

Start measurement leads from signal type, input conditions and grounding. Select pairs and shields for the actual measurement range and noise target. Record measurement signal, range, bandwidth and noise targets.测量引线以信号类型、输入条件和接地方式为起点，线对与屏蔽选择需要对应实际量程和噪声目标。选配时，记录测量信号、量程、带宽及噪声目标。

External interfaces on portable equipment便携设备的外部接口

Consider handling, plug grip and cable bends together. Align the exit direction with the device position during use. Define pairs, shielding, capacitance and leakage for the instrument input.便携设备连接要同时考虑操作手势、插头握持和尾部弯曲空间，线缆出口方向应与实际使用姿态协调。选配时，线对、屏蔽、电容与漏电要求按仪器输入定义。

Routing boundaries around hot equipment热区设备的布线边界

Separate continuous temperature, peaks and exposure time around hot equipment. Review wire, terminals and protective parts against their own limits. Confirm special conductors or compensation wire with exact material documents.热区布线需要区分连续温度、峰值和暴露时间，线材、端子及保护件应按各自限制评审。选配时，特殊导体或补偿导线须用准确材料文件确认。

Contact and fine-wire termination端子与细线端接

Contact selection depends on conductor construction, insulation diameter and tooling limits. Set wire preparation and crimp inspection for the actual contact part. Review fixed or moving duty, termination and reference grounding together.端子选择同时取决于导体结构、绝缘外径和工具范围；备线尺寸与压接检查应落到实际接点型号。选配时，固定或运动条件、端接和参考接地共同审核。

Equipment control-board connections设备控制板连接

Document pin-row views, mating direction and nearby wire support. Check access to the latch and contacts after assembly. Record measurement signal, range, bandwidth and noise targets.板端连接要给出针排视图、插合方向和近端线缆支撑；检查装配后是否仍能操作锁扣并查看接点。选配时，记录测量信号、量程、带宽及噪声目标。

Module connections in laboratory equipment实验室设备的模块配线

Coordinate sample areas, moving mechanisms and serviceable modules in laboratory equipment. Take isolation and cleaning requirements from the target device documents. Define pairs, shielding, capacitance and leakage for the instrument input.实验设备连接需要协调样品区、运动机构和可维护模块，电气隔离与清洁要求应来自目标设备文件。选配时，线对、屏蔽、电容与漏电要求按仪器输入定义。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Can ordinary copper wire replace compensation wire?普通铜线可以替代补偿导线吗？

Ordinary copper wire cannot replace specified compensating cable. Record measurement signal, range, bandwidth and noise targets.普通铜线不能替代指定补偿导线。记录测量信号、量程、带宽及噪声目标。

Can a low-noise designation be treated as a measurement result?低噪声名称能直接作为测量结果吗？

A low-noise label needs measurement conditions. Define pairs, shielding, capacitance and leakage for the instrument input.电缆低噪声名称需有测量条件。线对、屏蔽、电容与漏电要求按仪器输入定义。

Can a shield be connected freely to the instrument reference ground?屏蔽可以任意接到仪器参考地吗？

Do not arbitrarily connect shields to instrument reference ground. Confirm special conductors or compensation wire with exact material documents.屏蔽与仪器参考地不得随意并接。特殊导体或补偿导线须用准确材料文件确认。
