CONSTRUCTION结构解析

A closer look at the cable construction.从主干到端头，了解连接结构。

Use the 58909 kl trailer multi-plug braided sleeve harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以58909 KL型拖车多插头编织外套连接线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

02

Braided outer form编织形态外层

The outer covering has a braided form. Coverage segments and finishing can follow breakouts, routing passages and fixing positions.外层呈编织形态，可按分支出口、穿线位置和固定点定义覆盖段与收尾。

03

Multiple connector positions多端头连接位置

Multiple ends provide separate connection positions. Layout and spacing can follow the module arrangement.多个端头提供分别接入的位置，线束展开方向与各端头间距可围绕模块布局定义。

04

Braided outer protection编织外层

A braid can organize the bundle and protect its surface. Define its material, coverage and end treatment around the routing path; distinguish insulating braid from metallic shielding.编织层可用于束线与表面防磨；材料、覆盖长度和端部收口需结合安装路径确定。绝缘编织与金属屏蔽编织应分别定义。

05

Current path and end termination载流与端部连接

Select conductors and terminations from continuous and peak loads, installed length and temperature rise. Confirm lug bore, contact surface, insulation coverage and fixing as a set.依据持续与峰值负载、安装长度和温升要求选择导体与端接；端子孔径、接触面、绝缘覆盖及固定方式需要成套确认。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is a trailer harness a universal product?拖车线束是通用产品吗？

No. It is a broad family of tow-interface, chassis, lighting, brake, axle, auxiliary and equipment harnesses. Market, connector, voltage, trailer geometry, loads and installed options determine the released version.不是。它是由牵引接口、底盘、灯光、制动、车轴、辅助与设备线束组成的广泛家族，市场、连接器、电压、拖车几何、负载与安装选项共同决定放行版本。

Can two seven-pin trailer plugs use the same pinout?两个七针拖车插头一定使用相同针脚定义吗？

Not by assumption. Seven-position interfaces can belong to different standards and vehicle classes. Mating family, viewing direction, pin assignment, voltage, current and circuit function must be verified.不能直接假设。七位接口可能属于不同标准与车辆级别，必须核对对插家族、视图方向、针脚定义、电压、电流与回路功能。

Does a sealed connector make the complete harness waterproof?使用密封连接器就能让整根线束防水吗？

No. Housing, terminal, seal, cavity plug, mating part, cable exit, splice construction, covering, drainage, routing and assembly quality must work together under a defined exposure and test condition.不能。壳体、端子、线封、堵头、对插件、出线、接续结构、覆盖层、排水、走线与装配质量必须在规定暴露和测试条件下共同工作。
