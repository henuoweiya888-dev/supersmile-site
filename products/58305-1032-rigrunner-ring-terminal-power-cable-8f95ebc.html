PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Can wire gauge be selected from current alone?线径只按电流选择就可以吗？

No. Length, allowable voltage drop, ambient and conductor temperature, bundling, duty cycle, insulation rating, terminal range and protective device all affect the selection.不可以。长度、允许压降、环境与导体温度、成束、工作循环、绝缘额定值、端子适用范围和保护器件都会影响选择。

Does a plug that fits have the correct rating?插头能插上就代表额定值正确吗？

No. Connector geometry, current, voltage, grounding, polarization, temperature, environment and regional approval must all match the application.不代表。连接器几何、电流、电压、接地、极性、温度、环境及地区认可必须全部匹配应用。

Can an existing power cord sample be copied?可以依据现有电源线样品复制吗？

A sample helps confirm fit and visible construction, but hidden conductor area, material grade, ratings, approval scope and electrical load still require documentation or testing.样品有助于确认装配与可见结构，但隐藏的导体截面积、材料牌号、额定值、认可范围和电气负载仍需资料或测试确认。
