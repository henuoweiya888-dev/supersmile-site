CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the powercon panel socket to ring-terminal harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以PowerCON面板插座转环形端子线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Ring-terminal end环形端子端头

A ring-terminal end provides a mounting-hole connection. Lead direction, support and insulation coverage can follow the stud position.末端采用带安装孔的环形端子形式，可围绕螺柱位置安排接线方向、端头支撑与绝缘覆盖。

02

Panel-mounted connection面板安装连接

The interface is arranged at a panel or feedthrough, with front/rear clearance, fixing positions and cable exit defined around the enclosure.接口与面板或穿壳位置配合，前后端连接空间、固定孔位和出线方向可围绕机壳定义。

03

End transition layout端头转接与组合

The assembly organizes a transition or combined connection, with end directions, transition sections and fixing arranged around the equipment positions.组件以转接或组合形式组织连接，可围绕两端设备位置安排端头方向、过渡段与固定。

04

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

05

Current path and end termination载流与端部连接

Select conductors and terminations from continuous and peak loads, installed length and temperature rise. Confirm the end interface, contact surface, insulation coverage and fixing as a set; check mounting-bore size where ring terminals are specified.依据持续与峰值负载、安装长度和温升要求选择导体与端接；端部接口、接触面、绝缘覆盖及固定方式需要成套确认；仅在采用环形端子时核对安装孔径。

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Can wire gauge be selected from current alone?线径只按电流选择就可以吗？

No. Length, allowable voltage drop, ambient and conductor temperature, bundling, duty cycle, insulation rating, terminal range and protective device all affect the selection.不可以。长度、允许压降、环境与导体温度、成束、工作循环、绝缘额定值、端子适用范围和保护器件都会影响选择。

Does a plug that fits have the correct rating?插头能插上就代表额定值正确吗？

No. Connector geometry, current, voltage, grounding, polarization, temperature, environment and regional approval must all match the application.不代表。连接器几何、电流、电压、接地、极性、温度、环境及地区认可必须全部匹配应用。

Can an existing power cord sample be copied?可以依据现有电源线样品复制吗？

A sample helps confirm fit and visible construction, but hidden conductor area, material grade, ratings, approval scope and electrical load still require documentation or testing.样品有助于确认装配与可见结构，但隐藏的导体截面积、材料牌号、额定值、认可范围和电气负载仍需资料或测试确认。
