CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the df9 ffc adapter board flex cable assembly form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以DF9接口 · FFC转接板软排线组件的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

End transition layout端头转接与组合

The assembly organizes a transition or combined connection, with end directions, transition sections and fixing arranged around the equipment positions.组件以转接或组合形式组织连接，可围绕两端设备位置安排端头方向、过渡段与固定。

02

Flat conductor arrangement扁平排列结构

The cable uses a flat arrangement, allowing compact routing around insertion direction, folds and end orientation.线缆采用扁平排列形态，可围绕插入方向、折转位置和端头朝向安排紧凑布线。

03

Grouped cable-set form组合线组形式

Several connection pieces are organized as a cable set, allowing each section to be assigned and identified by assembly sequence and module.多段连接件以线组形式组织，可按装配顺序和设备模块划分各段用途与标识。

04

Conductor and insulation selection导体与绝缘选配

Select conductors and insulation from circuit loads, diameter limits, bending and exposure. A material name does not replace ratings or validation results.从电路负载、外径限制、弯曲方式与环境条件选择导体和绝缘体系；材料名称不应直接代替额定参数或验证结果。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Thin display cables and insertion boundaries薄型显示排线与插入边界

Check contact orientation, insertion length, stiffener thickness and latch access. Circuit count alone cannot establish mating fit. Distinguish FPC from FFC before defining pitch and circuit count.薄型连接需核对触点面方向、插入长度、补强厚度和锁止操作空间，不能仅凭线路数确定对插关系。选配时，先区分FPC或FFC，再确定节距与线路数。

Routing and retention in confined spaces狭小空间的布线与固定

Define bends, retention points and assembly order in confined spaces. Do not compensate for an incorrect interface direction or length with forced folds. Define same-side or opposite-side contacts and the actual insertion views.狭小空间布线需明确转弯、固定点和拆装顺序，不能用强行折线弥补接口方向或长度误差。选配时，明确两端接触面同向或反向及实际插入视图。

Routing and retention on moving equipment移动设备的走线与固定

Define travel, speed and retention at both ends of moving connections. Set separate targets and validation conditions for bending and torsion. Match total thickness, stiffeners and exposed-contact length.移动连接需明确动作行程、速度和两端固定，动态弯曲与扭转应分别设定目标和验证条件。选配时，匹配总厚度、补强板和裸露接触长度。

Display-module and control-board connections显示模块与控制板连接

Check insertion direction, contact orientation and frame clearance at display modules. Mark fixed folds on the assembly drawing. Define locking clearance, fixed folds and dynamic motion separately.显示模块连接需核对插入方向、触点朝向与边框空间，并将固定折弯的位置标入装配图。选配时，将锁止空间、固定折弯和动态动作分别定义。

Equipment control-board connections设备控制板连接

Document pin-row views, mating direction and nearby wire support. Check access to the latch and contacts after assembly. Distinguish FPC from FFC before defining pitch and circuit count.板端连接要给出针排视图、插合方向和近端线缆支撑；检查装配后是否仍能操作锁扣并查看接点。选配时，先区分FPC或FFC，再确定节距与线路数。

Board service and module removal板卡检修与模块拆装

Keep latches and cable ends accessible for board service. Review removal paths and cable allowance alongside the controlled pinout. Define same-side or opposite-side contacts and the actual insertion views.板卡检修要保留锁扣与连接端的操作空间，拆装路径和线缆余量应与受控针脚表一起确认。选配时，明确两端接触面同向或反向及实际插入视图。
