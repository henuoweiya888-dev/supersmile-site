CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the battery power circular connector y-branch harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以电池电源圆形接头Y形分支线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Y-shaped breakoutY形分出结构

The breakout follows a Y-shaped route toward separate ends. Bend clearance and fixing can be arranged around the split.分出位置形成Y形走向，可把一处接入分向不同端头。安装时围绕分出点安排弯曲和固定。

02

Circular connection end圆形连接端

The end uses a circular interface form, with connection positions planned around mating direction, retention and cable exit.端头具有圆形接口形式，可围绕对插方向、端部固定和线缆出口规划连接位置。

03

Plug-in connection end插接式端头

The end uses a plug-in form, with clearance arranged for insertion, removal and cable bends.端头采用插接形式，可围绕安装位置安排插入、退出及线缆弯曲空间。

04

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。
