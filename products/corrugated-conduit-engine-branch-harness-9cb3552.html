APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Vehicle control-module leads车辆控制模块引线

Separate interface cavities, power circuits and signal circuits at controller leads. Set branch dimensions from the module and mounting-point positions. Define automotive wire specification, model and supply part number from project documents.控制模块尾线需要清楚区分接口腔位、电源和信号回路；分支尺寸应对应模块与固定点的位置。选配时，汽车线材规范、型号和供货料号按项目文件确定。

Interfaces within engine-bay layouts发动机舱内的接口布置

Map heat sources, fluid exposure and service routes together. Allow assembly space around connector ends and cable supports. Select conductor material, area and stranding for load and terminal suitability.发动机舱连接要把热源、油液暴露和检修路径放在同一张布线图中，端头与固定点也需留出装配空间。选配时，导体材质、截面与绞合按负载及端子适配选择。

Conductor construction and termination fit导体结构与端接适配

Match conductor area and stranding to load and terminal limits. Insulation diameter also affects tooling, seals and cable retention. Record insulation material, wall thickness and diameter separately for seal and tool review.导体截面与股线结构需要对应实际负载和端子范围，绝缘外径还会影响工具、密封与夹持选择。选配时，绝缘材料、壁厚和外径分别记录，供线封与工具审核。

Vehicle lighting and body connections车载灯具与车身连接

Coordinate mating clearance, cable retention and body routing around lighting units. Define circuit functions from the target equipment wiring diagram. Define the operating boundary for temperature, fluid exposure and installation.灯具附近的引线需要协调插接空间、线缆固定和车身走线路径，电路功能以目标设备接线图为依据。选配时，温度、油液暴露和安装条件需要明确使用边界。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Define automotive wire specification, model and supply part number from project documents.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，汽车线材规范、型号和供货料号按项目文件确定。

Vehicle service connections车辆检修转接位置

Provide access for mating and service tools. Record end-face views and cable retention after service; appearance alone does not establish interchangeability. Select conductor material, area and stranding for load and terminal suitability.可拆卸尾线应保留插拔与工具空间，并记录端视图和维修后的固定方式，避免只凭外观确定替换关系。选配时，导体材质、截面与绞合按负载及端子适配选择。
