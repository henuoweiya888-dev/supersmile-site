ENGINEERING OVERVIEW工程概览

Design for sunlight, water, temperature and the actual installation route围绕日照、水、温度与真实安装路径进行设计

An outdoor cable may face solar UV, heat, cold, rain, condensation, standing water, wind movement, ice, abrasion, animals, chemicals and lightning-related transients. Above-ground, conduit, tray, rooftop, direct-buried, solar, antenna and mobile installations require different constructions and installation rules. State the exact listing, standard or customer qualification required by the destination market.

户外电缆可能面对太阳紫外、热、冷、雨、凝露、积水、风致运动、结冰、磨损、动物、化学介质与雷电瞬态。架空、导管、桥架、屋顶、直埋、光伏、天线与移动安装需要不同结构与安装规则。应明确目标市场要求的准确认证、标准或客户验证。

01

SYSTEM DEFINITION

系统定义

Map the exposure profile绘制环境暴露谱

Map UV, heat and cold over the complete route

绘制全路径的紫外、热与低温

Rooftops and exposed metalwork can run hotter than ambient air, while winter installation and wind chill challenge cold bend and impact performance. Carbon-black or stabilised compounds can improve UV resistance, but compound formulation, wall thickness and test evidence matter. Record continuous and peak temperature, solar exposure, colour, installation season and expected life. A material name such as PVC, PE or PUR does not define outdoor durability by itself.

屋顶与暴露金属表面的温度可能高于环境空气，而冬季安装与风冷又会考验低温弯曲和冲击性能。炭黑或稳定剂可改善耐紫外性，但配方、壁厚与试验证据同样重要。应记录持续和峰值温度、日照、颜色、安装季节与预期寿命；PVC、PE 或 PUR 等材料名称不能单独定义户外耐久性。

Water exposure must include every interface

进水评估必须覆盖每一处接口

Rain, spray, condensation, temporary immersion and permanent submersion are different conditions. Water can enter at cut ends, glands, connector seals, splices, branches or damaged jacket and then migrate along conductors. Define mating state, orientation, drainage, drip loops, cable entry direction and pressure or immersion method. An IP rating on one connector does not automatically transfer to the complete cable assembly or enclosure.

雨淋、喷溅、凝露、短时浸水与长期水下属于不同条件。水可能从切口、格兰、连接器密封、接头、分支或护套损伤处进入，并沿导体迁移。应定义插合状态、方向、排水、滴水弯、进线方向以及压力或浸水方法。单个连接器的 IP 等级不会自动转移到完整电缆组件或机壳。

02

CONSTRUCTION + INSTALLATION

结构与安装

Engineer route and electrical duty设计路径与电气工况

Route and support resist wind, abrasion and movement

路径与支撑用于抵抗风、磨损与运动

Cable weight, span, wind movement, ice, vibration and thermal expansion load supports and terminations. Specify tray, clips, catenary support, conduit, burial depth or mechanical guards as applicable. Preserve minimum bend radius and avoid sharp roof edges, standing water and unsupported connector loads. Flexible outdoor equipment leads and fixed outdoor wiring are different product categories; use the construction approved for the actual installation.

线缆重量、跨距、风致运动、结冰、振动与热胀冷缩都会对支撑和端接施加载荷。应按需规定桥架、夹具、承力件、导管、埋深或机械护板，保持最小弯曲半径，避开尖锐屋面边缘、积水与无支撑连接器载荷。户外柔性设备引线与固定户外布线属于不同产品类别，必须使用适合实际安装的结构。

Electrical design includes temperature, voltage drop and transients

电气设计还包括温度、压降与瞬态

Ampacity changes with ambient temperature, grouping, conduit, burial and solar heating. Long outdoor runs make voltage drop important, while communication and sensor links may need surge, grounding or shield coordination. Separation from lightning protection, power circuits and other services follows the site design. Cable selection cannot replace equipment-level overcurrent, surge and bonding protection.

载流量会随环境温度、成束、导管、埋设与太阳加热而变化，长距离户外路径还会放大压降问题；通信与传感器链路可能需要协调浪涌、接地与屏蔽。与防雷系统、电力回路和其他设施的间距应服从现场设计。线缆选型不能代替设备层级的过流、浪涌与搭接保护。

03

EVIDENCE + RELEASE

证据与放行

Verify the installed boundary验证安装边界

Verify the exact outdoor construction and installation

验证准确户外结构与安装状态

Release records should identify conductor, insulation, jacket, fillers, armour or shield, printing, connectors, seals and applicable standard. Inspection checks jacket damage, bend, support, connector orientation, labels and sealing workmanship. Depending on scope, agreed evidence may address UV, temperature cycling, water ingress, insulation, voltage, pull or flex. Field-installed systems still require competent installation and equipment-level verification after the cable leaves the factory.

放行记录应标识导体、绝缘、护套、填充、铠装或屏蔽、印字、连接器、密封与适用标准。检查项目包括护套损伤、弯曲、支撑、连接器方向、标签与密封工艺。依据范围，约定证据可涉及紫外、温度循环、进水、绝缘、耐压、拉力或弯曲。线缆出厂后，现场系统仍需合格安装与设备层级验证。
