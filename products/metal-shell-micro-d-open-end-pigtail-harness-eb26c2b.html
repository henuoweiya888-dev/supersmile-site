CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the metal-shell micro-d open-end pigtail harness form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以Micro-D金属外壳开口尾线束的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Micro-D interface formMicro-D接口形式

The compact Micro-D-form connection can be arranged around mating direction, rear support and cable exit.紧凑的Micro-D形式连接端可围绕插合方向、端头后方支撑和线束出口安排安装。

02

Open lead ends开放引线端

An end or some branches remain as open leads, with later terminations and insulation preparation defined for the assembly method.一端或部分支路以开放引线形式保留，可结合装配方式定义后续端头和绝缘处理。

03

Pigtail connection form尾线连接形式

A lead section extends behind the connector, allowing length, further termination and support to follow the equipment connection.插接端后保留引线段，便于按设备接线位置确定长度、后续端接与支撑。

04

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

APPLICATIONS连接的应用

Application directions应用方向

These are connection directions for project discussion. Suitability depends on interfaces, circuits, installation conditions and project validation; configuration can be adapted to the equipment.以下为可讨论的连接方向。实际适用性取决于接口、电路、安装环境和项目验证，可按设备需求调整。

Instrument inputs and measurement leads仪器输入与测量引线

Start measurement leads from signal type, input conditions and grounding. Select pairs and shields for the actual measurement range and noise target. Check the Micro-D family, shell size and complete insert configuration.测量引线以信号类型、输入条件和接地方式为起点，线对与屏蔽选择需要对应实际量程和噪声目标。选配时，核对Micro-D系列、壳号及完整插芯配置。

Contact and fine-wire termination端子与细线端接

Contact selection depends on conductor construction, insulation diameter and tooling limits. Set wire preparation and crimp inspection for the actual contact part. Record contact type, gender and connection table from end-face views.端子选择同时取决于导体结构、绝缘外径和工具范围；备线尺寸与压接检查应落到实际接点型号。选配时，按端面视图记录接点类型、公母及连接表。

Module wiring within enclosures机箱内部的模块配线

Coordinate board positions, module removal and wire bends within the enclosure. Preserve insulation clearance and support behind each termination. Define termination method, wire gauge and rear support for the specific contacts.机箱内部配线需要协调板卡位置、模块拆装和导线转弯空间；端接后的绝缘与支撑不能被外壳挤压。选配时，端接工艺、线规与后部支撑按具体接点定义。

Equipment control-board connections设备控制板连接

Document pin-row views, mating direction and nearby wire support. Check access to the latch and contacts after assembly. Confirm fasteners, shielding and acceptance specifications against project documents.板端连接要给出针排视图、插合方向和近端线缆支撑；检查装配后是否仍能操作锁扣并查看接点。选配时，锁紧件、屏蔽及验收规范按项目文件逐项确认。

Equipment panels and circular interfaces设备面板与圆形接口

Start with the cutout, panel thickness, mounting direction and rear clearance. Coordinate contact arrangement and cable exit with the complete assembly view. Check the Micro-D family, shell size and complete insert configuration.面板连接以开孔、板厚、装配方向和后方净空为起点；接点排列与电缆出口还需要对应完整总装视图。选配时，核对Micro-D系列、壳号及完整插芯配置。

External interfaces on portable equipment便携设备的外部接口

Consider handling, plug grip and cable bends together. Align the exit direction with the device position during use. Record contact type, gender and connection table from end-face views.便携设备连接要同时考虑操作手势、插头握持和尾部弯曲空间，线缆出口方向应与实际使用姿态协调。选配时，按端面视图记录接点类型、公母及连接表。
