PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is a construction vehicle harness a single standard loom?工程车辆线束是一根标准线束吗？

No. It is a broad family covering complete machines and many cab, engine, chassis, boom, valve, lighting, attachment and service assemblies. Platform, serial range and options determine the released version.不是。它是覆盖整机以及驾驶室、发动机、底盘、臂架、阀组、照明、属具与维修组件的广泛家族，平台、序列号范围与选装决定放行版本。

Can the same harness be used on an excavator and loader?挖掘机与装载机可以共用同一线束吗？

Only if their approved interfaces, circuits, loads, movement, environment and geometry are actually identical. Similar controllers or connectors do not establish interchangeability.只有当批准接口、回路、负载、运动、环境与几何确实相同时才可能共用；相似控制器或连接器不能证明互换。

What must be checked at a boom or articulation point?臂架或铰接点必须检查什么？

Fixed and moving ends, full travel, twist, bend radius, support spacing, unsupported mass, rub points, hydraulic-hose interaction and service positions should all be reviewed.需要审核固定端与运动端、完整行程、扭转、弯曲半径、支撑间距、无支撑重量、摩擦点、与液压管相互作用及维修位置。
