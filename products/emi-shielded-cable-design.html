Configuration & Project Review

01 Interference path

Identify the relevant noise source, sensitive circuit and frequency range before selecting shielding.

02 Cable screen construction

Select braid, foil, conductive sleeve or a combined screen with the required coverage and flexibility.

03 Termination and bonding

Define how each screen connects to the connector shell or chassis at both ends.

04 Branch and enclosure gaps

Review screen continuity at branches, service breaks and panel entries.

05 Mechanical protection

Check sleeve diameter, bend radius, abrasion and strain relief with the finished harness.

06 System-level validation

Agree grounding and EMC test conditions on the target equipment; material shielding alone does not establish system performance.

Send the part number, drawing or sample to confirm the replacement configuration and sourcing plan.
