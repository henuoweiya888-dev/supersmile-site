CONSTRUCTION结构解析

Connection structure and design details连接结构与设计要点

Use the fru lvds display branch cable form as a starting point and confirm these design points against drawings and the actual equipment.以FRU LVDS显示屏分支连接线的结构形式为起点，结合图纸和实际设备确认以下设计要点。

01

Trunk and branch layout主干与分支

Locate trunk and branch datums from equipment fixing points. Allow room for mating, bends and servicing so branches do not transfer installation loads to contacts.以设备固定点确定主干和各支路的尺寸基准，预留插拔、弯曲和维护空间，避免支路把拉力传递到端子。

02

Signal path信号路径

Define signal type, pair allocation, shield continuity and termination as well as the connector shape. Establish transmission performance from the actual cable, length and system validation.在接口外形之外确认信号类型、线对分配、屏蔽连续性和端接方法；传输能力应按实际线材、长度及整机验证结果确定。

03

Conductor and insulation selection导体与绝缘选配

Select conductors and insulation from circuit loads, diameter limits, bending and exposure. A material name does not replace ratings or validation results.从电路负载、外径限制、弯曲方式与环境条件选择导体和绝缘体系；材料名称不应直接代替额定参数或验证结果。

04

Interface and function review接口与功能确认

Equal pin count can conceal different power assignments同针数屏接口可能具有不同供电针位

05

Installation and acceptance安装与验收

Do not identify LVDS and eDP as equivalent by shapeLVDS与eDP不能按外形归同

PRODUCT QUESTIONS产品问答

Product & customization questions产品与定制常见问题

Is every display cable an LVDS cable?所有显示屏线都是 LVDS 线吗？

No. Displays may use LVDS, eDP, MIPI, FPD-Link, GMSL or proprietary parallel/serial interfaces. Connector appearance alone cannot identify the electrical interface.不是。显示系统可能使用 LVDS、eDP、MIPI、FPD-Link、GMSL 或专用并行/串行接口，仅凭连接器外观无法判断电气接口。

Can the cable be built from an old sample?可以依据旧样品开发吗？

A sample helps with connector, length and routing, but data rate, pin map, pair assignment, impedance, termination, shield grounding, power circuits and acceptance limits still require equipment data or engineering confirmation.样品有助于确认连接器、长度与走线，但数据速率、针脚表、线对分配、阻抗、端接、屏蔽接地、电源回路与接收限值仍需设备资料或工程确认。

Does 100-ohm cable guarantee a good LVDS link?使用 100Ω 线缆就一定能保证 LVDS 链路正常吗？

No. Connector and board transitions, pair balance, length, loss, termination, stubs, grounding, crosstalk and the actual transmitter/receiver also affect the complete channel.不能。连接器与板端过渡、线对平衡、长度、损耗、端接、支路、接地、串扰及实际收发器都会影响完整通道。
